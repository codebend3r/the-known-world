House Lorch is a house of landed knights in the westerlands, sworn to Casterly Rock. During the Faith Militant uprising, Ser Lyonel Lorch took up arms against King Maegor I Targaryen himself at the Great Fork alongside Lord Rupert Falwell, and Maegor answered by loosing Balerion the Black Dread upon the Lorches' own seat, burning it to ash. Their name and seat have gone quieter in the centuries since, though semi-canon accounts lay one more crime at a Lorch's feet: Ser Amory Lorch is said to have thrown the last Tarbeck heir, a boy of three, down a well during the Reyne-Tarbeck rebellion.

That same Amory Lorch struck the most infamous blow of the family's history a generation later, stabbing Princess Rhaenys Targaryen to death during the Sack of King's Landing. In the War of the Five Kings he serves Tywin Lannister as a brutal enforcer, hunting down Yoren's recruits, hanging Beric Dondarrion between two innocent beekeepers, and holding Harrenhal as castellan, until the castle falls to the northmen and Vargo Hoat's turncloak Brave Companions and Amory is thrown living into the bear pit. Ser Lorent Lorch succeeds him as head of the house.
