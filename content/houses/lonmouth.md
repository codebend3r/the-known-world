House Lonmouth is a noble house of the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End. Their seat is Lon Castle, a defensible keep nestled in a narrow vale amid the hills south of Summerhall, in the country bordering the Dornish Marches. The house is best known for its singular heraldry: arms quartered of six, alternating red lips strewn on yellow and yellow skulls strewn on black, a device that has earned its members some of the most memorable sobriquets in the realm. Their words, "The Choice Is Yours," are held to reflect that same duality, as though the house invites the world to decide between desire and death.

Two knights of House Lonmouth left a mark on the history of Westeros a generation apart. Ser Joffrey Lonmouth, called the Knight of Kisses, was a sworn sword of House Velaryon and the beloved companion of Ser Laenor Velaryon during the reign of King Viserys I. He perished of injuries sustained at the wedding tourney of Laenor and Princess Rhaenyra Targaryen, beaten senseless by Ser Criston Cole's morningstar; Laenor is said never to have left his bedside in the six days before he died. A generation later, Ser Richard Lonmouth, bearing the full title Knight of Skulls and Kisses, served as squire and close companion to Prince Rhaegar Targaryen. Richard competed at the great tourney at Harrenhal and afterward followed Rhaegar's banner, his subsequent fate remaining unknown after Robert's Rebellion swept away the old order. At the outset of _A Game of Thrones_ the house holds its valley seat in the stormlands, a minor but proud vassal of Storm's End.
