House Woods dwells within the wolfswood in the north, sworn to House Glover of Deepwood Motte. Asha Greyjoy thinks of them as one of the wood's clans rather than a house in the ordinary reckoning, though they answer Deepwood Motte's call all the same.

When Stannis Baratheon marches on Winterfell through the wolfswood, Lady Sybelle Glover lends him scouts drawn from House Woods, among them Ned Woods, known as Noseless Ned, who guides the host through the deep timber.
