House Blackmyre is a crannogman house of the north, dwelling amid the bogs and lizard-lions of the Neck and sworn to House Reed of Greywater Watch rather than to Winterfell directly. Like the other small crannogman houses, they keep no known arms or words, and the wider realm knows little of their ways.

No Blackmyre is recorded to have appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings, their doings hidden as ever behind the Neck's grey mists and hidden causeways.
