House Holt was one of a dozen First Men houses of the Greenblood whose lords stood as electors, and candidates, for the High Kingship of Dorne. No blazon of theirs survives in the chronicles.

A generation of war followed a disputed election among these houses, destroying three of their number outright; House Holt endured that reckoning only to be conquered along with the rest when the Andals came to Dorne.
