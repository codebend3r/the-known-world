House Hasty is a house of landed knights from the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End. Their seat is not recorded in any surviving reckoning, and they have never risen to the rank of lords; yet they bear ancient arms, a white bend cotised on purple, suggesting a lineage that stretches back well before the Conquest. Minor houses of their station were common throughout the stormlands, holding small holdfasts by the grace of Storm's End, their knights answering the call of the stag banner when war demanded it.

The house is known chiefly through the deeds of Ser Bonifer Hasty, called the Good, its head at the time of _A Game of Thrones_. In his youth Bonifer was counted among the most promising tourney knights of the realm. He wore the favor of Princess Rhaella Targaryen at the lists and named her queen of love and beauty, a devotion that could lead nowhere given the vast distance in station between a landed knight and a princess of the blood. When Rhaella was wed to her brother Prince Aerys, Bonifer laid down his lance and turned wholly to the Faith of the Seven, vowing that only the Maiden could fill the place in his heart that a princess had once occupied. In the War of the Five Kings he rode first with Renly Baratheon and then, after Renly's death at Storm's End, with Stannis. He was taken captive at the Battle of the Blackwater and submitted to King Joffrey, earning pardon. Jaime Lannister later brought him west to treat with Riverrun and left him as castellan of Harrenhal, where Bonifer and his company of devout men-at-arms, the Holy Hundred, keep ward over that ill-omened seat in the name of the Iron Throne.
