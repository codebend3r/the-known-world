House Knott is a mountain clan of the north, its people holding the hills and peaks north of the wolfswood beyond any castle wall. Their chief is called Lord Knott at Winterfell and simply the Knott by his own clan folk, a chieftain's style older than the lords of the lowlands who nominally rule the north.

When Stannis Baratheon marches through the northern mountains on his way to Deepwood Motte and Winterfell, he wins the mountain clans to his cause, and the Knotts count among the clansmen who take up his fiery cause against House Bolton.
