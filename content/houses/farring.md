House Farring is a lesser house of the crownlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Dragonstone. Rumor holds that Lady Annara Farring, seventh wife of the aged Lord Walder Frey, warmed the bed of his great-grandson Black Walder, though the truth of the tale is known to few.

The house's fortunes rise with Stannis Baratheon's claim during the War of the Five Kings. Bryen Farring squires for Stannis and looks on as the king burns the statues of the Seven at Dragonstone, later dying of the cold on the doomed march to Winterfell. His father, Ser Gilbert Farring, is left to command the garrison at Storm's End, holding the castle through Mace Tyrell's siege. Their cousin Ser Godry Farring, called the Giantslayer for cutting down a giant beneath the Wall, leads Stannis's vanguard on that same march north.
