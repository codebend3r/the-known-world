House Goode's seat and region are lost to record, but the name recurs in the annals of the Kingsguard and Queensguard. Brothers Ser Griffith and Ser Gregor Goode both served among the seven under Aegon the Conqueror's son, King Aegon I, and Ser Glendon Goode stood among Queen Rhaenyra's Queensguard during the Dance of the Dragons.

No Goode is recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings; whatever holdfast or lands the house once claimed have passed from the histories along with its arms and words.
