House Lake was one of a dozen First Men houses of the Greenblood whose lords took turns choosing a High King of Dorne from among their own number. A disputed election plunged the electing houses into a generation of war that destroyed three of their line outright, and whatever remained of House Lake was swept away when the Andals came to Dorne.
