House Sarsfield of Sarsfield was formed by the union of First Men and Andal nobles during the coming of the Andals to the westerlands, and bears a green arrow upon a white bend on green beneath the words "True to the Mark."

During the War of the Five Kings, Sarsfield colors marched with Lord Tywin Lannister's host as it left Harrenhal, and a Sarsfield squire in Ser Gregor Clegane's service was killed by Arya Stark at the inn at the crossroads, a death later laid at Sandor Clegane's door. Sarsfield's mounted archers rode among the forces escorting Tywin Lannister's corpse home to Casterly Rock and later marched with Ser Jaime Lannister to lift the siege of Riverrun, even as men bearing the house's arrow badge were counted among the broken men hanged for the massacre at Saltpans. Ser Melwyn Sarsfield, wed to Shierle Swyft, represents the house at the close of the third century.
