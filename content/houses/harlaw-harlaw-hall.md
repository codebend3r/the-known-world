House Harlaw of Harlaw Hall is a cadet branch of House Harlaw holding the family's own ancestral seat, abandoned by the main line generations ago. Lord Theomore Harlaw, having lost three newborn sons to the damp and unhealthy air of the old keep, built the great castle of Ten Towers as a drier, more comfortable home for his heirs, leaving Harlaw Hall to a lesser branch of the family thereafter.

At the time of the War of the Five Kings, Harlaw Hall is held by Sigfryd Harlaw, called Silverhair, great-uncle to Lord Rodrik Harlaw of Ten Towers, who keeps several grandsons about him at the old hall.
