House Langward is a minor house of the crownlands, sworn to the crown at King's Landing. Its best-remembered son is Ser Harrold Langward, who served in the Kingsguard through the reign of Maegor the Cruel. When King Maegor died mysteriously upon the Iron Throne and young Jaehaerys I Targaryen offered Maegor's white cloaks a choice between execution and the Wall, Ser Harrold chose trial by combat instead, and was slain by Ser Gyles Morrigen for his defiance in 48 AC.
