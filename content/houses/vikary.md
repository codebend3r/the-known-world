House Vikary is a house of landed knights sworn to Casterly Rock, their words unrecorded. Heralds of the Citadel note that their reversed Reyne colors beneath a bend sinister may mark the house's founder as a Reyne bastard, or a descendant of one, while the red boar's head hints at some old tie to House Crakehall, whose own arms bear a brindled boar.

Ser Lymond Vikary is reported slain at the Battle of Oxcross, but the news proves premature: he is in fact held prisoner at Wayfarer's Rest, and is presumably freed once its lord, Karyl Vance, bends the knee to the Iron Throne after the Red Wedding. Elsewhere in the riverlands, a man wearing a boar's-head badge is found hanged among the broken men responsible for the sack of Saltpans, evidence that at least one Vikary man-at-arms turned outlaw before the war's end.
