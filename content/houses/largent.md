House Largent was born from the chaos of the Dance of the Dragons. Ser Luthor Largent commanded the gold cloaks of King's Landing under Prince Daemon Targaryen, and when the city fell to the greens in 130 AC he turned his cloaks against them, killing his own second-in-command, Ser Gwayne Hightower, to hold the gates for Rhaenyra Targaryen's cause. When Rhaenyra took the Iron Throne she raised Luthor to lordship as reward, founding House Largent that same year.

Luthor did not long enjoy his new house's name: he was killed in the riots that tore through King's Landing later in 130 AC, and it is not known whether any of his blood survived him.
