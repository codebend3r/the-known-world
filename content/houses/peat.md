House Peat is a crannogman house of the Neck, sworn to House Reed of Greywater Watch rather than to Winterfell directly, in keeping with the old arrangement by which the Starks leave the bogs to govern themselves through their own lords.

No Peat has stepped into the wider chronicles of the Seven Kingdoms; the house is known only by name among the small crannogmen lines who share the Neck's marshes and moving waters.
