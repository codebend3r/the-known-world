House Swygert is a noble house of the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon. The Citadel's heraldic rolls give their arms as a white scroll with gold knobs on purple within a white tressure, and their words as "Truth Conquers."

No Swygert appears by name in the events of the War of the Five Kings; the house is remembered only by its blazon and its motto.
