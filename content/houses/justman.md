House Justman rose after the coming of the Andals, when Benedict Rivers, a bastard of both Blackwood and Bracken blood, spent thirty years grinding down the petty river kings before crowning himself. His wisdom and fair judgment earned him the name Benedict the Just, and his line the name Justman. Crowned King of the Trident, he and his son Benedict II pushed their domain out to Maidenpool, the Neck, Duskendale, Rosby, and the mouth of the Blackwater Rush.

For nearly three centuries the Justmans held the riverlands, until King Bernarr II watched his sons murdered by Qhored Hoare, King of the Iron Islands, in a Pyke keep remembered ever after as the Bloody Keep. Bernarr's broken campaign against the ironborn ended the line, and the riverlands fell into a century of anarchy before the Storm King Arlan III Durrandon brought them to heel. A pretender named Lucifer Justman, called the Liar, later claimed the old blood, though the maesters have never confirmed his descent.
