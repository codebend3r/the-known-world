House Westbrook is a minor house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Their arms, two green bars gemel upon a field of gold, are recorded by the Citadel's heralds, but no words have ever been set down for them.

No Westbrook has yet appeared in the chronicles of the War of the Five Kings, and the house's current fortunes are unknown.
