House Netley is a house of little prestige and, by ironborn reckoning, fairly new to the Iron Islands; no arms or words of theirs survive in any record. During the War of the Five Kings, Victarion Greyjoy spots Netley men among the captains and driftwood lords gathered for the kingsmoot on Old Wyk.
