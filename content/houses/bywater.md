House Bywater is a lordly house of the crownlands, sworn since the Conquest to the Targaryen kings and, since Robert's Rebellion, to House Baratheon of King's Landing. Little is recorded of the house's deeper origins, though a Lord Bywater stood among the last supporters of King Maegor I Targaryen at his death upon the Iron Throne, fleeing to his seat rather than face the wrath of the lords who turned against the crown afterward.

The house's best-known scion in recent memory is Ser Jacelyn Bywater, who lost a hand at the siege of Pyke during Greyjoy's Rebellion and thereafter wore an iron hand in its place, earning him the byname "Ironhand." Knighted by King Robert I Baratheon for his service, Jacelyn rose to captain the Mud Gate of King's Landing before Tyrion Lannister named him Commander of the City Watch upon Janos Slynt's exile to the Wall. He served loyally through the turmoil of _A Clash of Kings_, arresting Ser Boros Blount and seizing Prince Tommen on Tyrion's orders, only to be cut down by his own fleeing gold cloaks during the Battle of the Blackwater.
