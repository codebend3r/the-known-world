House Bettley is a noble house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock, blazoning three blue beetles upon a field of gold. Arya Stark spots their colors among Lord Tywin Lannister's host as it marches from Harrenhal during the War of the Five Kings.

Ser Jon Bettley, called Beardless Jon, escorts Lord Tywin's corpse back to Casterly Rock after the Red Wedding and later rides with Ser Lyle Crakehall to hunt outlaws near Darry following the fall of Riverrun. Bettley men-at-arms are also counted among the broken men whose bodies Brienne of Tarth finds hanged near Saltpans, victims of the war's lawlessness.
