House Hook was a noble house of the riverlands, river kings of old who once claimed the mouth of the Blackwater Rush in the fractious days of the Hundred Kingdoms. Whether they descended from the First Men or the Andals is not recorded.

No trace of the house survives beyond this claim to ancient kingship; House Hook is long extinct by the time the Seven Kingdoms are united.
