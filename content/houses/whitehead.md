House Whitehead of the Weeping Town holds its seat in that stormlands port, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End. Neither their arms nor their words have been recorded by the Citadel.

In the years after the War of the Five Kings, the house is led by an aged Lord Whitehead, whose young heir, Ser Addam Whitehead, departs the Weeping Town to join Jon Connington's cause following the fall of Griffin's Roost.
