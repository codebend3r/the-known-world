House Blackbar of Bandallon is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden and blazoning a black fess upon a field of silver. King Jaehaerys I Targaryen was hosted at Bandallon in 89 AC, where the lord's youngest daughter attempted, unsuccessfully, to seduce him.

House Blackbar declares for Renly Baratheon during the War of the Five Kings. Ser Leo Blackbar, wed to Olene Tyrell, leads the house into the events of _A Storm of Swords_, while an earlier Blackbar, Ser Elwood, is remembered for his part in the tourney at Ashford Meadow.
