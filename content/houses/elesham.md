House Elesham of the Paps once ruled the Paps as independent lords before bending the knee to Hugo the Hopeful, a King of Mountain and Vale of House Arryn. They hold House Woodhull as their own sworn vassal, and semi-canon heraldry gives them a black mullet between two grey piles inverted on a pink field.

In recent decades, Lord Elesham took to wife one of the daughters of Ser Elys Waynwood and Alys Arryn, though the marriage proved barren. No other Elesham is recorded among the events of the War of the Five Kings.
