Andrik the Unsmiling was no lord born, but a household captain of Lord Dunstan Drumm of Old Wyk, and reputed the strongest man in all the Iron Islands. After Balon Greyjoy's death he stood beside Dunstan's sons Denys and Donnel as one of the Bone Hand's champions at the kingsmoot, though his lord's boasting of past glories and chests found to hold only bronze cost Dunstan the crown, which passed instead to Euron Greyjoy.

When Euron's fleet wrested the Shield Islands from the Reach in 300 AC, unseating House Serry from Southshield, the new king named Andrik lord in Serry's place, alongside Harras Harlaw at Greyshield, Maron Volmark at Greenshield, and Nute the Barber at Oakenshield. Victarion Greyjoy read the honor for what it was: a means of stripping Dunstan Drumm of his strongest right hand by giving Andrik lands and a title of his own, far from Old Wyk.
