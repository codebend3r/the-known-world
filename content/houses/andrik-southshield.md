House Andrik of Southshield is a noble house of the Iron Islands, sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke. No page of the great chronicles records their arms, their words, or any deed of note; they are known only by name among the many small houses that man the longships and rocky holdfasts of the isles.

Whatever history the Andriks keep is theirs alone, unwritten in any account of the wars and kingsmoots that have shaped the Iron Islands within living memory.
