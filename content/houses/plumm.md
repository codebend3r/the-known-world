House Plumm is an ancient westerlands line reaching back to the days of the First Men, sworn to Casterly Rock beneath its purple roundels and the words "Come Try Me". The aged Lord Ossifer Plumm wed Princess Elaena Targaryen in 176 AC and is said to have died in the act of conceiving their son, though whispers later held that Ossifer perished before the marriage was ever consummated and that Elaena's son Viserys was truly fathered by King Aegon IV himself, a rumor immortalized in the crude jest that Ossifer "had a cock six feet long."

Whatever his true blood, Viserys Plumm inherited his father's lands and titles and ruled the house through the reigns of Daeron II and into Aerys I. Later Plumms carried the name through the tourney at Ashford Meadow and the hedge knight gatherings before the Second Blackfyre Rebellion, and semi-canon accounts hold that the house backed Tywin Lannister when he crushed the Reyne-Tarbeck rebellion.

Lord Philip Plumm leads the present house, his sons Ser Dennis, Ser Peter, and Ser Harwyn "Hardstone" Plumm serving Casterly Rock in turn, Harwyn as commander of the garrison at Darry. Far off in Essos, a distant kinsman called Brown Ben Plumm commands the Second Sons and claims descent from Viserys Plumm, boasting of "a drop of the dragon blood" when Daenerys Targaryen's dragon takes a liking to him, a claim Tyrion Lannister meets with the same old joke about Ossifer's endowment.
