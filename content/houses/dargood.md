House Dargood is a minor house of the crownlands, said to be distant kin to the once-great House Darklyn of Duskendale, extinguished in the reckoning that followed the Defiance of Duskendale. Unlike their Darklyn cousins, the Dargoods survived and continue to dwell in the town below Duskendale's walls, though no record sets down their arms, their words, or when first they rose among the crownlands' lesser families.

No Dargood is known to have played any part in the War of the Five Kings. When Brienne of Tarth comes to Duskendale seeking word of Sansa Stark, a servant of the town tells her that Dargoods, Darkes, and Darkwoods still live there in some number, kin of the vanished Darklyns clinging to the town their line once ruled.
