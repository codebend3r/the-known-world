House Errol of Haystack Hall is one of the principal houses sworn to House Baratheon in the stormlands. Semi-canon heraldry gives them a yellow haystack on an orange field, though their words have never been recorded.

During Aegon's Conquest, Lord Errol joined Lords Fell and Buckler in ambushing Orys Baratheon's host as it crossed the Wendwater. The three lords fled into the kingswood, but Rhaenys Targaryen set the woods ablaze astride Meraxes, and Lord Errol was killed before the Last Storm was fought.

When the War of the Five Kings divides the stormlands, House Errol first supports Renly Baratheon, then turns to Stannis Baratheon after Renly's death, its lord counted among the great lords, alongside Estermont and Varner, who parley with Stannis outside Storm's End. Lady Shyra Errol rules Haystack Hall at the war's start; after her death, her successor Sebastion Errol becomes Lord of Haystack Hall in her place.
