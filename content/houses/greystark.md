House Greystark of the Wolf's Den was an offshoot branch of House Stark itself, seated upon land that would, in later centuries, become the site of White Harbor. For five hundred years the Greystarks held the Wolf's Den and their place among the Kings in the North's own kin.

Their line ended when the Greystarks presumed to join House Bolton in rebellion against the Starks of Winterfell, a betrayal that cost the house its seat and its survival; nothing of them remains today save their name in the old chronicles of the North.
