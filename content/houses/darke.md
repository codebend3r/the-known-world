House Darke traces its first known member to Jonquil Darke, called the Scarlet Shadow, a bastard daughter of a Lord Darklyn of Duskendale who may have founded the house herself, or seen it founded by a sibling. Jonquil served as a sworn shield to Queen Alysanne Targaryen and duelled a wildling woman before the court of Lord Theomore Manderly during the queen's celebrated visit to White Harbor. Generations later, in the Dance of the Dragons, Ser Harrold Darke held to the black dragon's cause and died defending Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen at Dragonstone.

Like their Darklyn kin, the Darkes never recovered the prominence of that earlier age. By the time of the War of the Five Kings the name survives only in a servant at the Seven Swords inn in Duskendale, who claims Darke blood but holds no lands, title, or seat, the last visible trace of a house that once stood close to queens.
