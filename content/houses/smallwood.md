House Smallwood of Acorn Hall was already a name of note in the riverlands before the Targaryen conquest, a lord of the house having died fighting in King Arlan III Durrandon's campaign against King Humfrey I Teague. In the centuries since, the Smallwoods fought at the side of kings and against them in nearly equal measure: for the Iron Throne against Harren the Red, against the Faith Militant at Stonebridge, and for the black dragon of Rhaenyra Targaryen in the Dance of the Dragons, when Lord Joseth Smallwood held the westermen host at bay near Acorn Hall itself.

Lord Myles Smallwood's courage on the battlefield won him a Kingsguard appointment for his own sworn sword and, in time, the office of Hand of the King under Jaehaerys I Targaryen, though he proved better suited to war than to governance and was soon dismissed. Though the Smallwoods were formerly sworn directly to House Tully, they answer today to House Vance, though the surviving record does not say whether this is the Vance line of Atranta or of Wayfarer's Rest.

During the War of the Five Kings, Thoren Smallwood rode north as a ranger of the Night's Watch and died at the Fist of the First Men, torn apart by a wight bear. At Acorn Hall itself, Lord Theomar Smallwood was away at war when the brotherhood without banners sheltered there, leaving his wife Lady Ravella to receive Arya Stark; their daughter Carellen had already been sent to safety at a motherhouse in Oldtown. House Smallwood's banner was later seen among the river lords who bent the knee to House Lannister at the siege of Riverrun.
