House Sparr holds lands on Great Wyk some six leagues from Hammerhorn, sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke. Its lord is known simply as the Sparr, in the old ironborn fashion of a captain's house that needs no further styling.

The Sparr and his son Steffarion stand among Balon Greyjoy's bannermen during the War of the Five Kings, and carry word of the king's death to Aeron Greyjoy alongside Gormond Goodbrother. Later, Rodrik Sparr sails with the Iron Fleet to Meereen, and it is he who brings the red priest Moqorro before Victarion Greyjoy near the Isle of Cedars.
