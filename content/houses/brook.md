House Brook was numbered among the dozen First Men houses that held the banks of the Greenblood in the earliest age of Dorne, its lords taking their turn among those eligible for election as High King of Dorne. No blazon or motto of the house has come down to the present day.

The custom of election did not save the Greenblood houses from themselves: a contested succession set three of the twelve upon one another in a war that lasted a generation and destroyed them outright. House Brook survived that reckoning, only to fall in turn when Andal swords crossed the Greenblood, ending its line centuries before Nymeria's Rhoynar would unite Dorne beneath House Martell.
