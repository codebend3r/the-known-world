House Charlton was once counted among the river kings who warred for supremacy over the riverlands, most fiercely in the chaos that followed the fall of House Justman. Lord Theo Charlton stood beside Harwyn Hardhand, King of the Iron Islands, against the Storm King Arrec Durrandon at the battle of Fairmarket, one of the many contests that kept the riverlands divided for centuries before any single line could claim it entire.

Long after its days as a royal contender had passed, House Charlton remained a power in its own right, its knights blazoning three sprigs of mistletoe upon gold within a green border. A Lord Charlton took up arms for Prince Aegon Targaryen against King Maegor I in the Battle Beneath the Gods Eye, and during the Dance of the Dragons Lord Jon Charlton died fighting for the black dragon's cause at the Battle by the Lakeshore. In time, though, the once-independent Charltons were brought low, becoming vassals of House Frey after the Freys raised the Twins some six hundred years ago.

In more recent memory, Ser Clarence Charlton rode in the wedding tourney at Whitewalls, where he was soundly unhorsed by Ser Tommard Heddle. During the War of the Five Kings, Ser Andrey Charlton and his squire Lucas Roote were slain by Lannister forces, and Lord Charlton's banners flew among the Frey host at the siege of Riverrun, where Ser Jaime Lannister marked them among the besiegers.
