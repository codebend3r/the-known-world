House Marsh is a minor northern house whose frog-blazoned banner has led some to guess at crannogman blood from the Neck, though the guess sits poorly: "Marsh" is no crannogman name, and Bowen Marsh, the only Marsh known to living memory, is a plump, devout follower of the Seven rather than a small, old-gods crannogman of Greywater Watch.

Bowen Marsh serves the Night's Watch as Lord Steward, and rises to castellan of Castle Black when Lord Commander Jeor Mormont leads the great ranging beyond the Wall. He wins the Bridge of Skulls at heavy cost and grave injury to himself, and after Mormont's death at Craster's Keep he offers his own name for Lord Commander before withdrawing it to back Janos Slynt; the choice falls instead to Jon Snow. Bowen serves the new Lord Commander loyally by his own lights, but comes to see Jon's dealings with the wildlings as a betrayal of the Watch's oath, and is among the black brothers who stab Jon Snow to death at Castle Black, one of the blades driven home with the words "for the Watch."
