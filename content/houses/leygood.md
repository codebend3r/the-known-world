House Leygood traces its ancestry to Andal adventurers granted lands and wives by the Three Sage Kings of House Gardener during the coming of the Andals to the Reach. A Leygood rode among the Caltrops during the Dance of the Dragons and died at the Second Battle of Tumbleton; his son Ser Lucas Leygood later commanded the City Watch of King's Landing under the regents of Aegon III. In the reign of Aerys I, Ser Simon Leygood was among the suitors for Lady Rohanne Webber's hand.

No Leygood has been seen in the years of _A Song of Ice and Fire_, though their badge has: Leygood men march with Lord Randyll Tarly's host pacifying the crownlands and riverlands after the Battle of the Blackwater, and Brienne of Tarth finds a shield bearing their three thunderbolts among the dead at Duskendale.
