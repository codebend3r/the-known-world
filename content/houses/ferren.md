House Ferren is a minor house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock. No words have ever been recorded for them, and no chronicle preserves a deed or a name from their history.

Their colors are among those Arya Stark spots flying with Lord Tywin Lannister's host as it marches out of Harrenhal during the War of the Five Kings, the only appearance the house has yet made in the annals of the realm.
