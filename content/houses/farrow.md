House Farrow's origins are obscure even to the maesters: no record fixes their seat, their arms, or even the region they call home, though their service under Renly Baratheon's banners at Highgarden points toward the stormlands or the Reach.

Ser Richard Farrow rode with Renly's host during the War of the Five Kings, and was among the knights who wagered beneath Highgarden's walls over who would first claim Brienne of Tarth's maidenhead. When the bet came to light, Brienne sought Richard out and beat him bloody in the melee at Bitterbridge. He did not long outlive the humiliation, falling at the Battle of the Blackwater with Stannis's fire raging around him.
