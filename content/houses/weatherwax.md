House Weatherwax is a noble house of Westeros of unrecorded region, seat, and arms. The one Weatherwax remembered by the chroniclers is Anya Weatherwax, presented as a potential bride for the young King Aegon III Targaryen at the Maiden's Day Ball of 133 AC, held after the death of Queen Jaehaera.

No match came of the ball, and no Weatherwax has been heard from since.
