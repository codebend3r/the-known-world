House Roote of Lord Harroway's Town is a noble house of the riverlands. The Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with a two-headed brown horse upon a field of wavy green, a device perhaps drawn from the legend of Old King Andahar, whose ferry boat at Harroway was known as the king's two-headed water horse. Ser Richard Roote was counted among the first knights of the Kingsguard when Aegon the Conqueror founded the order in 10 AC, and a later Lord Roote backed Prince Aegon the Uncrowned's failed war against King Maegor I.

By the Dance of the Dragons the Rootes had acquired Lord Harroway's Town outright and thrown their support behind Princess Rhaenyra Targaryen, helping capture Stone Hedge for her cause. Ser Criston Cole broke their lord's forces along the shores of the Gods Eye, and Prince Aemond Targaryen put Harroway to the torch astride Vhagar in reprisal.

During the War of the Five Kings, Lucas Roote, squire to Ser Andrey Charlton, was slain by Lannister forces, and Sandor Clegane and Arya Stark later found Lord Harroway's Town half-drowned by the flooding Trident. House Roote knelt with the rest of the river lords who aided House Lannister's siege of Riverrun, and by the time of Petyr Baelish's rule as Lord Paramount of the Trident the house answers to Baelish rather than Riverrun directly.
