House Burley is one of the mountain clans of the north, holding no true castle but ranging the high country under the style of the Burley, or Lord Burley, and bound in loose fealty to Winterfell. Their knives, a white blade set on a blue pile upon white, are said to be as plain and hard as the clansmen themselves. In an earlier age, Lothor Burley rose high enough beyond the mountains to command the Night's Watch, greeting Queen Alysanne Targaryen at the Wall in the fifty-eighth year after the Conquest; a longbowman named Billy Burley, of uncertain kinship to the clan, served House Blackwood as its finest archer during the Dance of the Dragons.

The clan paid a heavy price during the War of the Five Kings: riding to Edmure Tully's wedding at the Twins, a company of Burleys was caught on the wrong bank of the Trident and cut down by Ser Gregor Clegane's men at the fords. Maester Aemon later sent ravens begging their aid against the wildlings massing at Castle Black. When Stannis Baratheon marched into the northern mountains, the Burleys were among the clans he won to his banners for the relief of Deepwood Motte and the march on Winterfell that followed.
