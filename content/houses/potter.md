House Potter's home region, arms, and words are all lost to record. The name is carried in the field chiefly by Ser Robin Potter, a knight of no great renown.

Beneath the walls of Highgarden, Robin joined a wager among knights over who would first claim Brienne of Tarth's maidenhead, and when the bet came to light, Brienne sought him out at the Bitterbridge melee and beat him bloody, leaving a scar he carries afterward. He survives the Battle of the Blackwater none the wiser for the lesson.
