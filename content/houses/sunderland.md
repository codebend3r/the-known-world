House Sunderland of Sweetsister is the chief house of the Three Sisters, that cluster of cold and fog-bound islands set in the Bite between the Vale and the North. In ages past the Sunderlands were kings, and the petty realm of the Three Sisters answered to no greater crown; the lesser houses of the islands — the Borrells of Breakwater, the Longthorpes, and the Torrents of Littlesister — were sworn to their rule, and remain so to this day. But the Sisters had the grave misfortune to lie between two greater powers, and for a thousand years and more they were a bone gnawed by Eyrie and Winterfell alike. The islands passed from Arryn to Stark and back again in blood, and in the savagery the singers name the Rape of the Three Sisters the Sistermen suffered such horrors that those wars are remembered yet with loathing.

In the end it was the Eyrie that prevailed, and the Sunderlands bent the knee to the Falcon, surrendering their crowns to hold the Sisters as vassals of the Vale. Yet theirs has ever been a grudging fealty, for Sisterton is a haven of smugglers and wreckers, a grey and gloomy town where customs men come to grief and few questions are asked of any ship that pays in silver. The lords of Sweetsister are reckoned poor and proud and not a little treacherous by their Valemen overlords, and in the present day Lord Triston Sunderland is said to groan beneath the weight of his debts, with sons to see established and little enough to do it. So the Three Sisters endure as they always have — cold, poor, faithless, and forgotten, save by the tax collector and the storm.
