House Trant of Gallowsgrey is a lesser stormland house sworn to Storm's End, of so little renown that the Citadel cannot now name its current lord. Their arms are a black hanged man on a blue field, with the motto _So End Our Foes_ set beneath; a grim device, much of a piece with the small grim seat the house keeps in the marches. A century before Robert's Rebellion the badge was already known among the gathered heraldry of the south: at the wedding tourney at Whitewalls in 212 AC, the hedge knight Ser Duncan the Tall rode under a borrowed shield bearing a hanged man, almost certainly a Trant blazon left unaltered by its last owner, and styled himself the Gallows Knight for those few passes.

By the time of _A Game of Thrones_ the only Trant who walks the page is Ser Meryn, sworn of the white cloak under King Robert and after him a creature of the Lannisters. He rode north in the king's escort to Winterfell, was sent to take Arya Stark from the Red Keep and there crossed swords with her water-dancing master Syrio Forel; thereafter, at King Joffrey's pleasure, it was Ser Meryn who laid the rod across Sansa Stark in open court. The lordship of Gallowsgrey passes on quietly through other hands while he serves his king.
