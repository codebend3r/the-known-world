House Mollen is a house of the north sworn to House Stark, later to House Bolton after Roose Bolton's rise to the wardenship. Their arms bear a snow-covered pine tree, but no chronicle names their seat, their words, or the head of their line.

Hallis Mollen serves as captain of the guard at Winterfell after Jory Cassel's departure south, and stands beside Catelyn Stark at the Whispering Wood. She later sends him north bearing Eddard Stark's bones. During the War of the Five Kings, a broken man wearing a Mollen pine-tree badge is found hanged among the raiders of Saltpans.
