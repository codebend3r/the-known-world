House Crabb is a noble house of the crownlands, sworn directly to the Iron Throne, whose lands lie upon the wild shores of Crackclaw Point. Archmaester Gyldayn numbers the Crabbs among the half-wild folk of that country, and their most storied ancestor, Ser Clarence Crabb, is remembered as a legendary hero of the point. His ruined seat, the Whispers, is said to take its name from the severed heads of pirates, wizards, lords, and knights that Clarence brought home to his wife, who raised them from death so they might counsel him and whisper among themselves; by the time of the War of the Five Kings, the Whispers had already stood in ruins, by one account, for a thousand years.

The Crabbs produced more conventional distinction as well. Ser Clement Crabb of the Kingsguard broke thirty lances against Ser Ryam Redwyne at the great tourney held in 98 AC for King Jaehaerys I's fiftieth year upon the throne, and the king named the two knights co-champions rather than see the contest run on; two more Crabbs, Ser Rupert and Ser Clarence the Short, likewise served in the Kingsguard in their times. During the Dance of the Dragons, House Crabb declared for Rhaenyra Targaryen, joining Lord Walys Mooton to retake Rook's Rest from the greens, and a Crabb later marched on King's Landing among the riverlords after Aegon II's death. Crabbs fought again beside Rhaegar Targaryen at the Battle of the Trident during Robert's Rebellion.

In more recent memory, Dick Crabb, called Nimble Dick, guided Brienne of Tarth to the Whispers in search of a fugitive fool, only to be murdered there by the very man he sought, Shagwell, in 300 AC. Another Crabb, Jack, called Red Jack, has taken the black and stands with the Night's Watch at Castle Black.
