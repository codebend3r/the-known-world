House Stane of Driftwood Hall rules its holdfast on the isle of Skagos, a wild and stony island off the northern coast that answers only loosely, and only when it answers at all, to Winterfell. In centuries past a Stane rose to First Ranger of the Night's Watch, dying not long after taking up the black, according to the Annals of the Black Centaur.

Like the rest of Skagos, House Stane's fealty is claimed first by House Stark and, after the War of the Five Kings leaves Winterfell in ruin, by House Bolton as the North's new wardens, though the wild Skagosi have never bent easily to any southron lord's writ.
