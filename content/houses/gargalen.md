House Gargalen of Salt Shore holds the southernmost seat in all of Westeros, a castle perched on the coast of the Summer Sea where Dorne gives way to open water. Their blazon, a red cockatrice with a black snake in its beak on a golden field, speaks to the ancient and predatory character of the house. Like many Dornish lords, the Gargalens trace ties to the Rhoynar who came with Queen Nymeria when she led her ten thousand ships to the shores of Dorne, and their seat passed into the Martell orbit during the wars that followed Nymeria's landing.

By the time of _A Game of Thrones_ the house is led by Lord Tremond Gargalen. The connection between Salt Shore and Sunspear runs deep: Prince Doran Martell fostered at Salt Shore in his youth, and it was Lord Gargalen who brought the young prince word that a sister, Elia, had been born to him. Lord Tremond rode in Prince Oberyn's train to King's Landing for the wedding of King Joffrey, and later at Sunspear refused to raise his cup when a toast was called for King Tommen, a small but pointed declaration of where Gargalen loyalties lay.
