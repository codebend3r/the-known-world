House Stonetree holds broad and storied lands on the island of Harlaw, sworn to House Harlaw of Ten Towers rather than to Pyke directly. Lord Quellon Greyjoy's first wife was a daughter of the house, and she bore him three sons, Harlon, Quenton, and Donel Greyjoy, before her death.

The stone tree of House Stonetree hangs among the banners in the hall of Ten Towers, where Asha Greyjoy notes it during her visit to Harlaw seeking allies in the War of the Five Kings.
