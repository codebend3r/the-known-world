House Cobb is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Neither its arms nor its words have been recorded.

During the long regency of King Aegon III Targaryen, Lady Samantha Tarly's famous letter proposing prospective brides for the young king numbered maidens of House Cobb among its Reach candidates, and Ser Agramore Cobb was later named to Aegon's Kingsguard. In more recent days, a Lord Cobb and a Ser Harys Cobb, who may in fact be one and the same man, have marched north in the army of King Stannis Baratheon.
