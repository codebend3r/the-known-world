House Florent of Brightwater Keep is an ancient Reach house that has never quite reconciled itself to being the second house of the Reach rather than the first. They descend through the female line from Florys the Fox, a daughter of Garth Greenhand — a descent the Florents argue is older and more legitimate than the Tyrells', who came up only as the Gardener kings' stewards. They were not raised to Highgarden after the Field of Fire, and they have never quite forgiven it.

Lord Alester Florent serves at his liege's court at Highgarden, but his brother Axell is castellan of Dragonstone, and his niece Selyse is wed to Stannis Baratheon — a marriage that may yet place a Florent's blood behind the Iron Throne where no Tyrell's ever sat.
