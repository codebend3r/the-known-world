House Tudbury is a noble house of the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon. The Citadel's heraldic rolls give their arms as a brown tortoise within a yellow lozenge on green, though their words go unrecorded and no Tudbury has yet appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings.
