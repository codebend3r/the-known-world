House Suggs is a minor house of no fixed region, its lands, words, and founding unrecorded by any maester of the Citadel. Only its arms are known with any certainty: a flying pig, an odd device even among the realm's many strange sigils.

The sole Suggs of note during the War of the Five Kings is Ser Clayton Suggs, a former hedge knight of Flea Bottom who rises to serve King Stannis Baratheon as one of his queen's men.
