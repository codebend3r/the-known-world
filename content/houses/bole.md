House Bole is a noble house of the wolfswood in the north, sworn to House Glover of Deepwood Motte rather than directly to Winterfell. Asha Greyjoy thinks of them more as a clan of woodsmen than a proper landed house, a reflection of how thinly the wolfswood is settled.

During the War of the Five Kings, Lady Sybelle Glover draws scouts from House Bole to guide Stannis Baratheon's army on its march through the wolfswood toward Winterfell.
