House Mullendore of Uplands is a noble house of the Reach, seated at Uplands east of Oldtown. Their arms, a scattering of black-and-orange butterflies on white, are among the more distinctive in the Reach, and the house is reckoned one of the principal bannermen of the Hightowers of Oldtown. Their origins are obscure; no founding legend survives, but the house is old enough to have acquired lasting ties to the city by the Whispering Sound, and in 55 AC a Lord Mullendore secured a match with a Templeton bride that was celebrated as a social triumph.

The house has not always aligned itself with its Hightower overlords. During the Dance of the Dragons, the Lord of Uplands declared for the blacks while the Hightowers threw their considerable weight behind the greens, a defiance that set the two houses briefly at odds. By the time of _A Game of Thrones_, Lord Martyn Mullendore holds Uplands and keeps the peace with his lieges. His kinsman Ser Mark Mullendore rides with Renly Baratheon's host, the man's cheerful nature and his trained black-and-white monkey from the Summer Islands making him one of the more colourful figures in that army. After Renly's death Mark passes to Stannis, fights at the Battle of the Blackwater, and loses part of his left arm along with his monkey before being taken captive and bending the knee to the Iron Throne.
