House Farwynd of Sealskin Point holds the westernmost shores of Great Wyk, sworn like all ironborn lords to House Greyjoy of Pyke. Their arms and words have never been recorded in any chronicle, and their name is bound up with a scattering of kindred branches strewn along the same wind-battered coast and the lonely islands beyond it, the strangest and most remote being the Farwynds of the Lonely Light.

Aeron Greyjoy counts the Farwynds among the queerest folk of the isles, though he reckons the Lonely Light branch queerer still, whispering that its people take the shapes of seals and other creatures of the sea. At the end of the third century the house is held by Lord Triston Farwynd, its doings otherwise passing largely unremarked upon by the mainland courts of Pyke.
