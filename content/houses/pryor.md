House Pryor of Pebble holds its small seat in the Vale beneath the Eyrie, its arms a black moon in partial eclipse of a golden sun upon dusty pink. Lord Dunstan Pryor ruled the house during the long reign of King Jaehaerys I Targaryen, his wife the Lady Alys Turnberry.

No Pryor has stepped into the chronicles of the present age; the house is remembered only for that one lord's marriage in the annals of the Old King's rule.
