House Turnberry is a noble house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock, blazoning nine strawberries on a white saltire, over green and red vairy in point. Generations past, Alys Turnberry served as lady companion to Princess Saera Targaryen, fell pregnant out of wedlock and bore a bastard daughter in the Vale, and afterward married Lord Dunstan Pryor of Pebble.

Ser Lambert Turnberry rides among the household knights who escort Tywin Lannister's corpse home to Casterly Rock, and vows to bring back Tyrion's head for the insult of his father's murder. He keeps close company with Queen Margaery Tyrell's circle, a closeness that costs him dearly when the tortured Blue Bard names him as one of her lovers. Cersei Lannister, eager to be rid of Margaery, has Lambert arrested and held in Qyburn's dungeons beneath the Red Keep.
