House Terrick is a noble house of the riverlands, sworn to House Tully at the start of the War of the Five Kings and, by the war's end, counted among the bannermen of Petyr Baelish's new lordship of the Trident. The Citadel's heraldic rolls give their arms as four hawks' heads countercharged, per saltire purple and gold.

During the Faith Militant uprising against King Maegor I, Ser Alyn Terrick took up arms against the Iron Throne at the Great Fork, and Queen Visenya Targaryen answered by loosing Vhagar to burn the seats of House Terrick and four neighboring houses, Blanetree, Deddings, Lychester, and Wayn, in a single night. No Terrick appears by name in the events of the War of the Five Kings.
