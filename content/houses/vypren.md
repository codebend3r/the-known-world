House Vypren traces its arms, a black toad on a white lily pad, to the Andal warlord Vorian Vypren, whose knights were held at bay for a day and a night at the Widow's Ford by three sons of Lord Darry before the Darrys were finally cut down. In later generations a Lord Vypren backed Harwyn Hardhand, King of the Iron Islands, in his war against the Storm King Arrec Durrandon, and during the Dance of the Dragons the house declared for the blacks, Lady Sabitha Vypren carrying that loyalty into her own marriage to Lord Forrest Frey and continuing to support the black cause as a widow.

Lord Lucias Vypren, wed to Lythene Frey, attends the wedding of Edmure Tully and Roslin Frey at the Twins alongside his son and heir, Ser Damon, only for the celebration to become the Red Wedding; the Vyprens are among the guests who survive the slaughter. Lucias later tracks a band of outlaws he suspects hanged Merrett Frey as far as Fairmarket before losing their trail. His daughter Elyana marries Ser Jon Wylde, tying House Vypren by marriage to House Wylde.
