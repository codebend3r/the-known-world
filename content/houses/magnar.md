House Magnar of Kingshouse rules from the island of Skagos, its very name drawn from _magnar_, an Old Tongue word for "lord," a title the house wears as its own. Some maesters number a Magnar among the many candidates ever proposed for the Night's King of legend, though no proof of it survives any more than for the rest.

No Magnar has been seen among the great councils of the realm in living memory, though Donal Noye recalls hearing Skagosi men at Eastwatch-by-the-Sea speak of their lord by name. The house followed Winterfell in the reign of Robb Stark, and by the war's end is reckoned, however loosely from so remote an island, among the bannermen of House Bolton.
