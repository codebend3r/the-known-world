House Hetherspoon is a house of landed knights sworn to Casterly Rock; the name and location of their keep go unrecorded in the great chronicles, as do their words.

Ser Tybolt Hetherspoon's daughter Melara was a childhood companion of Cersei Lannister, and in 276 AC went with Cersei and Jeyne Farman to hear Maggy the Frog's prophecies during the Lannisport tourney. Told that she would die rather than wed Jaime Lannister as she hoped, Melara swore with Cersei that speaking of Maggy's words would only make them true, then drowned in a well soon after, her own foretelling fulfilled.
