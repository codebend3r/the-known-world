House Tawney of Orkmont is one of the strongest houses sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke, holding lands on the island of Orkmont. Their words go unrecorded, but the Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with a scourge of nettles, red and black, on white.

Lord Balon Tawney heads the house during the War of the Five Kings, while kinsman Little Lenwood Tawney, a ship captain known for playing the fiddle in camp, stands among Victarion Greyjoy's supporters at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk.
