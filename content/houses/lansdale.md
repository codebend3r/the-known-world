House Lansdale is a noble house of Westeros whose seat, region, and arms have gone unrecorded. Its one certain son in the chronicles is Ser Loreth Lansdale, who served in Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen's Queensguard during the Dance of the Dragons and was cut down by Ser Alfred Broome's men when Rhaenyra fled back to Dragonstone after the Storming of the Dragonpit.
