House Grell is a riverlands house, once sworn to Riverrun and, since the Lord Protector Petyr Baelish was granted the Lord Paramountcy of the Trident, counted among his bannermen instead. Their arms bear three red martlets on a white bend, over blue.

Ser Desmond Grell served some forty years as master-at-arms of Riverrun, present at Eddard Stark and Catelyn Tully's wedding and still training bowmen there through the War of the Five Kings, standing in for Edmure Tully at the Battle of the Fords. He remained at his post through Lord Hoster Tully's funeral and the siege that followed, and after Riverrun's surrender took the black alongside Ser Robin Ryger to finish his days on the Wall.
