House Bullock is a house of unrecorded origin whose members served House Targaryen directly rather than any regional lord; neither their seat, their words, nor their sigil have survived in any chronicle.

Their one clear moment in history belongs to Ser Merrell Bullock, commander of the castle garrison at Dragonstone under King Jaehaerys I Targaryen, who was dismissed from his post by Queen Rhaena Targaryen after failing to prevent Elissa Farman's theft of three dragon eggs. Of his two sons, Ser Alyn remained close to home while Ser Howard abandoned his wife to flee across the narrow sea to Pentos with Lady Coryanne Wylde. No Bullock is known to have appeared in the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
