House Gower is a minor house of the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End, blazoning nine yellow trefoils on a black cross over ermine. No motto for the house has ever been recorded.

At the time of the War of the Five Kings the house is represented by Ser Gerald Gower, a knight in Stannis Baratheon's service at Dragonstone. Gerald questions Stannis's newfound faith in R'hllor after the defeat at the Blackwater, yet remains loyal enough to join the conspiracy that smuggles the boy Edric Storm out of Dragonstone and away to Lys on Lord Davos Seaworth's orders.
