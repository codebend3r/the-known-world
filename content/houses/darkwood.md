House Darkwood is one of several small crownlands houses claiming distant kinship with the extinct House Darklyn of Duskendale. No record preserves their arms, their words, or the year of their founding, but the family endured the destruction that consumed the Darklyns during the Defiance of Duskendale and lives on in humbler circumstances.

No Darkwood has appeared by name in the events of the War of the Five Kings. A servant of Duskendale tells Brienne of Tarth, while she searches the town for word of Sansa Stark, that a number of Darkes, Dargoods, and Darkwoods still make their home there, the last remnants of the town's old ruling line.
