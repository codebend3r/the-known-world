House Slate of Blackpool is an ancient First Men house seated at a small castle beside the deep, still lake for which it is named. The Slates trace their lineage to the age when the North was divided among petty kings, and their ancestors ruled the waters around Blackpool as kings in their own right before the long centuries of the Kings of Winter drew every northern lord beneath a single banner. When the Starks of Winterfell consolidated their dominion, the Slates bent the knee and have held Blackpool in fealty to that house ever since.

Their seat is modest by northern reckoning, a compact holdfast making careful use of its shoreline ground, but the lake it commands is said to be among the deepest in all the North, its black water hiding any bottom. At the onset of _A Game of Thrones_, House Slate remains a loyal but unremarkable banner house of the Starks, dwelling far enough from Winterfell that their name is seldom spoken at court yet close enough that their swords answer when the Starks call the banners south.
