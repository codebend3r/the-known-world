House Botley of Lordsport is one of the principal houses sworn to House Greyjoy, holding the port town of Pyke opposite the Greyjoys' own seat. They blazon a shoal of silver fish on pale green. Their wooden stronghold was burned to the ground by King Robert Baratheon during Greyjoy's Rebellion and rebuilt afterward as a modest keep of stone; decades earlier, in 134 AC, a Lord Botley was put to the sword by westermen during Lady Johanna Lannister's invasion of the Iron Islands.

The house fractures during the War of the Five Kings. Lord Sawane Botley declares for Theon Greyjoy's claim to the Seastone Chair against Euron Crow's Eye and is drowned for it; his brother Germund inherits the lordship by pledging Euron his fealty instead of Sawane's sons, while half the Botley lands are stripped away to reward House Wynch for their earlier support. Sawane's son Tristifer, the lawful heir, is left to press his claim and his old affections for Asha Greyjoy, who refuses his proposal even as she carries his house's silverfish banner among her supporters on Harlaw.

Wex Pyke, the mute bastard son of Sargon Botley, is taken up as Theon Greyjoy's squire and survives the sack of Winterfell by hiding in the branches of its heart tree, later revealing to Lord Wyman Manderly the truth of the massacre and Rickon Stark's whereabouts. At the kingsmoot on Old Wyk, Germund shouts his support for Euron while Tristifer shouts for Asha, a small echo of the split running through their whole house.
