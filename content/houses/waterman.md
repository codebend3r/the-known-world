House Waterman is a minor house of the north, of unrecorded seat and words. Its arms, brown crossed oars on a white field between a pair of blue flaunches, hint at a home somewhere along the White Knife, though no chronicler has set the matter down plainly.

The house followed Winterfell's banner in the years of Robb Stark's kingship, and answers to the Dreadfort as of the War of the Five Kings, though no Waterman of note has yet made a mark upon the histories.
