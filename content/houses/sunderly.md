House Sunderly of Saltcliffe is one of the noble ironborn houses of the isle of Saltcliffe, sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke. Neither their motto nor their arms appear in the novels themselves; the Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with a drowned man, pink and pale, adrift in a blue-green sea as fish nibble at his limbs.

A daughter of the house, remembered only as Lady Sunderly, became the second wife of Lord Quellon Greyjoy and bore him five children, Balon, Euron, Victarion, Urrigon, and Aeron, making House Sunderly the maternal line of the Greyjoy siblings who dominate the Iron Islands during the War of the Five Kings. Members of the house are among the captains gathered at Pyke when Balon calls his banners to claim the driftwood crown.
