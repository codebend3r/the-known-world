House Cray is a house of crannogmen dwelling in the Neck, in the north, sworn to House Reed of Greywater Watch rather than directly to Winterfell, as is the way of the bog-dwelling houses of that country.

No Cray has appeared among the notable figures of the realm during the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
