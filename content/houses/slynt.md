House Slynt is the newest of noble houses, raised up in a single stroke of royal favor. Janos Slynt, Commander of the City Watch of King's Landing, was made Lord of Harrenhal by King Joffrey I Baratheon as payment for betraying Eddard Stark, and for one season the jumped-up butcher's son ruled from the cursed castle on the Gods Eye.

The favor did not last. Tyrion Lannister, distrusting Janos, stripped him of Harrenhal and sent him to the Night's Watch, though he let the innocent Janos's son Morros keep the title of Lord Slynt with lesser lands. Harrenhal itself passed to Petyr Baelish after the Battle of the Blackwater. At the Wall, Janos schemed against Jon Snow, refused a lawful order, and was beheaded by the new Lord Commander himself with the blade Longclaw, leaving Morros and his younger siblings Jothos and Danos to carry the family name in King's Landing.
