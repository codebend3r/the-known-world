House Yelshire is a minor house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden, blazoning a black battering ram on barred green and gold beneath a white chief. No maester has recorded their words, their founding, or a single member of the house by name; they are known only by their banner among the many lesser lords who answer Highgarden's call.
