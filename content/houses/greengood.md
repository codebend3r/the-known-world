House Greengood is a house of crannogmen dwelling in the Neck, sworn not to Winterfell directly but to House Reed of Greywater Watch, as is the custom of the bog-born clans. No blazon, motto, or seat-name has been recorded for them, only their presence among the marsh-dwelling peoples of the swamp.

No Greengood has appeared by name in the chronicles of the War of the Five Kings; like much of crannogman history, theirs is kept apart from the record of the green-land lords.
