House Melcolm of Old Anchor is reckoned one of the principal houses sworn to the Eyrie, holding Old Anchor in the Vale of Arryn beneath a rusted anchor blazon. Neither their words nor any deed of theirs is recorded in the chronicles of the realm.

No Melcolm has been named among the players of the game of thrones in living memory, though their standing among Jon Arryn's bannermen suggests a house of some consequence still in the Vale's affairs.
