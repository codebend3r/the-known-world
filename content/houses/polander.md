House Polander is known to the heralds of the Citadel by arms alone, an unusual coat of gold lions clutching red hearts beside a broken silver sword. No record fixes the house to any region or seat, and its words are lost, if it ever spoke any.

No Polander has appeared in the chronicles of the present age; the house survives only as a name and a blazon in the Citadel's rolls of arms.
