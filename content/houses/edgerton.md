House Edgerton is a noble house of the crownlands, answering directly to House Baratheon of King's Landing. Semi-canon heraldry quarters their shield with black-and-white half-fusily panels opposite a flaming wheel on indigo, though their words have never been recorded.

No chronicle preserves any history of the Edgertons, and no member of the house has appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings or after.
