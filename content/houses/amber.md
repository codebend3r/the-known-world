House Amber was a noble house of the First Men who held lands in what is now the north long before it bent the knee to Winterfell. No record survives of their arms, their words, or the seat they once kept.

The Ambers were vanquished by the Kings of Winter of House Stark in the long wars that forged the northern kingdom, and the house did not survive to see the Seven Kingdoms made. Nothing remains of them today but their name in the old chronicles.
