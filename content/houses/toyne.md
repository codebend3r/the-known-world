House Toyne was a knightly stormlands house of old standing, bearing a winged heart upon their golden shields beneath the words _Fly High, Fly Far_. They served the kings of Storm's End in centuries past and the Iron Throne after the Conquest, and one of their number was raised to the white cloak in the reign of King Aegon IV Targaryen — Ser Terrence Toyne, called among the finest swords of his day. In 178 AC the king found Terrence abed with his mistress Bethany Bracken, and Aegon the Unworthy had the Kingsguard knight dismembered piece by piece in his sight, that Bethany might see her lover unmade before she too was put to death. Terrence's two brothers came to court swearing the king's blood in answer, and fell upon Aegon at the next dawn; Ser Aemon the Dragonknight stepped between them and the king and slew them both, though he took his own death-wound in the doing.

What remained of the Toynes after that black morning was driven out of Westeros, their lands forfeit, their name attainted. They went over the narrow sea and there sank into the long exile of all defeated houses, but the winged heart did not entirely fade. When Ser Aegor Rivers, called Bittersteel, founded the Golden Company three quarters of a century later out of the wreckage of the Blackfyre cause, Toyne swords were among the first to swear with him; and in the days of the Five Kings the captain-general of the company was Ser Myles Toyne, called Blackheart, whose shield still bore a winged heart upon a field of gold. He died before the trumpets of _A Game of Thrones_ sounded, and with him passed the last of the line that ever any maester knew by name.
