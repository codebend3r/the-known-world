House Gaunt is a minor crownlands house sworn to House Baratheon of King's Landing, its arms three black lances upon pink between black flaunches.

The one Gaunt remembered by the chroniclers is Ser Gwayne Gaunt, a knight of Aerys II Targaryen's Kingsguard, cut down by Ser Symon Hollard during the Defiance of Duskendale. No Gaunt is recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
