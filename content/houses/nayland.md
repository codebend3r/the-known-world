House Nayland of Hag's Mire holds land within the Twins' domain, likely landed knights sworn to House Frey; neither their arms nor their words have ever appeared in any chronicle. Ser Lucas Nayland, a knight of some repute, came down from Hag's Mire to attend the tourney at Whitewalls.

Ser Raymond Nayland was among the suitors who wagered on bedding Brienne of Tarth, and paid for it when she unhorsed him at the melee Renly Baratheon hosted at Bitterbridge.
