House Hawthorne is a noble house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock, said to trace its roots to the golden age of the First Men in the Dawn Age or the Age of Heroes. No motto of theirs is recorded, and no deed of note survives in the chronicles beyond their arms of interlocked thorn and flower.

No Hawthorne has been recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
