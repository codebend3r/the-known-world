House Spicer is among the newest of westerland houses, sprung from a saffron-and-pepper trader who wed a maegi out of Essos, a witch woman the smallfolk of Lannisport came to call Maggy the Frog. Their son's own wealth won him elevation from Lord Tytos Lannister, and in time the family's fortune bought his granddaughter Sybell Spicer a match with Gawen Westerling, lord of an ancient but impoverished line, a marriage that left the younger Westerlings scorned as descendants of upjumped merchants.

During the War of the Five Kings, Ser Rolph Spicer served as castellan of the Crag while his sister Sybell ruled it as lady in her captive husband's stead, and together they steered Sybell's daughter Jeyne into Robb Stark's bed and, disastrously for the Starks, into his marriage bed as king, shattering his pact with House Frey. Sybell's secret bargain with Tywin Lannister, an arrangement bought with promises of good marriages for her children, helped set the Red Wedding in motion; she later admitted to taking measures to keep Jeyne from bearing Robb's heir. For his part in the betrayal, Rolph was raised to Lord of Castamere, the very seat where Tywin Lannister had annihilated House Reyne a generation before, and where the Rains of Castamere are still sung.
