House Boggs of Crackclaw Point is a noble house of the crownlands, holding lands on that wild and thinly-settled peninsula. Neither their arms nor their words appear in any chronicle. Ser Mortimer Boggs, a knight and jouster of the house, attended the wedding tourney at Whitewalls in 212 AC, and in 283 AC men of House Boggs rode with Prince Rhaegar Targaryen at the Battle of the Trident during Robert's Rebellion.

No Boggs of Crackclaw Point is recorded to have appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings; the house's current head goes unrecorded.
