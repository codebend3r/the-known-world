House Wylde of the Rain House is one of the principal houses sworn to House Baratheon, blazoning a blue-green maelstrom on gold. Lord Jasper Wylde, called Ironrod, served as master of laws to Kings Viserys I and Aegon II Targaryen and backed the greens in the Dance of the Dragons, dying a traitor's death when Rhaenyra Targaryen took King's Landing in 130 AC; his kinswoman Coryanne Wylde's scandalous life, meanwhile, was set down for posterity in _A Caution for Young Girls_. Later, Ser Willem Wylde served in Daeron II's Kingsguard and fought for Prince Aerion at the trial of seven at Ashford Meadow in 209 AC, the same tourney where Ser Duncan the Tall first noted the house's arms among the gathered heraldry.

During the War of the Five Kings, Ser Gladden Wylde rides with Lord Eddard Stark's party sent to arrest Ser Gregor Clegane and is killed by Lannister men at the Mummer's Ford, while Ser Gawen Wylde, master-at-arms of Storm's End, dies in a cell after being caught trying to slip out of the besieged castle to surrender to Mace Tyrell's host. Lord Casper Wylde, the current Lord of the Rain House, is said to throw his support first behind Renly Baratheon and then Stannis after Renly's death.

By the time Stannis marches north following the Battle of the Blackwater, his uncle Ser Ormund Wylde rides with him, but the Rain House itself falls behind them to the Golden Company under Ser Laswell Peake, its defenders committed elsewhere.
