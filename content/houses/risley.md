House Risley of Risley Glade is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. The Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with a striking device, a black knight astride a rearing black horse bearing a golden lance and a white shield charged with the same device in miniature, a coat of arms scholars have linked to the Arthurian legend of the Black Knight.

Ser Victor Risley, Knight of Risley Glade, declared for the greens during the Dance of the Dragons and conspired with the Caltrops to murder the Two Betrayers. Raised afterward to King's Justice for the boy king Aegon III by Lord Unwin Peake, he was ultimately exposed in a plot against Queen Daenaera Velaryon and died demanding trial by combat against Ser Gareth Long. Generations later, in the reign of Aerys I, a Lord Risley wed a daughter of Lord Ambrose Butterwell and attended the ill-fated tourney at Whitewalls, where his son Ser Eden Risley had his leg crushed beneath his own horse.
