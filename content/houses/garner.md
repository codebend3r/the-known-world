House Garner is a minor house of the westerlands, sworn to Casterly Rock, blazoning three grey owls upon plates of white on a green field. No words have ever been recorded for them.

The one Garner remembered by name is Ser Alyn Garner, who rode in the tourney at Ashford Meadow in 209 AC during the reign of Daeron II Targaryen. No member of the house is recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
