House Dryland of Hellgate Hall ruled as Kings of the Brimstone in the age before Dorne's petty realms were bound into one, one among the scattered First Men kingdoms that once divided the deserts and hills of the south. Neither their arms nor their words have come down to the present day.

Their line ended with King Lucifer Dryland, the last man to hold Hellgate Hall and the crown of the Brimstone. When Nymeria led her Rhoynar across the narrow sea and Mors Martell took up her cause, Lucifer was among the six defeated kings of Dorne sent to take the black at the Wall, and House Dryland passed out of the world with him.
