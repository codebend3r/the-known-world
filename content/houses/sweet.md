House Sweet is a minor house whose exact home has gone unrecorded, though Lord Sweet's support for Stannis Baratheon suggests a seat somewhere in the stormlands or the crownlands. No maester has set down their arms or their words.

Lord Sweet stands among Stannis's queen's men and accompanies the king north to Castle Black during the War of the Five Kings.
