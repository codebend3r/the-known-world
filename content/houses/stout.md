House Stout of Goldgrass is an ancient First Men house, its short and blunt name itself a mark of that old blood, holding a modest keep near the eastern gate of Barrowton and sworn to House Dustin rather than to Winterfell directly.

Ser Wynton Stout serves long years as a ranger of the Night's Watch, standing castellan of Castle Black during Lord Commander Mormont's great ranging beyond the Wall, while Ronnel Stout musters with Roose Bolton's men to hold the Trident. At home, Lord Harwood Stout hosts Ramsay Bolton in Barrowton after Lady Barbrey Dustin bars him from Barrow Hall, and later travels to Winterfell for Ramsay's wedding to the girl passed off as Arya Stark.
