House Wells of the North, not to be mistaken for the unrelated house of the same name in Dorne, followed Winterfell's banner under the Starks and now answers to the Dreadfort in the years of the War of the Five Kings.

Ser Theodan Wells, called Theodan the True, joins the Warrior's Sons in King's Landing, though the maesters cannot say for certain whether he springs from this northern line or its Dornish namesake.
