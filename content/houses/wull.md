House Wull is the formal name of the Wulls, the most powerful of the northern mountain clans, holding the lands west of the mountains along the Bay of Ice since long before the Andals came to Westeros. Their chief is known simply as "the Wull," though at Winterfell he is given the courtesy of "Lord Wull." Generations of ironborn raiding along the Bay of Ice have left the clan with a lasting hatred of the Iron Islands.

Theo Wull, called Buckets, was one of the six companions who rode south with Lord Eddard Stark during Robert's Rebellion. He fell at the tower of joy in the Red Mountains of Dorne in 283 AC, and Eddard buried him there in a cairn built from the tower's own stones.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings the clan is led by Hugo Wull, called Big Bucket, who commands more fighting men than any other northern mountain clan by Jon Snow's reckoning. The Wulls answer Maester Aemon's call for aid against the wildlings at Castle Black, and Big Bucket brings his clansmen into Stannis Baratheon's army when the king marches on Winterfell, the mountain clans' old fealty to Stark passing to Dragonstone in the war's turning.
