House Ashwood is a noble house of the north, sworn to House Stark of Winterfell. The Ashwoods once held the Wolf's Den, though the family is no longer recorded as its master, and neither their arms nor their words have survived in any chronicle.

Whether the house still endures is not entirely certain; no Ashwood has appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings, and the north's shifting fortunes under House Bolton have left the family's fate unrecorded.
