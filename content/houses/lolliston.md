House Lolliston is a minor noble house of the riverlands, its arms three oaken barrels on white, its words and founding lost to the maesters' record. No Lolliston has been named among the players of the game of thrones in living memory.

The house followed the Tullys of Riverrun through the War of the Five Kings, and by its end answers instead to Petyr Baelish, raised Lord Paramount of the Trident in the war's aftermath.
