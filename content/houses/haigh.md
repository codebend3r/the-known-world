House Haigh is a riverlands house sworn to House Frey of the Twins, its lord Ser Leslyn Haigh wed to Lady Perriane Frey, one of Lord Walder's many daughters. A Haigh knight is remembered for a lesser reason at the tourney of Harrenhal, where his squire accosted a crannogman and he was afterward unhorsed by the mysterious Knight of the Laughing Tree and told to teach the boy honor.

The Haighs stood with their Frey kin through the Red Wedding: Ser Leslyn lost half an ear trying to run down the Greatjon, while Ser Harys, Leslyn's heir, fought a knight of House Vance with Black Walder Frey's help. In the war's aftermath, Harys and his brother Ser Donnel rode with their cousin Ser Arwood Frey to answer the sack of Saltpans, and Haigh banners were counted among those flying over the Frey camp when Jaime Lannister came to lift the siege of Riverrun.
