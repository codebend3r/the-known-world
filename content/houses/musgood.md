House Musgood is a stormlander house sworn to House Baratheon, quartering a golden pavilion on blue with a green laurel crown on white. Their seat and words are unrecorded. Their most notable son took the black rather than remain in the stormlands: Jack Musgood, known as Sleepy Jack, rose to Lord Commander of the Night's Watch and held the Wall during the King-Beyond-the-Wall Raymun Redbeard's invasion of the north.
