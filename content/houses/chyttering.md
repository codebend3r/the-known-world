House Chyttering is a lordly house of the crownlands, sworn to House Baratheon of King's Landing and blazoning three gold bendlets enhanced upon a white field. Their words, if any survive, are not recorded.

Lord Chyttering died fighting for Stannis Baratheon at the Battle of the Blackwater, leaving the house to his young son, Lucos, called "Little Lucos" by the diminished remnant of Stannis's following. In the grim aftermath of that defeat, Stannis numbered the boy lord among the few good men still left to him.
