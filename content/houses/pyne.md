House Pyne holds lands on Crackclaw Point in the crownlands, sworn to the Baratheons of King's Landing. Neither their arms nor their words have ever been recorded.

The house's one recorded distinction is an unnamed Pyne who served in the Kingsguard in an earlier age, a knight remembered by title alone.
