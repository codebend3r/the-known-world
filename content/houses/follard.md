House Follard is a crownlands house sworn to House Baratheon, its words "None so Wise" sitting oddly against its most famous embarrassment: sent to Braavos in 133 AC as part of Lord Manfryd Mooton's embassy, Lord Follard spread bribes among the Sealord's keyholders and magisters alongside Lord Cressey, then abandoned his post entirely, choosing to remain in the Free City with a courtesan rather than sail home.

Later Follards prove steadier. Dick Follard, called Deaf Dick, takes the black and dies defending Castle Black when wildlings storm it from the south while Lord Commander Mormont is beyond the Wall. Ser Perkin Follard serves among Stannis Baratheon's queen's men during the War of the Five Kings.
