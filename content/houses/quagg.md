House Quagg is a crannogman house of the Neck, sworn to House Reed of Greywater Watch rather than to Winterfell directly, one of the small lines that make their home among the bogs and moving waters of the marsh.

No Quagg has yet stepped into the wider chronicles of the Seven Kingdoms.
