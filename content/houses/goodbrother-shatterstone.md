House Goodbrother of Shatterstone holds a seat on Old Wyk, apart from the Hammerhorn line of Great Wyk that gives the Goodbrother name its renown. AWOIAF itself is uncertain whether the two are truly kin, listing Shatterstone only as a possible cadet branch of House Goodbrother of Hammerhorn, so no descent is asserted here.

No blazon, words, or named lord of Shatterstone survives in the chronicles; the house is known only by its seat and its place among the many small holdfasts that answer, however loosely, to Pyke.
