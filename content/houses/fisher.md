House Fisher of the Misty Isle was one of the earliest dynasties of First Men kings to claim dominion over the Trident, ruling from a seat whose very location has been lost to the maesters of the present age. Some chronicles name the Fishers the oldest line of river kings, others the second, and the _Annals of the Rivers_ compiled at Peasedale set them as the third. In the days of the Hundred Kingdoms a petty king of Fisher blood was numbered among those who held the mouth of the Blackwater Rush, and a crowned catfish, spotted grey on blue, was the device they bore upon their shields.

The Fishers passed out of all chronicle long before the Andals came down upon the rivers — slain, it is said, in wars with the Storm Kings out of the east or the ironmen out of the Sunset Sea, though no maester can now say which. In time the river lords looked to the Mudds of Oldstones for their kings, and the line of the catfish faded from memory, save in the songs of a few villages along the Blue Fork that yet recall a drowned isle and a crown that came up out of the water.
