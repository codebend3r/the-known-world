House Hutcheson is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. No motto of theirs is recorded, and no deed of note survives in the great chronicles beyond the hand and sun they bear upon their shield.

No Hutcheson has been recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
