House Harlaw of Grey Garden is a cadet branch of House Harlaw, sworn to the Lord of Harlaw at Ten Towers rather than to Pyke directly. Its master at the time of the War of the Five Kings is Ser Harras Harlaw, called the Knight, who wields the Valyrian steel blade Nightfall and stood for years as Lord Rodrik Harlaw's own designated heir.

That inheritance slipped from him during the turmoil after Balon Greyjoy's death: Harras stood as one of Asha Greyjoy's three champions at the kingsmoot, but when King Euron Greyjoy named him Lord of Greyshield after the taking of the Shields, Victarion Greyjoy read it as a calculated move to buy Harras's loyalty at the cost of Rodrik's succession, and it was Hotho Harlaw of the Tower of Glimmering who became heir to Ten Towers in his place.
