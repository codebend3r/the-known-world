House Greenhill's home region is not recorded, though maesters have guessed at the Reach, and some suspect the name itself began as a bastard's, in the fashion of House Longwaters, softened over generations from the westerlands' bastard surname of Hill.

Whatever its origins, the house's sole known representative in recent years is Ser Courtenay Greenhill, a household knight numbered among those said to keep close company with Queen Margaery Tyrell in King's Landing.
