House Broome is a noble house of the westerlands sworn to House Lannister, though neither its heraldry nor its words have been set down by any maester. The house's clearest mark on history was made in piety rather than war: the Broomes openly sided with the Faith Militant when the Poor Fellows and Warrior's Sons rose against King Maegor I Targaryen, a stand that ended when Maegor the Cruel loosed Balerion upon their castle, burning it alongside the seats of the Falwells, the Lorches, and the Doggetts.

Lady Lucinda Broome, celebrated for her devotion, wed Prentys Tully, Lord of Riverrun, during that same troubled reign, and her younger sister Ella Broome is remembered alongside her. Generations later, a Ser Alfred Broome rode in service to the Targaryens during the Dance of the Dragons. No Broome has appeared in the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_, and whether the house shares any blood with the similarly named House Broom remains a question no chronicler has resolved.
