House Goodbrother of Downdelving is a cadet branch of the ancient Goodbrother line of the Hammerhorn, seated at Downdelving on Great Wyk alongside its kin at Corpse Lake and Crow Spike Keep. It is among the least-recorded of the Goodbrother lines: sworn like the rest of that scattered name to House Greyjoy of Pyke, but with no lord, deed, or blazon of its own yet set down in any surviving chronicle.
