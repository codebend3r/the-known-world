The Freefolk are not a noble house but a free and scattered people, the descendants of those First Men who dwelt north of the line where Bran the Builder would raise his Wall, and who refused thereafter to bend the knee to any lord of the south. The maesters and lords of the Seven Kingdoms call them wildlings; they call themselves the free folk, and the name is the whole of their politics.

Beyond the Wall there are no castles, no taxes, no laws save custom, and no kings save those a man chooses to follow. From age to age a war chief or red sorcerer has gathered the scattered clans to his banner and named himself King-Beyond-the-Wall: Joramun, Gendel and Gorne, Bael the Bard, Raymun Redbeard, and others remembered only in song. Each in his time has hammered together a host great enough to threaten the Wall, and each has been broken upon it. Between such risings the free folk live as villagers, hunters, spearwives, raiders, ice river clans, cave-dwellers, giants, and Thenns, owing nothing to one another but custom and kin.

By the events of _A Game of Thrones_ they are gathered again, this time under Mance Rayder, a Night's Watch deserter turned king. He has called every band south of the Frostfangs to come down out of the haunted forest, not for plunder, but to flee south of the Wall, for the cold things long thought dead are stirring once more in the long night beyond the trees.
