House Redbeard is no ancient line but a title conjured at the Wall in 300 AC for Gerrick Kingsblood, a free folk raider who lets Jon Snow pass him and his people south of the Wall in exchange for oaths against the Others. Gerrick boasts descent from Raymun Redbeard, the King-beyond-the-Wall slain at the Battle of Long Lake in the time of Jon's grandfather's grandfather, though Tormund insists Gerrick's true forebear is the Red Raven, Raymun's younger brother, who fled that same battle rather than die in it.

Whatever his true blood, Ser Axell Florent presents him to Queen Selyse's court as "Gerrick of House Redbeard, King of the Wildlings," and Selyse promptly betroths his three daughters to Axell and his knights Ser Brus Buckler and Ser Malegorn of Redpool, the marriages to be sworn in the name of the Lord of Light, while Gerrick's son is yielded up as a hostage of the Night's Watch. A king in name only, Gerrick finds his new house has cost him his children as surely as it gave him a name.
