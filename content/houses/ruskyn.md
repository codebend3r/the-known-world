House Ruskyn is a noble house of Westeros whose region of origin has gone unrecorded. No arms, words, or seat survive in any known account.

The sole Ruskyn remembered by history is Ser Raynard Ruskyn, raised to Lord Commander of the Kingsguard in 135 AC during the Lysene Spring and the regency that followed the Dance of the Dragons.
