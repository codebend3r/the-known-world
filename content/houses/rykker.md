House Rykker of Duskendale rose to hold the town and the castle of the Dun Fort after the Defiance of Duskendale broke House Darklyn in 277 AC, taking as their arms two black warhammers crossed upon a white saltire on blue. At the court of King Aerys II Targaryen, a Lord Rykker once joked that Lord Tywin Lannister shat gold while Owen Merryweather talked of raising taxes on wine, and was stared into silence for it. When Robert's Rebellion came, Ser Jaremy Rykker fought on for Aerys and surrendered to House Lannister's men during the Sack of King's Landing; given the choice of the headsman or the Wall by Tywin Lannister himself, he chose to take the black, and served as a ranger under Benjen Stark until a wight killed him at Castle Black.

During the War of the Five Kings, Ser Helman Tallhart and Robett Glover marched on Duskendale only to be met and broken by Lord Randyll Tarly, their retreat cut down by Ser Gregor Clegane. Lord Renfred Rykker later rode with Tarly to Maidenpool, leaving his one-legged castellan Ser Rufus Leek to hold the Dun Fort and watch over his wife and children in his absence.
