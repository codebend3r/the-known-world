House Myatt is a westerlands house sworn to House Lannister, blazoning a spotted treecat upon mud brown. No seat or words of theirs are recorded, and no Myatt is named among the living. A Myatt banner of twenty to thirty westermen is spied near the Gods Eye during the War of the Five Kings, and a Myatt banner rides among those escorting Lord Tywin Lannister's corpse home to Casterly Rock.
