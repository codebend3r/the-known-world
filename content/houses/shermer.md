House Shermer of Smithyton holds a minor seat in the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Little is recorded of their lineage, and no Shermer has yet appeared by name in the chronicles of the realm.

During the War of the Five Kings, House Shermer answers Renly Baratheon's summons and declares for him alongside the great muster of Reach lords at Highgarden.
