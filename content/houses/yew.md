House Yew is a line of landed knights in the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock, of First Men stock old enough to claim descent from the legendary blind archer Alan o' the Oak. Fittingly, they blazon a golden longbow on white between two crimson flaunches. No words of theirs are recorded.

During the siege of Riverrun, Ser Manfryd Yew, the house's head, is set with Ser Raynard Ruttiger by Lord Daven Lannister to watch the river and see that no boats slip past downstream. Ser Brynden Tully escapes the castle regardless, swimming away on a moonless night, and Ser Jaime Lannister reflects that had Yew, Ruttiger, or their men heard a splash in the dark, they would have taken it for nothing more than a turtle or a trout.
