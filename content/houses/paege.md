House Paege is a house of landed knights sworn to Riverrun, blazoning twining red-and-white serpents on black. In ages past they supported Harwyn Hardhand, King of the Iron Islands, in his war against the Storm King Arrec Durrandon, and in 43 AC a Lord Paege backed Aegon the Uncrowned's rebellion against Maegor I Targaryen. Ser Harbert Paege later rode in the Whitewalls tourney, falling to Ser Franklyn Frey after five tilts.

The Paeges have married deep into House Frey: Sallei Paege wed Jammos Frey and bore him Big Walder, Dickon, and Mathis, while her sister Sylwa wed Whalen Frey and bore Hoster and Merianne. Ser Robert Paege rides as a companion of Lord Edmure Tully, and Ser Damon Paege takes Hoster Frey as his squire.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings, Ser Halmon Paege heads the house, and his son Garrett squires for Ser Jaime Lannister, accompanying him north to lift the siege of Riverrun as both squire and hostage. Though the Paeges have renewed their fealty to the Iron Throne, Jaime notes their banners are conspicuously absent from the rivermen besieging the castle.
