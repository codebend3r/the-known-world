House Flint of Flint's Finger is a noble house of the North, seated at the crooked spit of land on the southern shore of Blazewater Bay that lends the family its name, north of the Flint Cliffs and east of Cape Kraken. Theirs is reckoned the lesser of the two lowland Flint branches, weaker than the Flints of Widow's Watch across the kingdom, and the mountain Flints hold that both houses alike are sprung from younger sons of the First Flints who came down out of the high country in search of food, land, and wives. No words of theirs survive in the histories, and their arms are known only from a semi-canon blazon that gives them a grey stone hand, a fitting device for a hard folk clinging to a stony finger of shore.

The Finger has not always been a Flint holding. In the deep past it was wrested by the ironborn, who kept it until a weak High King of the Iron Islands let it slip in the century after Gerold Lannister, King of the Rock, put the isles to the torch. By the time of _A Game of Thrones_ the house is a modest bannerman of Winterfell, its lord unrecorded in the annals of the war; and when the Red Wedding breaks House Stark and Roose Bolton is named Warden of the North, the Flints of Flint's Finger bend the knee to the Dreadfort as their new liege.
