House Horpe is a noble house of the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon. Ser Harys Horpe, called Death's Head Harry, was one of the Warrior's Sons put to death in Maegor I Targaryen's trial of seven.

Ser Richard Horpe stands among Stannis Baratheon's queen's men, and with Ser Justin Massey drags the king from the field to save his life after the disaster at the Blackwater. He goes on to become one of Stannis's principal sworn swords, serving as his second-in-command during the march north against the Boltons, while a Horpe knight bearing moths upon his shield kills the wildling Dormund in the battle beneath the Wall.
