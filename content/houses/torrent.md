House Torrent of Littlesister is a noble house of the Three Sisters, ruling Littlesister as vassals of House Sunderland of Sisterton rather than swearing to the Eyrie directly. Neither their arms nor their words have been recorded.

Lord Alesandor Torrent holds Littlesister during the War of the Five Kings, one of the vassal lords of the Three Sisters answering to Sunderland rather than to the Eyrie's distant authority.
