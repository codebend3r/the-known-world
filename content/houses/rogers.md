House Rogers of Amberly is a noble house of the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End. The Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with nine silver unicorns encircling a maze upon a black field, though their words are not recorded.

Ser Corwin Rogers rode in the tourney at Ashford Meadow in 209 AC, while in an earlier generation Ser Harrold Rogers wed Branda Stark, a daughter of Rodrik Stark called the Wandering Wolf, tying the house briefly to the blood of Winterfell.
