House Morrigen of Crow's Nest is a lordly house of the stormlands, sworn since time out of mind to the Storm Kings at Storm's End and after them to the Baratheons. They blazon their shields with a black crow in flight on a storm-green field; their words, if any were ever sworn, do not survive the books that the Citadel keeps. The Morrigens have ever been a martial line, and the histories first set them at the head of an army at the Last Storm, where Dickon Morrigen and the Bastard of Blackhaven shared the van of King Argilac Durrandon and were both burned out of their saddles by Queen Rhaenys and her dragon Meraxes.

What followed bound the house to the Targaryens by blood and fire alike. In the reign of Maegor the Cruel, Ser Damon the Devout commanded the Warrior's Sons out of King's Landing and challenged the king to a trial of seven; Maegor won by a hand's breadth, and Damon died on the floor of the sept. After Maegor's death, Damon's young nephew Ser Gyles came to court bearing the family's loyalty in his own hands, slew Ser Harrold Langward of Maegor's Kingsguard in a trial by combat, and was raised to Lord Commander of King Jaehaerys's Kingsguard for his pains. A later Morrigen, Ser Grance, was remembered chiefly for riding at the great tourney at Ashford Meadow, and in the years before Robert's Rebellion the Lord of Crow's Nest fell into a quiet border quarrel with Lord Armond Connington that drew Prince Rhaegar's ear.

By the events of _A Clash of Kings_ the house is held by Lord Lester Morrigen, with his brothers Ser Richard, his heir, and Ser Guyard, called Guyard the Green, who rode in Renly Baratheon's Rainbow Guard. When Renly was murdered, Crow's Nest bent to King Stannis; Guyard led the van of his foot at the Battle of the Blackwater, and was cut down by Ser Garlan Tyrell wearing the dead king's armour. Lord Lester held to Stannis after the river, and is thought to have ridden north with him; in his absence Crow's Nest itself was taken by Ser Tristan Rivers and the Golden Company.
