House Buckwell of the Antlers is a noble house of the crownlands, holding its seat at Antlers and sworn directly to the Iron Throne, now House Baratheon of King's Landing. Its knights bear a rack of golden antlers on a field of vair beneath the words "Pride and Purpose."

When the realm turned against King Maegor I Targaryen in favor of Prince Jaehaerys, Lord Buckwell was among the last lords still gathered at Maegor's call; when the king died mysteriously upon the Iron Throne, Lord Buckwell fled across the narrow sea to Pentos alongside Lord Rollingford rather than face the new regime. Centuries later, Ser Jarman Buckwell took the black and commanded the scouts of the Night's Watch's great ranging beyond the Wall, surviving the massacre at the Fist of the First Men when so many of his brothers did not; Lord Commander Jon Snow would remember him as one of the good men the Watch had lost.
