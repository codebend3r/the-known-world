House Cave is a minor house of the crownlands, holding lands on Crackclaw Point. Neither their arms nor their words have survived into memory, and the Caves themselves maintain that they are sworn only to the Iron Throne rather than to any intermediate lord.

Little of the house's history is recorded beyond the fact that one of its members once served in the Kingsguard, a mark of unusual favor for so obscure a line. No Cave has appeared among the notable figures of the realm during the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
