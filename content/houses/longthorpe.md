House Longthorpe of Longsister holds Longsister, one of the Three Sisters in the Vale of Arryn, sworn not to the Eyrie directly but to House Sunderland of Sweetsister, the paramount of the Sisters alongside the Borrells of Breakwater and the Torrents of Littlesister. Neither their arms nor their words have come down through any chronicle.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings, Lord Rolland Longthorpe rules Longsister as its lord, one of the three island lords of the Bite alongside Sunderland of Sweetsister and Torrent of Littlesister.
