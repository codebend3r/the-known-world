House Cupps is a minor noble house whose home region, words, and blazon have gone unrecorded even in the Citadel's heraldic rolls. No maester has set down when the Cupps rose to their station or what banner they fly into battle, and the house is listed among the Seven Kingdoms' many families of uncertain rank, remembered chiefly by name alone.

The only Cupps of note in the annals of the realm is Ser Jon Cupps, who wed Leyla Hightower, a daughter of Lord Leyton Hightower of Oldtown. The match has led some maesters to place the Cupps lands somewhere near Oldtown or the Arbor, though no record confirms it, and the house's true seat, if it holds one, remains as much a mystery as its arms.
