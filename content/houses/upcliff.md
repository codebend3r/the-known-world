House Upcliff of Witch Isle dates back to the Age of Heroes and has long carried a sinister reputation among the Vale's noble lines. During the coming of the Andals, Ursula Upcliff, a reputed sorceress who styled herself bride of the Merling King, fought for Robar II Royce, High King of the Vale, and died at the hand of Torgold Tollett in the Battle of the Seven Stars. The house's arms show a cresting wave, sea green on black; their words are not recorded.

The marriage of Arwen Upcliff to Alester II Arryn, King of Mountain and Vale, brought Witch Isle into House Arryn's realm, and the Upcliffs have remained sworn to the Eyrie ever since.
