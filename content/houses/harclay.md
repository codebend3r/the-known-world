House Harclay is one of the mountain clans of the north, dwelling in the hills beyond the wolfswood and led by a chieftain styled the Harclay or Lord Harclay after the fashion of the clans. Their arms show three blue moons, waxing, full, and waning, on a white bend over blue.

Ronnel Harclay served as a ranger of the Night's Watch, riding out with the great ranging against Mance Rayder and surviving the slaughter at the Fist of the First Men to take command of the survivors at Craster's Keep after Thoren Smallwood's death; Maester Aemon later sent to his house for aid in the defense of Castle Black. When King Stannis Baratheon marched into the northern mountains, House Harclay was among the clans won over to his cause, joining his host for the relief of Deepwood Motte and the march on Winterfell.
