House Staedmon holds Broad Arch in the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End. Little else of their lineage has come down through the histories.

During the War of the Five Kings, Lord Alesander Staedmon presumably follows the stormlands in declaring first for Renly Baratheon and then for Stannis after Renly's death. He is taken prisoner at the Battle of the Blackwater and brought before King Joffrey Baratheon, to whom he swears fealty rather than face the block.
