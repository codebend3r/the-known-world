House Drinkwater is a house of landed knights in Dorne, sworn to House Yronwood of Yronwood. No record sets down their arms or words, and little beyond a single knightly household is known of them.

Ser Drinkwater, a landed knight, is survived in the tale of the realm by his son, Ser Gerris Drinkwater, and by twin daughters, one of whom gave Prince Quentyn Martell his first kiss. Ser Gerris later rides with Quentyn across the narrow sea on his ill-fated mission to Meereen, seeking Quentyn's marriage to Daenerys Targaryen and the dragons that might come with it.
