House Willum is a Lordly house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden, its arms three crossed silver longswords beneath a dragonbone skeleton on a white chief. Shields bearing crossed swords once hung upon the walls of the Shieldhall at Castle Black, by Jon Snow's reckoning, suggesting that Willums of an earlier age may have taken the black.

When the War of the Five Kings comes, Lord Willum declares for Renly Baratheon, and his sons Josua and Elyas attend the feast at Bitterbridge in his name. After Renly's death the house follows Stannis Baratheon, but Lord Willum and both sons are taken captive at the Battle of the Blackwater and bend the knee to King Joffrey I Baratheon rather than share Stannis's fate.
