House Rodden is a minor house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. No record of their arms, words, or seat has survived in any widely known account.

The sole Rodden remembered by the chronicles is Richard Rodden, who joined the Caltrops in support of King Aegon II Targaryen during the Dance of the Dragons and died of wounds taken at the Second Battle of Tumbleton, the day after the fighting ended.
