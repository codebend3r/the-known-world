House Hunt is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tarly of Horn Hill. No words of theirs are recorded, though their arms, a brown deer bound and slung upon a pole, hint at the house's namesake pursuit.

Ser Hyle Hunt meets Brienne of Tarth at the gates of Maidenpool and follows her across the riverlands, by way of the Quiet Isle and Saltpans to the inn at the crossroads, where he proposes marriage and is refused; he is known to have fathered at least one bastard daughter, and possibly more. His cousin, Ser Alyn Hunt, hunts outlaws in the country around Maidenpool in those same years.
