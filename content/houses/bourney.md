House Bourney is a minor lordly house of uncertain region, its seat said to lie somewhere near the Blackwater Rush, placing it within reach of the crownlands, the riverlands, or the Reach without settling the matter. No maester has recorded its coat of arms or its words, and the house appears in no chronicle before the Dance of the Dragons.

Its one moment upon the page belongs to Lord Owain Bourney, who declared for Rhaenyra Targaryen at the start of the Dance but turned his cloak for the greens during the First Battle of Tumbleton, only to be run through the eye by Lord Unwin Peake for the betrayal. No Bourney is known to have played any part in the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
