House Brune of Brownhollow is an old family of landed knights from Crackclaw Point in the crownlands, sworn since the Conquest to the Iron Throne and, since Robert's Rebellion, to House Baratheon of King's Landing directly. Archmaester Gyldayn judged the Brunes of Crackclaw half-wild, and the house's own legends run to rough men made good: the Brothers Brune, champions who are said to have brought peace to the Point, and Bernarr Brune, a squire who cut down the outlaw who had slain his lord and won a knighthood for it, later standing among the seven champions of Maegor I's trial by combat. It is not recorded which branch of the Brunes, Brownhollow or their cousins of the Dyre Den, either man belonged to.

The Brownhollow Brunes declared for the black dragon in the Dance, joining Lord Walys Mooton to lift the siege of Rook's Rest, and rode again for the Targaryens two centuries later at the Battle of the Trident. In the present age the Knight of Brownhollow is Ser Bennard Brune, while a distant and disowned cousin, Ser Lothor Brune, called Lothor Apple-Eater, made his own name far from Crackclaw: turned away by the family when he came seeking kin after his father's death, he took service with Petyr Baelish, fought at the Blackwater, and rose to command the household guard of the Eyrie.
