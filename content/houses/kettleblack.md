House Kettleblack is a knightly house of the crownlands with an undistinguished past and a reputation, in the present, for unscrupulous ambition. Its members run tall and dark-haired with hooked noses, and Oswell Kettleblack has served Lord Petyr Baelish for years, his sons Osmund, Osfryd, and Osney known to Petyr since childhood. Osmund makes his name as a sellsword in the Stepstones and the Disputed Lands before the family's fortunes turn toward King's Landing.

During the War of the Five Kings the three brothers pose as sellswords for hire, secretly Baelish's men all along, even as Queen Cersei Lannister believes she alone commands their loyalty. Osmund wins a cloak in the Kingsguard after Ser Boros Blount's disgrace, Osfryd captains first Cersei's red cloaks and then the City Watch's gold cloaks, and Osney serves as the queen's blunt instrument, murdering a High Septon on her order and later attempting to seduce Queen Margaery Tyrell into a scandal of Cersei's design. The scheme collapses when the High Sparrow tortures a confession from Osney, dragging Cersei down into the Faith's custody alongside him, and by the war's end Ser Kevan Lannister has the elder Kettleblack brothers imprisoned, weighing exile to the Wall against trial by combat.
