House Harlaw of the Tower of Glimmering is one of several cadet branches sprung from the main line of House Harlaw of Ten Towers, alongside the Harlaws of Grey Garden, Harlaw Hall, and Harridan Hill. Like the others, it holds no lordship in its own right, but answers Ten Towers as a sworn house of the ruling branch on the isle of Harlaw.

At the time of the War of the Five Kings, the Tower of Glimmering is held by Hotho Harlaw, called Hotho Humpback, a cousin of Lord Rodrik Harlaw the Reader, who has a twelve-year-old daughter.
