House Kellington is a minor house of the stormlands, sworn directly to House Baratheon of Storm's End. Their words are lost, and no chronicle names a Kellington among the lords and knights who have shaped the history of the stormlands; the locked book upon their arms keeps whatever history they have to themselves.
