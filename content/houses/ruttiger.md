House Ruttiger is a house of landed knights sworn to Casterly Rock, in the westerlands. The Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with an orange bend engrailed upon black; their words are not recorded.

During the siege of Riverrun, Ser Daven Lannister charged Ser Raynard Ruttiger and Ser Manfryd Yew with watching the river so that no boats slipped past downstream. Brynden Tully the Blackfish escaped Riverrun regardless, swimming away on a moonless night; Ser Jaime Lannister later reflected that had Ruttiger or Yew heard a splash in the dark, they would have taken it for nothing more than a turtle or a trout.
