House Waxley of Wickenden holds its seat in the south of the Vale of Arryn, sworn to House Arryn of the Eyrie. Their arms show six candles, white with red flames in silver holders, upon a grey field bordered in black, and their words, "Light in Darkness," suit a house whose gifts are made of beeswax and hope.

Ser Edmund Waxley, the Knight of Wickenden, sends the widowed Lady Lysa Arryn scented candles in hopes of winning her hand, though the courtship comes to nothing before her death at the Eyrie. A knight bearing the six candles of House Waxley later attends Lord Nestor Royce's interrogation of Alayne Stone, and Lord Waxley is seen among the guests at Lord Lyonel Corbray's wedding in Gulltown.
