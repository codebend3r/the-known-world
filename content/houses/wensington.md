House Wensington is a minor house of the stormlands, its words "Sound The Charge" fit for arms that bear two crossed golden trumpets beneath a chief of three black Baratheon stags. The stags atop the shield have led some maesters to wonder, unproven, whether the house was founded by a scion of House Baratheon or the older House Durrandon.

Wensington banners are seen at the Wall among Stannis Baratheon's host during his expedition to relieve the Night's Watch, suggesting the house declared first for Renly and then followed Stannis after Renly's death at Storm's End, with at least part of the house remaining loyal to his cause beyond the Blackwater.
