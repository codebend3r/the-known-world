House Blackberry is a minor house of the Seven Kingdoms whose precise home is not recorded, though its service to House Baratheon of Dragonstone suggests roots in the crownlands or the stormlands. Neither their arms nor their words have survived in any chronicle.

Jate Blackberry serves as captain of the gate at Dragonstone before the Battle of the Blackwater, and is feared dead by the time Ser Davos Seaworth returns seeking him. Omer Blackberry, one of Stannis Baratheon's king's men, is counted among the protectors who spirit Edric Storm away from Dragonstone.
