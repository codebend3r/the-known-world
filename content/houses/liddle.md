House Liddle is the formal name given to one of the mountain clans of the north, dwelling in the high peaks north of the wolfswood. Their chief is known simply as "the Liddle," though at Winterfell he receives the courtesy of "Lord Liddle." Their words go unrecorded in any account, as clansmen who keep to the high places rather than the halls of the Seven Kingdoms.

Bran Stark encounters a Liddle in the mountains on his journey to the Wall, who shares oatcakes and blood sausage without asking his name. When Stannis Baratheon marches on Deepwood Motte, Lord Torren Liddle's son Morgan, called "Middle Liddle," rides among the mountain clansmen who answer the king's call, fighting in the battle and later making his peace with Asha Greyjoy for cursing her in the field.
