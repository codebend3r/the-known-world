Lord Rupert Falwell, called the Fighting Fool, led his house's swords into the Faith Militant uprising against Maegor I Targaryen, falling at the Great Fork in 42 AC after cutting down two of the king's Kingsguard. Maegor answered by loosing Balerion over the Falwell seat and putting it to the torch.

Generations later, during the misrule of Lord Tytos Lannister, a private quarrel between Falwell and House Jast went unreferred to Casterly Rock; the two houses settled it with a melee that left nine dead and twenty-seven maimed without resolving the grievance. Falwell banners are counted among Lord Tywin Lannister's host as it marches out of Harrenhal during the War of the Five Kings.
