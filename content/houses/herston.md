House Herston is a noble house of the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End. No motto of theirs is recorded, and no deed of note survives in the great chronicles beyond the rooster's head they bear upon their shield.

No Herston has been recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
