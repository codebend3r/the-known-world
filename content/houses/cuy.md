House Cuy of Sunhouse is a noble house of the Reach, seated at Sunhouse, a castle on the southern coast east of the Redwyne Straits and southeast of Oldtown. The Cuys trace their origins to Andal adventurers who crossed the narrow sea during the coming of the Andals and were granted lands and wives by the Three Sage Kings of House Gardener. Their blazon, six yellow flowers on blue, is said to echo the fields of sunflowers that give Sunhouse its second name, Sunflower Hall.

Sworn to House Hightower of Oldtown, the Cuys have kept to the western marches of the Reach through the centuries with little note. At the outset of the War of the Five Kings, Lord Cuy's house threw its strength behind Renly Baratheon when the younger Baratheon raised his banner in the south. Ser Emmon Cuy was named to Renly's Rainbow Guard, earning the byname Emmon the Yellow for the golden color of his cloak. When Renly was slain by sorcery outside Storm's End, Ser Emmon rushed into the king's pavilion alongside Ser Robar Royce, only to be cut down in turn by a grief-maddened Ser Loras Tyrell.
