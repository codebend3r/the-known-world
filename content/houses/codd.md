House Codd is a noble house of the Iron Islands, sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke and blazoning a silver codfish upon a field whose tincture has gone unrecorded. Their words, unusually candid for so proud a people, are "Though All Men Do Despise Us," an acknowledgment of the low esteem in which other ironborn hold them: their men reputed thieves and cowards, their women said to lie with their own fathers and brothers.

Despite this reputation, the Codds have not lacked for notable men in recent years. Lucas Codd, called "Left-Hand Lucas," attached himself early to Euron Greyjoy's cause and claimed the horn Dragonbinder from Cragorn at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk after Euron was chosen king, later joining his kinsmen in the sack of the Shields. Tom Codd, known as "Bloodless Tom," captains the longship _Lamentation_ in the Iron Fleet. Dagon Codd, left behind at Moat Cailin when Victarion Greyjoy sailed for the kingsmoot, was killed by Adrack Humble for refusing to accept the Bastard of Bolton's terms of surrender.
