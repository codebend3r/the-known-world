House Norcross is a reach house sworn to House Tyrell, marked by an embattled black cross on white. Ser Tyler Norcross fought for the greens in the Dance of the Dragons, joining Lord Ormund Hightower's host and the conspiracy of the Caltrops before falling at the Second Battle of Tumbleton; a later Norcross, Ser Jeffory, called Neveryield, won renown in the Kingsguard.

Ser Hosman Norcross, a household knight at Brightwater Keep, wed Delena Florent after Robert Baratheon despoiled her on her cousin Selyse's wedding night, an arrangement Ser Colin Florent preferred to giving his daughter to a dwarf; the child of that night, Edric Storm, was fostered instead with Lord Renly Baratheon. Ser Bayard Norcross, a household knight of Highgarden, is arrested on Cersei Lannister's order as part of her scheme against Queen Margaery Tyrell.
