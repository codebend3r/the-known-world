House Humble is counted among the lesser houses of the Iron Islands, descended from thralls and salt wives rather than conquerors, though some Humbles claim roots on Great Wyk. Ironborn custom holds that twins run often in the blood.

At the kingsmoot on Old Wyk, Quellon Humble casts his lot with Euron Greyjoy, while Will Humble loses a wager and is made to eat his own boot; Quellon is later seen at the feast for the taking of the Shields, tearing down a tapestry to wear as his own. Adrack Humble, one-armed, serves in the garrison Victarion leaves at Moat Cailin, slays Dagon Codd when he refuses safe passage, and is flayed alive on Ramsay Bolton's order after the ironborn surrender the fortress. Burton Humble sails with Victarion's fleet toward Slaver's Bay aboard the Iron Victory.
