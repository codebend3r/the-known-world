House Foxglove's home region is a matter of guesswork among the maesters; some point to the Reach, noting the fox charge borne by House Florent of Brightwater Keep, but neither the Foxgloves' arms nor their seat has ever been set down in any surviving record.

During the War of the Five Kings, both a Lord Foxglove and a Ser Willam Foxglove march north in Stannis Baratheon's army, though whether these are one man described twice or two members of the same house remains uncertain.
