House Goodbrother of Corpse Lake is one of the cadet branches of the ancient Goodbrother line of the Hammerhorn, seated at Corpse Lake on Great Wyk alongside its kin at Crow Spike Keep and Downdelving. Like the rest of that scattered and numerous name, it is sworn in the end to House Greyjoy of Pyke, though no lord of Corpse Lake, nor any blazon or words of the branch, has yet found its way into the surviving chronicles of the isles.
