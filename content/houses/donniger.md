House Donniger is a noble house of the Vale, sworn to House Arryn of the Eyrie. Semi-canon heraldry gives them a red sun rising from a grey-green sea against a yellow sky, though the house's words have never been set down.

No chronicle preserves any deed of the Donnigers, and no member of the house has appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings or after. They are known to the wider realm, if at all, only by their banner.
