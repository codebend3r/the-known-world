House Wynch of Iron Holt holds a seat on the far side of Pyke from Greyjoy's own castle and ranks among the Greyjoys' principal bannermen, flying a bloody moon on purple; no motto of theirs survives. In 133 AC, Alester Wynch commanded the Greyjoy garrison at Faircastle after Lord Dalton Greyjoy's death, and was slain there by Gunthor Goodbrother in a quarrel over Lysa Farman.

Wynch longships and captains stand among Balon Greyjoy's forces at Pyke during the War of the Five Kings, while Ser Aladale Wynch, a ranger of the Night's Watch, falls to wildlings at the Bridge of Skulls.

After Balon's death, Lord Waldon Wynch is the first man to hail Euron Greyjoy as King of the Isles and the North, and is rewarded with half the lands of House Botley for it, even as other Wynches gather in support of Victarion Greyjoy's own claim at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk.
