House Greenwood was a noble house of the First Men who once held lands in what is now the north, ruling in their own right before the Starks of Winterfell rose to dominate the region. No record survives of their arms, their words, or the seat they once kept.

Like so many petty kingdoms of the First Men, House Greenwood was vanquished by the Kings of Winter in the long wars that forged the northern realm, and nothing of the house endures today save its name among the old chronicles.
