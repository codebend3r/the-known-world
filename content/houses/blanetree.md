House Blanetree is a noble house of the riverlands, sworn to House Tully of Riverrun and blazoning maple leaves of green and brown strewn upon a field of yellow. During the Faith Militant uprising, Queen Visenya Targaryen rode Vhagar against the rebellious river lords and put the seats of House Blanetree to the torch in a single night, alongside those of Deddings, Lychester, Terrick, and Wayn.

The house survived that dragonfire to endure into the present age; Zhoe Blanetree is wed to Ser Tytos Frey of the Twins, one of the many small threads binding the riverlands' lesser houses to House Frey.
