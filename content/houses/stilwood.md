House Stilwood is presumed a westerlands house of minor standing, sworn to House Lannister, though nothing of its lineage or arms has been recorded.

Joss Stilwood squires for Ser Gregor Clegane during the War of the Five Kings, riding with the Mountain's company as it burns and pillages its way through the riverlands.
