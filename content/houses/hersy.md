House Hersy of Newkeep is a noble house sworn to House Arryn of the Eyrie, seated at Newkeep on the north-eastern coast of the Vale. Their arms show a white winged chalice upon a field of pink, a device whose religious character recalls the Andal kings who carried the Faith of the Seven across the Narrow Sea and into the mountains of the Vale. The Hersys are reckoned among the older Andal lines, holding their stretch of coast since the great invasion swept the First Men from the high passes and the lowland shores alike.

By the time of _A Game of Thrones_ the house answers to the Eyrie as it has for thousands of years, one of many proud but lesser lordships holding the Vale's long coastline for their liege. They have raised no great names in recent memory, yet endure in the way of old houses that have learned to outlast the ambitions of their neighbors.
