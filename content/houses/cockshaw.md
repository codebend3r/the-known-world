House Cockshaw is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden, blazoning three feathers, red, white, and gold, upon a field of black.

The house's most memorable scion is Lord Alyn Cockshaw, who competed in the Whitewalls tourney during the unrest of the Second Blackfyre Rebellion, where Ser Glendon Flowers unhorsed him. Alyn schemed to have Ser Duncan the Tall murdered by having him thrown down a well, but the plot went awry, and it was Alyn himself who plunged to the bottom and almost certainly drowned for his treachery.
