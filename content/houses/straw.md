House Straw's seat and region are lost to the record, though the name may share kinship with House Errol or House Selmy of the stormlands, whose sigils likewise draw on the harvest, a haystack and stalks of wheat.

In 50 AC, Ser Casper Straw led thirty armed septons in defending the novice Princess Rhaella Targaryen when Ser Orryn Baratheon attempted to take her by force from her motherhouse at the Starry Sept in Oldtown. Generations later, a Dick Straw serves far from Westeros as a sellsword in the Windblown company across the narrow sea.
