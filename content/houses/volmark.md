House Volmark of Volmark holds large lands on the island of Harlaw and answers to House Harlaw of Ten Towers rather than to Pyke directly, their arms a black leviathan upon a grey sea. Their blood runs back to Black Harren himself: after Harren Hoare burned in Harrenhal, Lord Qhorin Volmark claimed the driftwood crown as King of the Iron Islands through his grandmother, a sister of King Harwyn Hardhand, only to be slain by Aegon the Conqueror during the invasion of the Iron Islands in 2 AC. A later Lord Volmark died by the sword when westermen invaded under Lady Johanna Lannister in 134 AC.

That Hoare blood still matters generations later: at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk, Tarle the Thrice-Drowned argues young Lord Maron Volmark has the strongest claim on the Seastone Chair of any man present, though Maron himself throws his support behind Victarion Greyjoy. Volmark banners answer Lord Rodrik Harlaw's summons to the kingsmoot in Asha's name, gathering at Ten Towers before sailing to Old Wyk.

After the ironborn seize the Shield Islands, King Euron Greyjoy names Maron Lord of Greenshield as well as Volmark, a calculated honor. Victarion reads it as a bid to buy the young lord's loyalty and prestige through his descent from Black Harren, giving House Volmark holdings in both the Iron Islands and the Reach at once.
