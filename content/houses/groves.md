House Groves's seat and region are unrecorded, and maesters are uncertain whether it shares any blood with the similarly obscure House Graves, though nothing in the surviving record confirms the kinship.

Its one notable son is Ser Regis Groves, raised to King Aegon III Targaryen's Kingsguard in 131 AC as one of four former supporters of Rhaenyra's blacks honored with a white cloak in the years after the Dance of the Dragons. No Groves has been recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
