House Whitehill is a minor house of the north, its arms a white inverted pile on indigo beneath an arch of four-pointed stars. It followed Winterfell's banner under the Starks in the days before the War of the Five Kings.

By the time Roose Bolton is named Warden of the North, House Whitehill answers to the Dreadfort in his stead, though no Whitehill of the novels has yet stepped forward to be named or given a seat of record.
