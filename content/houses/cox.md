House Cox of Saltpans is a house of landed knights in the riverlands, presently sworn to House Baelish as Lord Paramount of the Trident, having formerly answered to House Tully of Riverrun. Ser Quincy Cox holds the title of Knight of Saltpans.

When the town of Saltpans was put to the sword and fire by outlaws wearing the stolen helm of Sandor Clegane, Ser Quincy shut himself within his castle rather than come out to defend the smallfolk, and was found there, safe, when riders from the Twins arrived to investigate the ruin. Septon Meribald later spoke in his defense at the Quiet Isle, observing that Quincy's sons and good-sons were dead or away at war, his grandsons still too young to fight, and his household reduced to two married daughters — he had, in truth, little left to face the outlaws with.
