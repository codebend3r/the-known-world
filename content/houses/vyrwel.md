House Vyrwel of Darkdell is one of the principal houses sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden, its arms a silver wyvern within a red double tressure on sable. During the reign of King Aerys I Targaryen, Lord Vyrwel and his lady wife attended the fateful wedding tourney at Whitewalls, where a man in Vyrwel's own service falsely claimed to be one of Lord Bloodraven's spies after the Second Blackfyre Rebellion collapsed, and was killed for the lie by a knight sworn to House Costayne.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings, Igon Vyrwel serves as captain of the guards at Highgarden itself, a mark of the house's continued trust with the Tyrells.
