House Bolling is a house of landed knights in the stormlands, sworn to House Baratheon of Storm's End. Their arms bear a golden stag beneath a bend sinister upon the Baratheon and Durrandon colors reversed, a device the heralds read as marking their founder a bastard, or the descendant of one, of those storm kings' line.

Ser Theo Bolling, wed to Lady Elinor Costayne, fought for King Maegor I Targaryen against the Poor Fellows, only for the king to have him arrested, charged with treason, and executed the same day, 47 AC, so that Maegor might take Elinor as his own wife. During the War of the Five Kings, the hedge knight Ser Creighton Longbough claims to have slain Ser Herbert Bolling at the Battle of the Blackwater.
