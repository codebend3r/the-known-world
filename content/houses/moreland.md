House Moreland is an ancient westerlands line of First Men blood, tracing its descent to the legendary Pate the Plowman in the Dawn Age or Age of Heroes. Neither their arms nor their motto appear in the great chronicles, though semi-canon heraldry records them as per pale barry russet and green, and no seat of theirs is named.

Lord Robin Moreland, head of the house at the close of the third century, rides among the lords escorting Tywin Lannister's funeral procession from King's Landing back to Casterly Rock, wearing russet and green beneath a cloak of crimson silk.
