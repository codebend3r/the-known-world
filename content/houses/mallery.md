House Mallery was one of the last houses to stand behind King Maegor I Targaryen in 48 AC, though its lord fled home the moment Maegor died upon the Iron Throne. Ser Raymund Mallery, a knight of Maegor's Kingsguard, turned his cloak for the new king Jaehaerys I alongside Ser Olyver Bracken, but rather than reward the pair Jaehaerys sent them to the Night's Watch; two years on, Raymund rebelled against the Watch itself, hoping to make himself lord of a castle at the Wall, and died fleeing north of it at wildling hands.

In the years of _A Game of Thrones_, Lord Eddard Stark chooses Lord Lothar Mallery to ride with Beric Dondarrion's party hunting Ser Gregor Clegane, and Lothar drowns attempting to cross the Mummer's Ford.
