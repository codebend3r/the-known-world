House Chelsted is a lordly house of the crownlands, sworn to House Baratheon of King's Landing, blazoning its arms per bend green and white with a spiked mace crossed by a silver dagger.

Its best-remembered scion is Lord Qarlton Chelsted, who served King Aerys II Targaryen first as master of coin and then, in the king's final days, as his penultimate Hand. When Qarlton tried to dissuade the increasingly unstable king from setting King's Landing ablaze rather than surrender it, Aerys had him seized, dipped in wildfire, and burned alive for his defiance, replacing him with the pyromancer Rossart. Ser Jaime Lannister remembers him only as the "mace-and-dagger" Hand, one more casualty of the Mad King's final descent.
