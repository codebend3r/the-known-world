House Brune of the Dyre Den holds its seat in Crackclaw Point in the crownlands, sworn first to the Iron Throne and, since Robert's Rebellion, to House Baratheon of King's Landing. Cousins to the landed knights of Brune of Brownhollow, the Dyre Den Brunes share the same half-wild reputation Archmaester Gyldayn gave the Crackclaw houses, and the same claimed forebears: the legendary Brothers Brune who pacified the Point, and Bernarr Brune, hero of Maegor I's trial of seven, though the records do not say which Brune branch either man truly belonged to. Neither the house's arms nor its words have ever been set down.

The Dyre Den Brunes rode for Rhaenyra Targaryen in the Dance of the Dragons, helping break the siege of Rook's Rest before marching on King's Landing itself after Aegon II's death, and later stood with Prince Rhaegar at the Battle of the Trident. In the present age Lord Eustace Brune rules from the Dyre Den, his sons and grandsons riding off to the War of the Five Kings in turn, while travelers such as Brienne of Tarth have passed within sight of his walls on the road to the Whispers.
