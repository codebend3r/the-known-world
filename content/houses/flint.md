House Flint of the mountains, calling themselves the First Flints, is one of the oldest houses in the North, dwelling in the high peaks north of the Wolfswood since before the Long Night. They trace their line to the ancient First Men kings of Breakstone Hill, who bent the knee to the Kings of Winter only after long centuries of resistance, and are reckoned by maester and clansman alike as the root stock from which the other Flint branches grew. The Flints of Widow's Watch and Flint's Finger are held to be the blood of younger sons who came down from the mountains in search of food, land, and wives; the mountain Flints have never let them forget it.

Unlike the lords of the lowlands, the chief of the clan carries no lordly style among his own people, being known simply as The Flint, though Winterfell has always rendered him the courtesy of "Lord Flint." Their most famous daughter is Arya Flint, who wed Rodrik the Wandering Wolf, youngest son of Lord Beron Stark, and through her granddaughter Lyarra became the great-grandmother of Eddard Stark. Old Nan held that it was Arya's blood, running through Brandon Stark, that made the boy such a fool for climbing. In the generations before the events of _A Game of Thrones_ the clan aided Lord Cregan Stark in hunting down the free folk raiders of Sylas the Grim, and they have kept that loyalty unbroken to the present day.

At the opening of the War of the Five Kings the clan is led by Torghen Flint, called Old Flint, an aged chief whose sons Donnel and Artos ride to Castle Black to join Stannis Baratheon's march on Winterfell, hoping to free the girl they believe to be Ned's daughter from the Boltons. Old Flint himself travels to Castle Black for the wedding of Alys Karstark, a reminder that for all their remoteness the mountain Flints count themselves among the loyal bannermen of Winterfell and answer when their king has need.
