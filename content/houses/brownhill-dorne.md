House Brownhill was one of the dozen First Men houses that held the Greenblood in ancient Dorne, its lords among those from whom a High King of Dorne might be chosen. No record of its arms or words has come down through the ages, and it is not to be confused with the unrelated stormlander house of the same name.

The Greenblood houses turned on one another after a contested election, a war of a generation's length that destroyed three of the twelve outright. House Brownhill weathered that bloodletting only to be conquered when the Andals came to Dorne, its line ending long before Nymeria's Rhoynar would forge the Dornish kingdom anew under House Martell.
