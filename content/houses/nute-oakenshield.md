Nute the Barber was no lord born, but a thickset, bandy-legged ironborn raider and Lord Victarion Greyjoy's best man aboard the Iron Victory, so named for a youth spent throwing axes so true it was said he could shave a man with one from twenty yards. He stood among Victarion's champions at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk, and later fought in the taking of the Shield Islands from the Reach, killing a man aboard a captured Serry ship.

When Euron Greyjoy's fleet took Lord Hewett's Town on Oakenshield in 300 AC, the new king named Nute lord there in Humfrey Hewett's place, alongside Andrik the Unsmiling at Southshield, Harras Harlaw at Greyshield, and Maron Volmark at Greenshield. Victarion, who suspected his brother had raised Nute up chiefly to steal his best man away, urged him to refuse; Nute pointed out that Victarion could offer him no lands of his own, and accepted the lordship gladly.
