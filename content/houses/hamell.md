House Hamell is a minor house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock, blazoning an erminois maunch on pink within an erminois border. No words for the house have been recorded.

No Hamell has appeared by name in the chronicles of the War of the Five Kings, though the house's banner was counted among those that escorted Lord Tywin Lannister's body home to Casterly Rock.
