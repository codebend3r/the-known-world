House Mandrake once loomed large in the histories of the Seven Kingdoms, or so Jon Connington remembers hearing, though no chronicle preserves their seat, their arms, or the manner of their fall. AWOIAF's own accounting numbers them among the exiled houses of Westeros, their bloodline, if any remains, scattered across the narrow sea rather than settled on any lord's lands.

Whether the name survives at all is uncertain: Ser Marq Mandrake, once enslaved, serves as a captain of the Golden Company and swears his sword to Prince Aegon Targaryen's invasion of Westeros, but sellswords in the free companies may call themselves whatever they please, and it is far from certain that Marq carries any true Mandrake blood.
