House Shett of Gulltown is one of two branches of House Shett in the Vale, the other holding Gull Tower; it is not known which line descends from the ancient Shett kings, First Men rulers of Gulltown who called themselves Kings of the True Men, among them Osgood Shett, Third of His Name. Whichever branch first held the crown, both are landed knights today, and the Gulltown Shetts answer to House Grafton, Lord of Gulltown.

Ser Uther Shett, a knight of Gulltown, is among the noble men of the Vale who join Lord Yohn Royce's attempt to depose Petyr Baelish as Lord Protector during the years after the War of the Five Kings.
