House Shepherd is one of the youngest houses of the Iron Islands, a captain's line of small stature that has never held great lands or ancient renown. No record of their arms or words has ever come to light.

Daegon Shepherd, called the Black Shepherd, captains the longship Dagger, and stands with his kinsman Qarl Shepherd among the ironborn who gather around Victarion Greyjoy at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk called to choose Balon Greyjoy's successor.
