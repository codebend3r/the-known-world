House Dunn is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Semi-canon heraldry gives them a shield divided per pale black and pink, bearing a two-headed pelican counterchanged between the two fields, though their words are not recorded.

When the War of the Five Kings divides the great houses of the realm, House Dunn declares for Renly Baratheon. No individual Dunn is named among the events that follow.
