House Kenning of Kayce was founded by Herrock Kenning, an ironborn warrior of House Kenning of Harlaw, during the twilight of the driftwood kings. Called the Whoreson, Herrock used his warhorn to signal the whores of Kayce to fling open a postern gate, letting his men take the town for House Lannister; his horn survives as the family's cherished heirloom. The Kennings may once have ranked only as landed knights, since later lords used the style Knight of Kayce, but by the War of the Five Kings the head of the house is styled Lord of Kayce outright.

Lord Terrence Kenning's banners march with Tywin Lannister's host out of Harrenhal, and later help escort Tywin's body home to Casterly Rock. Ser Kennos of Kayce, sworn to Terrence's household, rides beside Ser Jaime Lannister in his campaign through the riverlands.
