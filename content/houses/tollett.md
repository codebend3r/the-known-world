House Tollett of the Grey Glen is a noble house of Andal descent seated in the Vale of Arryn, sworn to the Royces of Runestone. Their lineage stretches to the wars of the Andal Invasion, when Torgold Tollett, called the Grim, rode with Ser Artys Arryn at the Battle of the Seven Stars. A giant of a man who fought bare-chested and laughing, Torgold carved a seven-pointed star into his flesh and wielded twin axes against the First Men kings. He fell to King Robar II Royce, slain by the blade Lady Forlorn, but his deeds on that field bought the Tolletts a lasting place among the houses of the Vale.

In the centuries that followed the Andal conquest, House Tollett settled into its role as a minor lordship beneath the Royces, holding the Grey Glen against the austere winters of the Vale. The house reached beyond its borders at least once in the Targaryen era, when Ser Jon Tollett served in the Kingsguard of King Maegor I, though his tenure won him no particular honour. At the time of _A Game of Thrones_, Lord Uthor Tollett holds the Grey Glen and answers the call of Bronze Yohn Royce. The grim words of the house, When All Is Darkest, find their most visible expression in Eddison Tollett, called Dolorous Edd, a younger son who took the black and served as a steward of the Night's Watch on the Wall.
