House Varner is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Their ancestry reaches back to the coming of the Andals, when adventurers from Andalos crossed the narrow sea and were granted lands and wives by the Three Sage Kings of House Gardener. The weasel upon their ermine blazon is said to have been the device of the Varner line since its days on Andalos, carried across the sea along with the seven-pointed star and the Andal tongue.

At the outset of the War of the Five Kings, Lord Steffon Varner marched with the great host that gathered beneath the walls of Bitterbridge in support of Renly Baratheon's claim. When Renly died by shadow-work in his tent, Steffon was among the lords who transferred their allegiance to Stannis. He rode with Stannis to Storm's End and later fought at the Battle of the Blackwater, where he was badly wounded in the knee and taken captive. He bent the knee to King Joffrey I Baratheon thereafter.
