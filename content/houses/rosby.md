House Rosby of Rosby is an old noble house of the crownlands, holding the castle and village of Rosby on the eastern shore of Blackwater Bay, northeast of King's Landing. The house traces its line back through the Andal settlement of the region, though no chronicle records a founding lord by name. Their blazon, three red chevronels on ermine, speaks to heraldic age, and the Rosbys long served the succession of lords and petty kings who held the lands about the Blackwater before Aegon the Conqueror remade the realm. When Rhaenys Targaryen flew Meraxes from the Aegonfort during the Conquest, Rosby yielded without a fight, becoming one of the first seats in the crownlands to bend the knee to House Targaryen. In reward, Lord Jon Rosby was named Castellan of Sunspear and Warden of the Sands during the First Dornish War, though he met his end when Princess Meria Martell returned and threw him from the Spear Tower in what the chronicles call the Defenestration of Sunspear.

In the centuries that followed, the Rosbys remained close to the Crown, their fortunes rising and falling with the tides of Targaryen politics. Ser Rayford Rosby died defending King Maegor I in his trial of seven, and his lord chose to drink hemlock rather than survive the king, leaving a young son to receive mercy from Jaehaerys I at Dragonstone. During the Dance of the Dragons the house was nearly undone by the vacillations of its lord, who first supported the blacks, then turned his cloak for the greens to preserve his head, and was executed by Queen Rhaenyra when she took King's Landing nonetheless. At the time of _A Game of Thrones_, House Rosby is headed by Lord Gyles Rosby, a sickly man known across the court for a rattling cough that has dogged him for years. Lord Gyles attends the Red Keep regularly, a minor but familiar face among the courtiers of King's Landing, and later serves Queen Regent Cersei as lord treasurer.
