House Sawyer is a noble house of Westeros whose region, arms, and seat have gone unrecorded.

The only Sawyer known to the chronicles is Ser Harry Sawyer, who wagered beneath the walls of Highgarden over which knight would be first to claim Brienne of Tarth's maidenhead. When the bet came to light, Brienne sought him out and defeated him in the melee at Bitterbridge; he survived to fight again at the Battle of the Blackwater.
