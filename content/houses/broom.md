House Broom is an ancient line of the westerlands, its roots reaching back to the golden age of the First Men, and one of the principal houses sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock. Its knights ride to war beneath a silver helm crested with a sprig of broom, worn on a field checked black and green.

In the present age the house is represented at Casterly Rock itself by Ser Benedict Broom, its master-at-arms, who drills the garrison of the Lannister seat. Maesters have long debated whether House Broom shares any blood with the similarly named House Broome, a question the historical record has never settled.
