House Harlaw of Harridan Hill is one of several cadet branches sprung from the main line of House Harlaw of Ten Towers, alongside the Harlaws of Grey Garden, Harlaw Hall, and the Tower of Glimmering. Like the others, it holds no lordship in its own right, but answers Ten Towers as a sworn house of the ruling branch on the isle of Harlaw.

At the time of the War of the Five Kings, Harridan Hill is held by Boremund Harlaw, called Boremund the Blue, a cousin of Lord Rodrik Harlaw the Reader, with three sons of his own to carry the line forward.
