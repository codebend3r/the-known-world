House Frey of the Crossing is the youngest of the great Riverlands houses, and the wealthiest after the Tullys themselves. Six hundred years ago they were petty knights with a stretch of bog beside the Green Fork; today they hold the only bridge across that river for many leagues, and a Frey toll has been paid by every army, merchant, and lord who would cross between the Trident and the North.

Lord Walder Frey is past ninety by the time of *A Game of Thrones*, on his eighth wife, with so many sons and grandsons and great-grandsons that the maester at the Twins keeps a written list to remember them by. He is reputed to bear an old grudge — many old grudges — and a long memory; his liege lord Lord Hoster Tully has been heard, in private, to call him the Late Lord Frey, for arriving on the field at the Trident only after the battle was won.
