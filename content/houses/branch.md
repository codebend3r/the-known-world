House Branch is one of the wolfswood clans of the north, a First Men line so old that no maester has set down its founding, its arms, or its words. Like its neighbors among the clan-houses, it holds no true castle, its people scattered through the wolfswood and bound not to Winterfell direct but to House Glover of Deepwood Motte.

The house's sole appearance in the chronicles of the realm comes during the War of the Five Kings: Lady Sybelle Glover, holding Deepwood Motte in her husband's absence, sends men of House Branch, among them one Benjicot Branch, to serve as scouts for Stannis Baratheon's march on Winterfell through the wolfswood. Asha Greyjoy, a captive in Stannis's train, thinks of the Branches as little more than a clan under arms, a fair measure of how the great houses of the north regard their wolfswood kin.
