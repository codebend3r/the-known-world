House Goodbrother of Orkmont bears the name of the great and scattered Goodbrother line of the Hammerhorn, holding the island of Orkmont rather than a seat on Great Wyk itself. Whether it truly descends from the Hammerhorn branch or merely shares its name is a question the maesters leave open. Sworn, as all Goodbrothers are, to House Greyjoy of Pyke, no lord of Orkmont has yet been named in any surviving chronicle.
