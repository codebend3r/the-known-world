House Holt is a minor house of the north that once held the Wolf's Den, though the castle has long since passed from their hands. Little else of their history is recorded, and by the time of the War of the Five Kings the house answers House Bolton rather than Winterfell directly.

Ser Maynard Holt serves the Night's Watch as captain of the ship Talon in the years of the War of the Five Kings.
