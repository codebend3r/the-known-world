House Cressey is a lordly house of the crownlands, sworn to House Baratheon of King's Landing, blazoning seven silver coins upon a red bend sinister between two silver helmets on blue.

During the regency of King Aegon III Targaryen, a Lord Cressey took part in Lord Manfryd Mooton's diplomatic mission to Braavos in 133 AC, where he and Lord Follard were tasked with spreading bribes among the city's keyholders, magisters, and other notables while Manfryd treated directly with the Sealord.
