House Oldflowers is a reach house sworn to House Tyrell, their name and their sigil, ten white hands debruised by a bastard's bend sinister, hinting at descent from some bastard son of House Gardener in the days when the Gardener kings still ruled the Reach from Highgarden. Ser Garth Oldflowers won a name for himself jousting at the Tourney at Ashford Meadow during the reign of Daeron II Targaryen. Their words are not known, and no Oldflowers has appeared among the living since.
