House Templeton of Ninestars is one of the oldest and most storied knightly houses of the Vale of Arryn. Their line traces to the Andal invasion, when Ser Luceon Templeton, the Knight of Ninestars, fought beneath the banner of Ser Artys Arryn at the Battle of the Seven Stars. When Artys offered to raise the Templetons to a proper lordly title after the victory, Luceon refused, declaring that knighthood was his true calling. That proud refusal has defined the house ever since: for all the generations since, the head of House Templeton has borne the style of Knight of Ninestars rather than lord, yet in power and in arms their line rivals many a full lordship.

Despite their knightly rank, the Templetons are counted among the great houses of the Vale in all but name. They are said to be able to raise a thousand men from their seat at Ninestars, and their counsel carries weight in the high halls of the Eyrie. At the Battle of the Redgrass Field in 196 AC, the Knight of Ninestars rode in the loyalist host commanded by Lord Donnel Arryn against the first Blackfyre Rebellion, and was slain by Daemon Blackfyre himself. In the years of _A Game of Thrones_, Ser Symond Templeton holds the title. Cold-eyed and sharp-bearded, he stands among the Lords Declarant who challenge Petyr Baelish's regency over the boy lord Robert Arryn, lending the weight of Ninestars to the Vale's demand for a reckoning with the Mockingbird's ambitions.
