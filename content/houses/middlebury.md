House Middlebury is a minor house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. No maester's chronicle records their seat, their words, or any deed of their line; only their quartered arms, a black saltire on yellow, green, and white lozenges, survive in the heraldic record. No Middlebury has yet appeared among the living in the great struggles of the realm.
