House Flint of the mountains, sometimes called the Flints of the hills, is one of the mountain clans of the North, dwelling in the high country north of the wolfswood. They name themselves the First Flints, and they hold that theirs is the eldest branch of the line, and that the Flints of Widow's Watch to the southeast and the Flints of Flint's Finger to the southwest were both founded by younger sons who left the mountains to find food, land, and wives. Some maesters wonder whether they are one and the same as the vanished Flints of Breakstone Hill, who reigned as First Men kings in the age after the Long Night until the Kings of Winter brought them to heel. Neither their arms nor their words are set down in any book, for the clans keep their own memory rather than any herald's roll.

Their chief is styled simply "The Flint," though at Winterfell he is given the courtesy of "Lord Flint." The clan's blood runs in the Kings of Winter themselves: Arya Flint of the mountains wed Rodrik Stark, and their daughter Lyarra became the wife of Lord Rickard Stark and the mother of Eddard Stark, so that the lords of Winterfell carry the mountain Flints in their veins.

In the days of _A Game of Thrones_ the First Flints are led by Lord Torghen Flint, a grey and grizzled chief better known as Old Flint. When Stannis Baratheon comes north to break the Boltons and marches his host through the mountains toward Winterfell, the Old Flint answers the call as his forebears answered the Starks, bringing his hardy clansmen down out of the high snows to fight beneath the stag and flaming heart.
