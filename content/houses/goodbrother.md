House Goodbrother of the Hammerhorn is among the largest and most ancient of the houses of the Iron Islands, sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke and seated upon Great Wyk, the broadest of the isles. Their stronghold, the Hammerhorn, rises from the bleak hills of the island's eastern reach, a grim seat of black stone amidst sheepfolds and salt grass, and from it the Goodbrothers have warded the windward coast since the dawn of years. So numerous are their branches that the maesters reckon them as scattered as gulls upon a cliff, and beyond the chief line at the Hammerhorn the name is borne by the Goodbrothers of Shatterstone, of Crow Spike Keep, and of Corpse Lake — kin enough that an ironborn lord might count a Goodbrother in every fleet that ever put out from the western shore.

In the days following the death of Balon Greyjoy, when the priest Aeron Damphair called the captains and kings to choose a successor in the old way, Lord Gorold Goodbrother rode from the Hammerhorn to the holy strand of Old Wyk to take his part in the kingsmoot. There, beneath the ribs of Nagga and upon the Grey King's seat, the assembled lords and reavers raised up Euron Crow's Eye in despite of Lady Asha and her uncle Victarion, and the storied independence of the ironborn passed once more beneath a single crown. Through reaver-king and Lord Reaper alike the Goodbrothers have kept their stony hold and their old name, a kindred as enduring as the grey rock of the islands themselves.
