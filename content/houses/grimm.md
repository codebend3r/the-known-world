House Grimm of Greyshield has long ruled its island of the Shield Islands from the castle of Grimston, one of the Reach's first lines of defense against raiders out of the Iron Islands. During the Dance of the Dragons the Grimms of that day declared for Rhaenyra's blacks, though they surrendered to Prince Daeron Targaryen and Lord Ormund Hightower rather than face the dragon Tessarion; in more peaceful years a Grimm daughter was named among the maidens proposed as a bride for King Aegon III, and Greyshield sent galleys to serve under Alyn Velaryon, the Oakenfist, against Lord Dalton Greyjoy.

That old rivalry came full circle during the War of the Five Kings, when Euron Greyjoy's ironborn descended on the Shield Islands. Lord Guthor Grimm's own septon judged that the gods had spoken after Ser Harras Harlaw bested seven of Grimston's defenders, and surrendered the castle. Euron gave Greyshield to Harras as his reward, and Lord Guthor remains a captive in his own keep, his house's rule over the island deposed in all but name.
