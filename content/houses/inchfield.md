House Inchfield is a minor noble house of the Reach, holding lands in the country around the Chequy Water and sworn to the lords of Coldmoat. Their arms, a bend sinister chequy black and white on a pale grey field, are modest bearings for a house that has served its neighbors quietly across many generations, producing knights rather than lords. The Inchfields number among that broad class of lesser Reach houses whose names pass without remark through the great chronicles but whose swords and castellans keep the machinery of greater lordships running.

The most notable son of the house in memory was Ser Lucas Inchfield, called Lucas Longinch for his remarkable height, who served as castellan of Coldmoat under Lord Wyman Webber and continued in that office for Wyman's daughter, the Lady Rohanne. Ser Lucas was a formidable knight -- tall, ugly, and purposeful -- who guarded his mistress's interests with the tenacity of a man who hoped to make himself her husband. He met his end in trial by combat at the Chequy Water, defeated and drowned by the hedge knight Ser Duncan the Tall during the dispute with House Osgrey of Standfast recorded in _The Sworn Sword_. A later Inchfield, Ser Owen, rode with King Renly Baratheon's host at the opening of the War of the Five Kings.
