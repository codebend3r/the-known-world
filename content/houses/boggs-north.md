House Boggs of the Neck is a crannogman house of the north, unrelated to the like-named house of Crackclaw Point in the crownlands. Like their fellow bog-dwellers, they are sworn to House Reed of Greywater Watch rather than to Winterfell directly, and keep no known arms or words.

No Boggs of the Neck is recorded to have appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings, their doings as hidden as the causeways of the Neck itself.
