House Weaver is a young house of the Iron Islands, low in prestige and unrecorded in arms or words, sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke.

Members of the house are seen at the kingsmoot called on Old Wyk after Balon Greyjoy's death, where Romny Weaver stands among Victarion Greyjoy's supporters and lifts his voice in the old reaving songs.
