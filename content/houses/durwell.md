House Durwell is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Their lands lie alongside those of House Webber, though no record sets down their arms, their words, or their seat.

During the reign of Aerys I Targaryen, the Durwells earned a reputation as renowned livestock thieves. No member of the house has appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings or after.
