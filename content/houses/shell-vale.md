House Shell was a First Men house that held the Fingers, that ragged claw of peninsulas reaching into Sisterton's grey waters, before the Andals ever crossed the narrow sea. Their last king, Dywen Shell, ruled the Fingers as any petty king of that broken country: warily, and at war with his neighbors.

Dywen made the mistake common to so many First Men kings when the Andals came: he hired an Andal warlord's swords to settle an old feud, only to have those same swords turned against him. He was burned alive inside his own wooden longhall, and the Andal knight Corwyn Corbray, whose line would inherit the Fingers, took Dywen's widow to warm his bed. House Shell did not survive the telling of the tale.
