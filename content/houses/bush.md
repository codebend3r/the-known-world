House Bush is a noble house of unrecorded region, its seat, words, and sigil all lost to time. It is not known whether the house shares any blood with the similarly named House Bushy of the Reach.

Its one notable son was Ser Owen Bush, who served in King Maegor I Targaryen's Kingsguard. Rumor held that he had been the knight ordered to cut out Queen Ceryse Hightower's tongue, though most maesters since have judged the tale slander spread by Maegor's enemies. Ser Owen met a scandalous end in 48 AC, found dead outside a brothel, the manner of his mutilation suggesting a jealous rival or a wronged husband rather than any royal command. No Bush is known to have appeared in the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
