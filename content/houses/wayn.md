House Wayn is a Lordly house of the riverlands, its arms four wheels counterchanged upon a quartered field of blue and white. Whether the Wayns share any blood with House Waynwood of the Vale has never been settled by the maesters. During the Faith Militant uprising, Lord Tristifer Wayn took up arms against the Iron Throne and fought at the Great Fork; a century later, Visenya Targaryen and Vhagar burned Wayn's seat in a single night alongside those of Blanetree, Deddings, Lychester, and Terrick.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings the house has no lord of note, but Utherydes Wayn, a man of an age with Hoster Tully, serves faithfully as steward of Riverrun. The seat itself now answers to House Baelish's new lordship of the Trident rather than to Riverrun directly.
