House Grey is a riverlands house, likely no more than landed knights, now counted among the bannermen of Petyr Baelish as Lord Paramount of the Trident, having answered to Riverrun before him. Their arms and words have not survived in any account.

Their one recorded deed of note belongs to Ser Garibald Grey, who led rivermen fighting for Rhaenyra Targaryen's blacks during the Dance of the Dragons, through the Battle by the Lakeshore and the Butcher's Ball, before dying at the First Battle of Tumbleton, burned by the dragon Tessarion.
