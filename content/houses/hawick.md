House Hawick of Saltpans is a noble house of the riverlands, holding the port town of Saltpans on the northern shore of the Bay of Crabs. Neither their arms nor any deed of theirs are recorded in the great chronicles, and little beyond their name survives of their history.

The house was sworn to House Tully of Riverrun at the time of the War of the Five Kings, and later to Petyr Baelish as Lord Paramount of the Trident. Bellena Hawick, wed to Ser Hosteen Frey, is the only Hawick of note in the years the War of the Five Kings is fought.
