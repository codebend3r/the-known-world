House Strickland lost its lands and its place in Westeros in the wake of the First Blackfyre Rebellion, when its lord rose with Daemon I Blackfyre and was crushed at the Battle of the Redgrass Field. He fled across the narrow sea with Bittersteel and the other defeated rebels, and when the Golden Company was founded in 212 AC, House Strickland's exiled line joined it from the very beginning.

Four generations of Stricklands have since served the Golden Company, culminating in Ser Harry Strickland, who rises from paymaster to captain-general and leads the company's landing in Westeros in support of the pretender Aegon Targaryen's claim to the Iron Throne.
