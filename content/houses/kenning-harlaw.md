The Kennings of Harlaw were once bitter rivals of House Harlaw itself, warring for mastery of their shared island until Harlaw broke them and made them vassals. From this defeated line sprang Herrock Kenning, an ironborn warrior who left Harlaw to carve out the cadet House Kenning of Kayce in the westerlands.

Ralf Kenning serves Victarion Greyjoy's fleet during the War of the Five Kings and urges him to stand for the Seastone Chair at the kingsmoot; afterward he holds Moat Cailin as castellan under the Iron Throne's men, until a crannogman's poisoned arrow leaves him near death and Theon Greyjoy grants him a mercy killing.
