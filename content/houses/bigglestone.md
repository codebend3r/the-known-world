House Bigglestone is a noble house of the riverlands, sworn to House Tully of Riverrun. During the Dance of the Dragons the Bigglestones declared for the blacks, and their lord was slain fighting at the Battle by the Lakeshore in 130 AC.

No Bigglestone is recorded to have appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings, and the house's seat, arms, and current head go unrecorded by the maesters.
