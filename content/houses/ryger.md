House Ryger of Willow Wood is one of the principal houses sworn to House Tully of Riverrun, blazoning a weeping willow, green on white. During Robert's Rebellion the Rygers broke with their liege lord Hoster Tully and remained loyal to King Aerys II Targaryen, a rare act of defiance among the river lords of that war.

By the War of the Five Kings, Ser Robin Ryger served as captain of the guard at Riverrun, while Tristan Ryger, of uncertain kinship to the main branch, was counted among Lord Edmure Tully's own friends and companions. Even after renewing their fealty to the Iron Throne, House Ryger's banners were conspicuously absent from the rivermen mustered to besiege Riverrun, a quiet dissent Ser Jaime Lannister took note of. Once the castle surrendered, Robin Ryger set off with Ser Desmond Grell to take the black at the Wall, and by the time Petyr Baelish rules the Trident as its Lord Paramount, House Ryger answers to him rather than to Riverrun directly.
