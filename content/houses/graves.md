House Graves is a minor house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden, known chiefly by its striking blazon of a counter-charged double-headed eagle on green-and-white gyronny. Maesters of the Citadel have long puzzled over whether the Graves share blood with the similarly obscure House Groves, though nothing in the surviving record settles the question either way.

No Graves has been recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings, and the house's words, seat, and history remain otherwise unknown.
