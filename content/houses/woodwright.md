House Woodwright is a minor house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden, blazoning a red pall on a field of oak. No maester has set down their words, nor is their founding remembered. The earliest Woodwright of record, Ser Denys, served as a sworn sword to Lord Corlys Velaryon, the Sea Snake, in the early days of Aegon's dynasty.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings the house is represented at Highgarden by the brothers Ser Portifer and Ser Lucantine Woodwright, household knights who pay court to Margaery Tyrell alongside the other young lordlings and hedge knights hoping to catch her eye.
