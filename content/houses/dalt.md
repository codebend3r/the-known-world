House Dalt of Lemonwood is a house of landed knights seated at Lemonwood, south of the mouth of the Greenblood and close to the shadow city of Sunspear. Their origins are ancient and somewhat obscure: they may descend from the First Men who crossed the Arm of Dorne before it was broken, settling along the banks of the Greenblood where they dug canals and planted the lemon groves that still bear fruit today, or they may be Andals who carved a place for themselves by the sword in a later age. In the elder days a loose confederacy of Greenblood families raised up a High King of Dorne by election, not unlike the driftwood kings of the Iron Islands; when that kingdom fell to civil war and shattered into petty lordships, the Dalts were among the houses that endured.

When Nymeria and her ten thousand ships landed on Dornish shores and House Martell began their long conquest, the Dalts were among the earliest banners to swear allegiance. That early fidelity placed them in good standing with Sunspear for generations thereafter. They are a house of modest power: the Knight of Lemonwood commands respect enough among lesser men but holds little sway over princes. At the time of _A Game of Thrones_, the seat is held by Ser Deziel Dalt, whose younger brother Ser Andrey is a childhood companion of Princess Arianne Martell.
