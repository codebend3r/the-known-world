House Graceford of Holyhall is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to Highgarden and long distinguished by an uncommon devotion to the Mother, the face of the Seven who governs mercy, fertility, and the coming of new life. Their seat at Holyhall houses one of the largest septs outside of Oldtown dedicated near-exclusively to the Mother's aspect, and the pale wavy stripe on their arms is said to recall both the river meadows of their lands and the silver veil of the Crone's lantern, though it is the Mother's visage the house places at the center of its shield. Their ancient words, Work Her Will, are taken by some septons as a rare example of a secular house subordinating its identity entirely to a divine charge.

At the opening of _A Game of Thrones_ the house is held by Lady Alyce Graceford, who attends the court of the great lords as part of the Tyrell following. She is with child, and the household at Holyhall carries on in the pious, quietly prosperous manner that has characterized the Gracefords for generations; they are not a house that wins renown on the battlefield or in the tournament, but one that tends its sept, honors its liege, and endures.
