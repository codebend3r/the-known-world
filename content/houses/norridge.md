House Norridge is a reach house sworn to House Tyrell, blazoning a flight of flaming arrows rising bendwise on blue. Ser Jeremy Norridge served his squirehood at Highgarden alongside Prince Daeron Targaryen, and the two friends died together in 251 AC fighting the rebellion of the Rat, the Hawk, and the Pig. During the War of the Five Kings, men wearing Norridge or Sarsfield arrow-badges are among the broken men found hanged for the raid on Saltpans.
