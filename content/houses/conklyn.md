House Conklyn is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden, though some maesters believe it may in truth be a knightly house owing its fealty to House Rowan instead. Its lands border those of House Webber. Neither its arms nor its words have been recorded.

Ser Clifford Conklyn once pressed an old family claim to Leafy Lake, though nothing is recorded of how, or whether, that claim was ever settled. No Conklyn has appeared among the notable figures of the realm during the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
