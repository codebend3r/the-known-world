House Appleton of Appleton is a noble house of the Reach, holding its namesake seat in fealty to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Their quartered arms pair an apple tree with a gatehouse, and though the house's words are not recorded, its holdfast has stood long enough to give its name to both the family and their lands.

No Appleton is known to have played a notable part in the great wars of the Seven Kingdoms, and none has yet appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings; the house keeps to its orchards and its gate.
