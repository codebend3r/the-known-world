House Peckledon serves House Lannister of Casterly Rock, its arms ten purple mullets ranked on gold beneath the words "Unflinching." Arya Stark marks their colors among Lord Tywin's host marching out of Harrenhal, one of the few times the house's banner is noted at all.

The name is carried into the war chiefly by Josmyn Peckledon, who at the Battle of the Blackwater kills two knights, wounds a third, and captures two more, earning acclaim as one of the battle's heroes along with a new horse, armor, and the promise of knighthood. He testifies at Tyrion Lannister's trial to having seen him empty the poisoned cup, and afterward squires for Ser Jaime Lannister on the ride to Riverrun, where he becomes smitten with Pia at Harrenhal.
