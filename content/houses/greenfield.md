House Greenfield of Greenfield is a house of landed knights sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock, its seat so old the Greenfields themselves trace it to the days of the First Men. Their castle, once called the Bower, is said to have been raised entirely of weirwood, marking it as a relic of the Dawn Age, built before the Pact ended the wars between the First Men and the children of the forest. Their arms bear a white double tressure on green; no words have ever been recorded for the house.

Ser Preston Greenfield served in the Kingsguard of King Robert I Baratheon, a duty he discharged with a harshness Sansa Stark remembered bitterly, until he died at the High Septon's side during the bread riots of King's Landing, hacked apart by the mob so thoroughly his white armor was found caked entirely in his own blood. Ser Garth Greenfield, the Knight of Greenfield at the time of the War of the Five Kings, was taken captive in the Whispering Wood and spent the remainder of the war a prisoner at Raventree Hall.
