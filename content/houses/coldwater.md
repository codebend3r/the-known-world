House Coldwater of Coldwater Burn is an ancient noble house of the First Men seated at Coldwater Burn, a castle near the mouth of a river west of the Fingers in the Vale of Arryn. Among the oldest bloodlines in the Vale, the Coldwaters descend from the petty kings who ruled the region before the Andal invasion. When Robar II Royce sought to unite the Vale's First Men lords against the advancing Andals, the Coldwaters were among those who bent the knee before him, proclaiming him High King of the Vale, the Fingers, and the Mountains of the Moon. They fought beneath his banners at the Battle of the Seven Stars, where Robar's host was defeated and the old order of the Vale was broken.

Following the Andal conquest, the Coldwaters submitted to Artys I Arryn, the first King of Mountain and Vale, and retained their seat under the new dispensation. In the centuries since, they have been sworn to House Royce of Runestone, a bond that echoes the ancient alliance forged before the battle that ended their independence. At the time of _A Game of Thrones_, House Coldwater is led by Lord Royce Coldwater, who holds the castle and the lordship of Coldwater Burn.
