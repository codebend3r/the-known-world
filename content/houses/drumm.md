Some Drumms were rock kings of Old Wyk in ancient days, ruling the island the ironborn hold holiest and the first the First Men settled upon reaching the isles. The rock king Regnar Drumm, called Raven-feeder, was chosen High King of the Iron Islands by kingsmoot after Erich I Greyiron renounced his crown, and in time the Drumms joined with the Greyjoys, Hoares, and Orkwoods, and the Andals besides, to bring down the hereditary line of House Greyiron altogether.

The house counts many boasted names among its dead: Dale the Dread and Roryn the Reaver, raiders of old renown; Dagon Drumm, said to have practiced necromancy; Gormond the Oldfather, claimed to have fathered a hundred sons; and Hilmar the Cunning, who is said to have won the Valyrian steel sword Red Rain from a green-lands knight with nothing but his wits and a wooden cudgel. In 134 AC a Lord Drumm of Old Wyk was put to the sword by westermen during Lady Johanna Lannister's invasion of the Iron Islands.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings, Old Wyk is held by Lord Dunstan Drumm, called the Bone Hand, who wields Red Rain and captains the ship Thunderer. He brings Old Wyk behind Balon Greyjoy's rebellion, and after Balon's death puts himself forward at the kingsmoot to claim the Seastone Chair, with his sons Denys and Donnel and his household captain Andrik the Unsmiling standing as his champions. His long telling of the Drumms' past glories, and chests found to hold only bronze, cost him the crowd's favor, and Euron Greyjoy is chosen king in his place.
