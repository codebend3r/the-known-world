House Fell has held Felwood in the northern stormlands since before Aegon's Conquest, sworn to Storm's End. When Orys Baratheon crossed the Wendwater at the head of the Targaryen host, Lords Fell, Errol, and Buckler ambushed his column and fled into the kingswood, only for Rhaenys Targaryen to set the forest ablaze atop Meraxes and burn them out. A later Lord Fell died less gloriously, smothered by Dornish assassins in a brothel during the First Dornish War.

The house paid again in the Dance of the Dragons, when Lady Fell was put to death for refusing to renounce her oaths to Rhaenyra Targaryen, even as Ser Willis Fell served King Viserys I's Kingsguard, carried Princess Jaehaera to safety at Storm's End after King's Landing fell, and rose to Lord Commander of the Kingsguard under Aegon III before dying of Winter Fever. Generations later, at the outbreak of Robert's Rebellion, Lord Fell joined Lords Grandison and Cafferen in marching against Robert Baratheon, only to be defeated in three battles at Summerhall and killed in single combat; his son, called Silveraxe, was taken captive and afterward turned to Robert's cause.

At the time of the War of the Five Kings, Felwood is held by Lord Harwood Fell, who marches north with Stannis Baratheon's host and dies of a chill after falling through river ice on the road to Winterfell.
