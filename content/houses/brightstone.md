House Brightstone was a First Men house that ruled the Fingers, the narrow peninsulas of the eastern Vale, long before the Andals came to Westeros. No record of its arms or words survives, only the name of its last king.

King Jon Brightstone, hoping to be rid of his enemies, hired an Andal warlord and his sellswords to fight at his side. The Andal turned on him instead: within the year Jon Brightstone had been captured, tortured, and beheaded, and House Brightstone died with him. The Andal knight who led that betrayal, Corwyn Corbray, took Jon's daughter to wife and founded House Corbray in the dead king's stead, a lineage that endures in the Vale to this day.
