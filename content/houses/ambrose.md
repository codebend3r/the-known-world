House Ambrose is a noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. They blazon their arms with red ants strewn upon a field of yellow, and their motto, "Never Resting," speaks to the industry the ants are meant to signify. A knight of the house, Ser Aegon Ambrose, died fighting for King Maegor I Targaryen in the trial of seven against the Warrior's Sons, and in later years Marq Ambrose schemed among the Caltrops for King Aegon II during the Dance of the Dragons, while Ser Aubrey Ambrose lent his sword to Daemon Blackfyre's doomed cause.

Lord Arthur Ambrose leads the house at the time of the War of the Five Kings, following his Tyrell overlords into Renly Baratheon's camp and then, after Renly's death, back into the Lannister-Tyrell alliance. His squire son Alyn, betrothed to Elinor Tyrell, fights at the Blackwater, while his kinsman Ser Edmund Ambrose is bested by Brienne of Tarth in the melee at Bitterbridge after making a wager on her maidenhood.

The ants of House Ambrose are seen marching with Randyll Tarly's host to pacify the crownlands after the Blackwater, and Brienne later spots Ambrose horse-trappings among the dead at Duskendale, a reminder of how far the house's men have ridden in service of the Tyrells.
