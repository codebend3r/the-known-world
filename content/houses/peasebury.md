House Peasebury holds Poddingfield in the stormlands, a lordly house sworn to Storm's End, its arms a burst pea pod set among rows of peas on white. No chronicle marks when the pod first bloomed on a Peasebury shield, only that the house has kept its small seat through the long centuries of Baratheon and Durrandon rule alike.

Lord Robin Peasebury declares for Renly Baratheon and, after Renly's death, remains one of the few storm lords still loyal to Stannis following the Battle of the Blackwater. He follows Stannis north into the winter campaign, where four of his soldiers are burned by the queen's men for cannibalism at a crofters' village, a horror Robin himself is rumored to have shared in.
