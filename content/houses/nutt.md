House Nutt is a riverlands house of no recorded arms or words, sworn first to House Tully and, since Petyr Baelish's elevation as Lord Paramount of the Trident, to House Baelish. In an age before the Targaryen Conquest, when the Storm Kings of House Durrandon pressed their occupation of the riverlands, Lady Jeyne Nutt rose against them and, for a brief season, styled herself Queen of the Trident.
