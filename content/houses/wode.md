House Wode is a house of landed knights sworn to whoever holds Harrenhal, their lands lying near the crownlands border close by Sow's Horn. Their arms show three white hedgehogs on yellow, and their words, "Touch Me Not," suit a house so armed. During the Dance of the Dragons, Ser Oswald Wode rode with the blacks and fell to Ser Criston Cole's vanguard along the Gods Eye in 130 AC, on the march toward Harrenhal.

In the early days of the War of the Five Kings, Ser Willis Wode aids Catelyn Stark and Rodrik Cassel after her seizure of Tyrion Lannister, fighting off mountain clansmen on the road through the Mountains of the Moon before parting ways with her at the Bloody Gate. When the Iron Throne later grants Harrenhal to Petyr Baelish, House Wode passes into his lordship along with the rest of Harrenhal's vassals. By the time Jaime Lannister marches to lift the siege of Riverrun, the Wode brothers' earth-and-timber halls stand burned to the ground and abandoned, their fate unknown.
