House Forrester is one of the clan names of the wolfswood, its trackers and hunters sworn to House Glover of Deepwood Motte alongside kin-clans like Woods, Branch, and Bole. Their arms and words have never been set down, and Asha Greyjoy reckons the Forresters more clan than proper lordly house.

When Stannis Baratheon marches through the wolfswood toward Winterfell, Lady Sybelle Glover lends him guides drawn from these clans, Forresters among them, to speed his army's passage. No Forrester by name is otherwise recorded in the histories of the Seven Kingdoms; a far more storied telling of the family's fortunes belongs to a separate account of events at Ironrath, outside the scope of this chronicle.
