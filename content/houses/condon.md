House Condon is a minor northern house sworn to House Cerwyn of Castle Cerwyn, who in turn hold their seat in the shadow of Winterfell. Like many of the smaller houses clustered along the kingsroad south of the great castle, the Condons have endured the long winters of the North by keeping close to a stronger neighbour rather than by carving out wide lands of their own. Their arms — an eagle's head set between crossed tridents, red on white — bespeak a house old enough to have its own heraldic identity, if modest enough never to have pressed a claim above its station.

At the opening of _A Game of Thrones_, Ser Kyle Condon stands as Lord Medger Cerwyn's right hand. When Robb Stark calls his banners and the northern lords march south to war, Kyle Condon rides with the Cerwyn column. He is also noted in some accounts among the knights attached to Ser Wylis Manderly's following, suggesting a household accustomed to serving where the North's greater lords have need of a steady sword.
