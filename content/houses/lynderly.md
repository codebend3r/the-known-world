House Lynderly of the Snakewood is a noble house of the Vale, sworn to the Arryns of the Eyrie. Their seat takes its name from the dense forest at its heart, and it was there that the house's founding legend was made. During the Andal Invasion, when most of the incoming knights drove the First Men from their lands by fire and sword, the Lynderly founder chose a different course. He walked alone and unarmed into the Snakewood to parley with the First Men chieftains within. After a day and a night of counsel, a pact was sworn. The two peoples lived in cautious proximity, and over generations the old forest clans were folded into the Lynderly household entirely. The house blazons that treaty in their arms: green serpents wriggling across a black field, a heraldry as tangled and alive as the wood they govern.

At the time of _A Game of Thrones_, Lord Jon Lynderly rules from the Snakewood and has positioned himself among the Vale lords most amenable to Petyr Baelish, the newly installed Lord Protector. When Littlefinger requests that lords of the Vale send sons to ward at the Eyrie and serve as squires to young Lord Robert Arryn, Jon obliges and dispatches his heir, Terrance, to the castle. Another Lynderly, the knight Lymond, moves in the tourney circuits of the Vale and takes part in the contest to fill the Brotherhood of Winged Knights.
