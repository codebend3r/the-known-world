House Crayne is a noble house of the Vale of Arryn, sworn to House Arryn of the Eyrie. Neither its arms nor its words have been recorded.

Ser Symond Crayne served in the Kingsguard of King Maegor I Targaryen, but upon Jaehaerys I's ascension to the Iron Throne he was sentenced to take the black and ended his days as a brother of the Night's Watch. Generations later, when the death of Lady Jeyne Arryn plunged the Vale into a war of succession, House Crayne threw its support behind her chosen heir, Ser Joffrey Arryn.
