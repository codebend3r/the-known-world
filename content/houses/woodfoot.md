House Woodfoot of Bear Island once ruled that isle in the Bay of Ice, sworn to Winterfell, in the ages before House Mormont held it. Some maesters number a Woodfoot among the many candidates said to have been the legendary Night's King, though the truth of that old tale, if it has any, is lost.

The house was wiped out entirely when ironmen invaded Bear Island, and no Woodfoot survived to keep the family's name, arms, or words for later chroniclers to record.
