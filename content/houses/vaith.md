House Vaith of the Red Dunes descends from Andal adventurers who settled the hills east of Dorne's deep sands, giving their name to both castle and river. When Queen Rhaenys Targaryen flew Meraxes to Vaith during Aegon's Conquest, she found the castle abandoned, and during the First Dornish War an entire army under Lord Harlan Tyrell vanished into the sands while marching to take it. A later Lord Vaith was murdered for the ransom on his head amid the bloody reprisals of the Dragon's Wroth, before his successor finally bent the knee to Daeron I Targaryen at the Submission of Sunspear.

Lady Cassella Vaith rose to become the third mistress of King Aegon IV, and generations later an aged, reputedly mad Lady Vaith was visited by the hedge knight Ser Duncan the Tall and his squire Egg, who by his own account might have shown her more courtesy than he did. Their arms, three black leopards on a yellow pile over orange, appear nowhere in the novels themselves but survive in semi-canon heraldic record.

Lord Daeron Vaith holds the Red Dunes during the War of the Five Kings; Vaith lies along the route Arianne Martell plots to smuggle Myrcella Baratheon across the Dornish desert toward the Hellholt and Sandstone, a plan cut short when Areo Hotah intercepts them on the Greenblood.
