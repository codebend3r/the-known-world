House Rankenfell's home region is unrecorded, its arms and words lost with it. The name is remembered at the Wall alone, and not kindly.

Marq Rankenfell, called Mad Marq, once held the Lord Commander's seat at Castle Black, and is counted among the very worst commanders the Night's Watch has ever suffered to lead it.
