House Wythers is a minor house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell, bearing a grey squirrel on white within a red border. Their words and founding are unrecorded.

Ser Ottyn Wythers serves as a commander of the Night's Watch and rides beyond the Wall on the great ranging in search of Benjen Stark and the missing rangers, dying at the Fist of the First Men when a panicked horse kicks him in the face. In King's Landing, Ser Willam Wythers serves as captain of the guard to Queen Margaery Tyrell.
