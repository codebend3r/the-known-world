House Doggett is a noble house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock. Semi-canon heraldry credits them with a shield divided per bend, a white unicorn upon green and a black raven upon white, though no words for the house have come down in any account.

During the Faith Militant uprising, Ser Joffrey Doggett, called the Red Dog of the Hills, was chosen Grand Captain of the Warrior's Sons and rose to prominence as a rebel commander against King Maegor I Targaryen. Maegor answered by flying to the Doggett seat astride Balerion and putting it to the torch, burning alive Joffrey's father, mother, and young sister along with their sworn swords, serving men, and household.

Joffrey himself survived Maegor's reign and made his peace with Jaehaerys I Targaryen, the king who succeeded his uncle and abolished the Faith Militant. Jaehaerys rewarded him with a place among his Kingsguard. No Doggett is recorded among the players of the War of the Five Kings or the events that follow it.
