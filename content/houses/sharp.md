House Sharp is a noble house of the Iron Islands, sworn to House Greyjoy of Pyke. Neither their arms nor their words have been recorded.

Harrag Sharp, called Sheepstealer, rode with Theon Greyjoy's crew in the taking of Winterfell and was killed during its sack. At the kingsmoot on Old Wyk that followed Balon Greyjoy's death, the family split: Alvyn Sharp threw his support behind Victarion Greyjoy, while Harrag's own father Harmund backed Asha Greyjoy, who confronted him over whether he valued his dead son's life so cheaply that he would trade his other sons for the same price.
