House Stackhouse holds lands in the northern Reach bordering those of House Webber, and may answer to House Rowan of Goldengrove as much as to distant Highgarden. No arms have ever been recorded for the house.

During the reign of King Aerys I Targaryen, Lord Stackhouse coveted the Horseshoe Hills that belonged to neighboring House Webber, a boundary dispute remembered in the region long after, as later recounted in the tale of Ser Duncan the Tall.
