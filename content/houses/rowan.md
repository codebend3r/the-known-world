House Rowan of Goldengrove is one of the principal houses of the northern Reach, holding the rich farmlands along the river Mander where it bends through the heart of the realm's grain country. Like the Florents and the Oakhearts, the Rowans claim descent from Garth Greenhand — their own line through his daughter Rowan Gold-Tree, whose hair is said to have turned to autumn leaves of beaten gold.

Lord Mathis Rowan is reckoned one of the more capable lords of the Reach. He has a friendly rivalry with Randyll Tarly for the title of finest commander in his liege's service, and unlike many a Reach lord he keeps his quarrels in the field rather than the wine cup.
