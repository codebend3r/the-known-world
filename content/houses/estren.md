House Estren of Wyndhall is a noble house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock. The exact location of Wyndhall itself has never been disclosed. Semi-canon heraldry gives them a green saltire between four red double-headed eagles on white, though neither arms nor words appear in any book.

Lord Regenard Estren rides with Ser Jaime Lannister's army in the siege of Riverrun and is taken captive at the Battle of the Whispering Wood. He spends the rest of the War of the Five Kings a prisoner at the Twins, and is released only after the Red Wedding brings Robb Stark's war to its end.
