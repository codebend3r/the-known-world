House Shett of Gull Tower is a knightly house of the Vale of Arryn, holding the modest seat of Gull Tower and sworn through the Bronze Yohn's line of House Royce of Runestone to the falcon kings of the Eyrie. They blazon their arms with three golden wings, set bendwise upon a field checkered in white and black (a charge that recalls, the maesters note, the seabirds that gave their lost city its name). In the present day the house is led by Ser Damon Shett, styled Knight of Gull Tower, who rode out with Lord Yohn Royce and the lords of the Vale when they gathered to wrest the Lord Protectorship from the hands of Petyr Baelish. Of their words, if ever they bore any in their lesser estate, the records of the Citadel make no mention.

Yet the Shetts were not always so diminished, for in the dim years of the Andal coming they were kings, and Gulltown (the Vale's one true city and chiefest port) was theirs to rule. The last of that royal line was King Osgood III Shett, who, beset by the Royces of Runestone, made common cause with the Andal knight Ser Gerold Grafton, sealing the bond with a weaving of marriages between their houses. Together they broke the Royces in battle, but Osgood fell in the fighting, and there are those who whisper that it was Grafton's own hand that struck him down. Returning to the city, Ser Gerold seized the crown, put the king's heir to the sword, and made himself master of Gulltown, drowning the rising of the smallfolk in blood; thus did the Graftons supplant the Shetts, who endured thereafter only as landed knights beneath the new lords their kin had wrought. So passed a kingly house into the ranks of the Vale's lesser men, remembered now chiefly in the wings upon their shield and the salt towers of the place they once commanded.
