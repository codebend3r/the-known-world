House Wydman is a house of the Vale of Arryn, sworn to House Arryn of the Eyrie, whose words, "Right Conquers Might," commemorate the tourney that made the house's name. Its founder, an otherwise unrecorded knight, unhorsed five opponents from the Vale and the westerlands in a single tourney, and the Wydmans have since worn trophies of that triumph on their arms: a chief bearing the devices of Houses Redfort, Lynderly, Waynwood, Brax, and Lannister above five broken lances.

No Wydman of note is recorded by the time of the War of the Five Kings; a knight bearing the house's broken-lance arms is glimpsed among the lords and knights crowding the High Hall of the Eyrie when Tyrion Lannister demands trial by combat, but goes unnamed.
