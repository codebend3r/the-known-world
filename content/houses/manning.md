House Manning is a minor noble house of the crownlands, sworn to the Iron Throne. Their arms, a red sea lion between two black pallets, suggest to those who study such things a house holding some stretch of shore along Blackwater Bay, though no seat is recorded for them.

No Manning has been named among the players of the game of thrones in living memory.
