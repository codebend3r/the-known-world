House Serry of Southshield once ruled the southernmost of the Shield Islands, the Reach's first line of defense in the ages when ironborn raiders tried to sail up the Mander to sack Highgarden's wealth. They bore a white rose upon a red escutcheon within a red embattled border, on white, though their words are not recorded.

When Euron Greyjoy's fleet came for the Shields in 300 AC, Ser Talbert Serry, heir to Southshield, died defending the island in single combat against Victarion Greyjoy. With his son dead and the island lost, Lord Osbert Serry fled to Highgarden rather than kneel, and Euron named his household captain Andrik the Unsmiling lord of Southshield in the Serrys' place. Even landless, the family kept ties to the Reach's first house: Lia Serry, a kinswoman of uncertain closeness, was already wed to Ser Theodore Tyrell, a cousin once removed of Lord Mace Tyrell himself.
