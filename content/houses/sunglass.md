House Sunglass of Sweetport Sound is a noble house of the crownlands, sworn to Dragonstone. Their seat lies on the southern shore of Blackwater Bay, a modest holdfast whose very name speaks to the piety that has long defined the family. The Sunglasses are devotees of the Faith of the Seven, and their three war galleys bear the names Piety, Prayer, and Devotion, fitting emblems for a house whose seven-starred blazon mirrors the sacred symbol of the Seven themselves. The origins of the house are obscure, as befits a minor lordship of the crownlands, though they were well established as bannermen of Dragonstone well before the coming of Robert Baratheon.

Lord Guncer Sunglass, the head of the house at the time of _A Game of Thrones_, is a deeply religious man given to prayer three times daily, moonstones at his throat and wrists marking him as a son of the Faith. When Stannis Baratheon calls his banners against King Joffrey, Guncer answers, and his three galleys are pressed into the royal fleet. Yet after Melisandre burns the sept on Dragonstone and men of House Rambton perish in R'hllor's flames, Lord Guncer can no longer give his loyalty to a king who has abandoned the Seven. He approaches Stannis and renounces his support, and is thrown into the dungeons beneath Dragonstone for his conscience. While Stannis fights at the Blackwater, Queen Selyse Florent orders Guncer and the surviving sons of Hubard Rambton burned alive as offerings to R'hllor. With Guncer dead, his brother inherits the seat but, finding no safety in the crownlands, flees across the narrow sea to Volantis, leaving Sweetport Sound lordless.
