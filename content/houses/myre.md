House Myre of Harlaw was once a bitter foe of House Harlaw for mastery of their shared island, until Harlaw broke them and bound them as vassals. Their grim arms, ten black nooses on white within a border of blood, still mark the longships they send to war.

During the War of the Five Kings, Myre banners gather on Harlaw in support of Asha Greyjoy's claim, though Jon Myre, called Pinchface, instead brings Euron Greyjoy a dozen longships and backs him at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk.
