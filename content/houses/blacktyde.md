House Blacktyde of Blacktyde Castle is an ancient line of the Iron Islands, holding the rocky isle of Blacktyde off the northern reaches of that storm-wracked archipelago and bending the knee to the Greyjoy Lords Reaper of Pyke. Their arms are vairy green and black, a fur older than any kraken's claim, for the Blacktydes were salt kings in the dawn of days and reckon their descent from Joron I Blacktyde, called Maidensbane, a High King of the Iron Islands in the Age of Heroes who is remembered for raiding the Arbor and bearing off every maid beneath the age of thirty — and for the corpse of Gyles Gardener, King of the Reach, which he hewed to pieces and cast upon his fishhooks for bait. From such reaving stock the house has dwindled to a lordly vassal, yet the isle of Blacktyde has bred no shortage of captains who paid the iron price upon the narrow sea, and its lords have long sailed with the great fleets of Pyke when the longships were called to war.

In our own age the house is best known through Lord Baelor Blacktyde, whose father fell in Lord Balon's first rebellion against the Iron Throne. Taken to ward upon the green lands, Baelor spent eight long years a hostage, and when at last he came home to his island he came clean-shaven and clad in velvets, a worshipper of the Seven of the mainland rather than the Drowned God of his fathers — a thing near unheard of among the ironborn, who hold such conversions for the rankest cowardice. At the kingsmoot upon Old Wyk, Lord Baelor gave his voice to Asha Greyjoy and her counsel of peace, having no wish to see more fathers and sons drowned in a war the isles could not win. When Euron Crow's Eye claimed the driftwood crown, Baelor would not bend, declaring that _"Balon was mad, Aeron is madder, and Euron is maddest of them all."_ For that defiance the Crow's Eye had him taken, and his body carved into seven pieces — a black jape upon the seven gods to whom the lord had turned — and so ended one of the few pious men ever to be born of salt and stone.
