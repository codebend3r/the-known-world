House Ryder of the Rills was a noble house that once ruled the Rills in the southwestern reaches of what is now the north. No record of their arms or words survives.

The Ryders were First Men kings in the years after the Long Night, ruling the Rills in their own right until the Kings of Winter of House Stark defeated them and reduced their line to vassals. No Ryder has been recorded in the centuries since, and by the time of the War of the Five Kings House Ryswell rules the Rills in their place, leaving the Ryders presumed extinct.
