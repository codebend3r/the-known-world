House Saltcliffe of Saltcliffe is one of the noble ironborn houses of the isle of Saltcliffe, which they rule and for which they are named. The Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with a nine-headed serpent, black on silver; their words are not recorded.

The Saltcliffes answered Lord Balon Greyjoy's summons to Pyke when he crowned himself King of the Isles and the North at the outset of the War of the Five Kings. After Balon's death, Lord Donnor Saltcliffe stood among the bannermen who backed Euron Greyjoy's claim at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk, and continues to serve among his captains.
