House Hogg of Sow's Horn is a house of landed knights sworn to House Hayford, holding a stout stone keep in the crownlands near the riverlands border, close by the lands of House Wode.

Jon Hogg, called Big Jon, fought and died for the Iron Throne during the Faith Militant uprising against King Maegor I Targaryen. Ser Harys Hogg was a favorite of the smallfolk at tourneys in the early reign of Jaehaerys I. Priscella Hogg, a companion of Queen Daenaera Velaryon, was implicated in a poisoning plot against the royal household; on account of her sex Lord Torrhen Manderly stayed the harshest part of her sentence so long as she gave herself to the Faith and kept her vows.

By the War of the Five Kings, Sow's Horn is held by Ser Roger Hogg, whose walls turn back both Karstark riders and Ser Amory Lorch's Lannister men sent to burn the keep down. Ser Jaime Lannister meets with him there as he passes through the crownlands.
