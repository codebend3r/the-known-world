House Uffering traces its ancestry to Andal adventurers granted lands and wives by the Three Sage Kings of House Gardener during the Andals' coming to the Reach, and remains sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden in the centuries since. Neither their words nor their arms appear in the novels themselves, though the Citadel's heraldic rolls credit them with oak and iron paly beneath a white chief bearing seven green hands.

Ser Rolland Uffering became the fourth husband of the thrice-widowed Lady Rohanne Webber, only to die of the Great Spring Sickness; his sister, the pious and simple Lady Helicent Uffering, is remembered as the last of the line to appear in any chronicle.
