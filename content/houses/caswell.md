House Caswell is a noble house of the reach, seated at Bitterbridge upon the river Mander, where the roseroad crosses the water on its long march from Highgarden to King's Landing. The control of that crossing has made the Caswells a house of greater consequence than their modest size would suggest, for any host moving between the Reach and the crownlands must pass beneath their walls or seek a longer road. Like most of the old families of the Mander, they bent the knee first to the Gardener kings of Highgarden and, after the Field of Fire, passed into the service of House Tyrell, beneath whom they have served as loyal bannermen ever since. Their arms, a golden centaur loosing an arrow upon a white field, are an old blazon of the southern reaches.

The fortunes of the house have turned more than once upon the misfortune of sitting astride so vital a road. During the Dance of the Dragons the Lord of Bitterbridge kept faith with Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen and was brought before the King's Justice and beheaded for refusing to bend the knee to Aegon II; after Prince Daeron the Daring and his dragon began the sack of Bitterbridge, the lord's widow hanged herself from the castle's gatehouse. In the War of the Five Kings the curse of the crossing returned: Lord Lorent Caswell, a wispy young man, followed his liege Mace Tyrell in declaring for King Renly Baratheon, whose vast host of stormlords and reachmen encamped beneath Bitterbridge and held a great tourney before marching east. After Renly's murder and the fall of Storm's End to Stannis, Lord Lorent shut himself up within his walls, and in time the Caswells followed the Tyrells in bending the knee to King Joffrey.
