House Foote of Nightsong is the youngest of noble houses, born out of the War of the Five Kings itself. Ser Philip Foote, a westerlands knight of House Foote, distinguishes himself in the fighting at King's Landing and is rewarded with the stormlands seat of Nightsong after the Battle of the Blackwater, styled Lord of Nightsong and Lord of the Marches.

Raised up in place of the old holders of that castle, the new house still bears the chevron-and-dagger of its westerlands parent, a reminder that its lord's rise came from Lannister service rather than stormlander blood.
