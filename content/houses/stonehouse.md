House Stonehouse of Old Wyk is one of the main houses sworn to Pyke, its lord answering to the old ironborn style of "the Stonehouse." In 134 AC, a Lord Stonehouse was put to the sword by westermen during Lady Johanna Lannister's invasion of the Iron Islands, the same campaign that claimed a Lord Drumm of Old Wyk.

When Dagmer Cleftjaw summons Old Wyk to Balon Greyjoy's banner during the War of the Five Kings, the Stonehouses answer alongside the Drumms. Red Ralf Stonehouse serves as one of Victarion Greyjoy's captains at Moat Cailin and champions him at the kingsmoot on Old Wyk, while the current Stonehouse himself attends clad in ragged sealskins; when Euron Greyjoy is chosen king instead, Red Ralf later leads a squadron of the Iron Fleet toward Slaver's Bay and vanishes off the coast of Sothoryos.
