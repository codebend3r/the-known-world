House Parren rose among the westerlands during the coming of the Andals, its blood a union of First Men and the incoming Andal nobility. A Lord Parren joined Prince Aegon the Uncrowned's rebellion against King Maegor I Targaryen and fought at the Battle Beneath the Gods Eye in 43 AC, one of the few deeds the house left in the annals of the Seven Kingdoms.

Sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock, the Parrens outlived that battle by centuries, their arms noted as late as the tourney at Ashford Meadow in 209 AC, where Ser Duncan the Tall picked out their blazon among the assembled heraldry. Semi-canon sources record the house as extinct by the time of _A Game of Thrones_, its line having died out sometime between that tourney and the War of the Five Kings.
