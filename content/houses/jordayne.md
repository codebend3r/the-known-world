House Jordayne of the Tor is among the older noble houses of Dorne, seated at the Tor on the southern coast of the Sea of Dorne. Before Nymeria and her ten thousand ships landed on Dornish shores, the Jordaynes held a prominence in the region that even drew the early Martells into their orbit. During Nymeria's War they rode among the bannermen of King Yorick V Yronwood, opposing Mors Martell and his Rhoynar queen across nine years of bitter fighting. When the war ended and the last Dornish kings bent the knee, the Jordaynes submitted as well, and have since held the Tor as loyal vassals of House Martell.

At the time of _A Game of Thrones_, the house is led by Lord Trebor Jordayne, whose daughter Myria is his heir. The golden quill on their checkered green field has made the Jordaynes a house associated with letters and learning among the Dornish lords, and their seat at the Tor commands the coast with quiet authority.
