House Belgrave is a noble house of Westeros whose home region is no longer recorded, though two of its members left a mark on the histories the maesters kept. Ser Gyles Belgrave served in the Kingsguard of King Aegon II Targaryen, and chose execution at the hands of Lord Cregan Stark over taking the black when accused of treason during the Hour of the Wolf.

Generations later, a proud Lord Belgrave was humbled by King Baelor I Targaryen, who ordered him to wash the ulcerous feet of beggars as penance for his pride. No Belgrave is recorded to have appeared in the events of the War of the Five Kings.
