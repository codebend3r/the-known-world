House Wendwater of Wendwater holds a modest seat along the river of the same name, where the kingswood presses close to the road south of King's Landing. The house descends from Andal adventurers who crossed Blackwater Bay during the long migration, finding the First Men lords of that country already weakened by generations of war. Rather than seize land by fire and sword, the founders of the house settled peaceably beside the river, took on its name as their own, and built a sturdy crossing over the water. The three trees of their blazon (green in summer, red in autumn, bare in winter) speak to a family that has always known how to endure, and their words, For All Seasons, echo that same patient temper.

Controlling the Wendwater bridge placed the house astride one of the few reliable passages between King's Landing and the deep kingswood, giving them a quiet importance that outlasted stronger neighbours. When Aegon the Conqueror united the Seven Kingdoms, the lords of Wendwater bent the knee without contest and were confirmed in their seat within the new crownlands. They have remained minor bannermen ever since, their name appearing rarely in the chronicles of great events, their bridge gathering its tolls through summer and winter alike. At the opening of _A Game of Thrones_ they owe fealty to House Baratheon as the royal house of the Seven Kingdoms.
