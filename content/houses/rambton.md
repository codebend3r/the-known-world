House Rambton of the crownlands blazons a white ram's head, golden-horned, on red, arms Lord Eddard Stark himself notes among the banners at the Hand's tourney. Little else is recorded of the house before the fires of the Red God came to Dragonstone.

When Stannis Baratheon's queen's men come to burn the sept of Dragonstone, Ser Hubard Rambton leads his three sons to defend it, and in the brawl that follows he and one son cut down four of the queen's men before falling themselves. His two surviving sons do not long outlive him: Queen Selyse Florent has them burned alive for treason alongside Lord Guncer Sunglass, who had refused to support Stannis after the killing. Whether any Rambton blood remains after that day is not recorded.
