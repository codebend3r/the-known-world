House Hardy holds lands on Crackclaw Point in the crownlands, sworn to House Baratheon of King's Landing, though neither their arms nor their words have ever come down to us. Lord Lucifer Hardy is remembered there as a rare peacemaker, a lord whose strength of arms quieted the point's endless small wars for the length of his life, only for the old quarrels to resume once he was gone. A Hardy knight is also recorded to have served among the Kingsguard, unnamed in what survives of the record.

No Hardy has appeared among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
