House Meadows of Grassy Vale holds Grassfield Keep for House Tyrell, its banner a ring of many-colored flowers on green. During the Faith Militant uprising, King Maegor I Targaryen gave Lord Meadows command of six lordly hosts against the Poor Fellows of Wat the Hewer at Stonebridge; the Poor Fellows were slaughtered on the banks of the Mander, but not before Wat cut down half a dozen knights, Lord Meadows among them.

Young Lord Elwood Meadows, cousin to the Fossoways, serves as Ser Cortnay Penrose's second-in-command when Stannis Baratheon besieges Storm's End. When Cortnay dies, Elwood names himself castellan and yields the castle to Stannis rather than hold out further. He remains loyally at Stannis's side afterward, serving beneath Ser Gilbert Farring as castellan and later as seneschal of Storm's End, one of the few Reach lords to throw in with the king on Dragonstone.
