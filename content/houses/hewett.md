House Hewett of Oakenshield rules one of the Shield Islands guarding the mouth of the Mander, their walled seat above the harbor of Lord Hewett's Town standing as the Reach's first line of defense against ironborn raiders sailing up from the sea. Their arms, an oaken escutcheon studded with iron, recall the thick gates of that castle. In the years before the War of the Five Kings, Ser Glendon Hewett serves at Eastwatch-by-the-Sea, and is among those who drag Jon Snow from his bed at Castle Black to accuse him of betraying the Night's Watch.

In 300 AC, Euron Greyjoy's ironborn fleet takes the Shield Islands, and Oakenshield falls with them. Lord Humfrey Hewett is bound and muzzled in his own hall while Euron's captains feast, his wife and daughters made to serve the conquerors; his bastard daughter Falia Flowers becomes Euron's lover, and word later reaches King's Landing that Lord Hewett himself is dead. Euron grants Oakenshield to Nute the Barber, stripping the Hewett name of the lordship it had held for generations.
