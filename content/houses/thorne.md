House Thorne is a noble house of the crownlands, sworn to House Baratheon of King's Landing. According to the Citadel's semi-canon rolls, they blazon their shields with a silver flail on red within a black border rayonne. In generations past a Thorne knight sat the Kingsguard: Ser Rickard Thorne, charged with protecting the boy prince Maelor Targaryen during the Dance of the Dragons, died with his charge when a mob overran them at Bitterbridge. Later, Ser Adrian Thorne commanded the City Watch of King's Landing during the regency of the boy king Aegon III.

Ser Alliser Thorne stood the battlements of King's Landing when Tywin Lannister's sack came during Robert's Rebellion, and chose the Night's Watch over the headsman's block. As master-at-arms at Castle Black he trains Jon Snow and the other recruits with open contempt, and later stands for Lord Commander himself, coming in sixth before withdrawing to back Janos Slynt, who loses to Jon regardless. Thorne offers Jon no resistance when Slynt is seized for execution, but when Jon later sends him ranging beyond the Wall with Dywen, Alliser goes believing it a death sentence, warning Jon only that if the Others kill him, he will remember, and return.
