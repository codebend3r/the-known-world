House Long once held Wolf's Den on the White Knife, though it passed from their hands long before the events of the present day, and no chronicle records when or why. No arms or words for the house survive.

By the time of _A Dance with Dragons_, the Long name is carried across the narrow sea rather than in the north: Ser Lucifer Long rides among the Windblown, one of the free companies contesting the siege of Meereen, though sellswords take what names please them and his kinship to the northern house is unconfirmed.
