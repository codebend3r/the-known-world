House Borrell of Sweetsister rules that island of the Three Sisters from Breakwater castle in Sisterton, sworn to House Sunderland rather than to the Eyrie directly. Their arms show a white spider crab on grey-green, and their many titles, Lord of Sweetsister, Shield of Sisterton, Master of Breakwater Castle, Keeper of the Night Lamp, recall a house descended from pirate kings until the Starks came down on them with fire and sword some 4,700 years before the Conquest. For five thousand years the Borrells have carried "the mark," a webbing between the three middle fingers that runs true in the blood.

During the Dance of the Dragons, Prince Jacaerys Velaryon won House Borrell to the side of the blacks on his visit to Sisterton, though the Sunderlands' later meddling in two Blackfyre Rebellions cost the Sistermen dearly. At the start of Robert's Rebellion, Lord Borrell let Eddard Stark sail on for White Harbor and asked only that he keep silent of his stay, in case the rebellion should fail.

Lord Godric Borrell rules Sweetsister at the time of the War of the Five Kings. When Stannis Baratheon leaves Ser Davos Seaworth stranded on Sweetsister, Godric quietly sends him on to White Harbor rather than deliver him to Lord Triston Sunderland, a small mercy that may have changed the course of the war in the north.
