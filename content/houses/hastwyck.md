House Hastwyck is a minor noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Little of its history survives beyond a single scandal of the Targaryen era: Ser Morgil Hastwyck slandered Queen Naerys Targaryen, reputedly at King Aegon IV's own encouragement, and was slain for it by Prince Aemon the Dragonknight.

No Hastwyck has been recorded among the living at the time of the War of the Five Kings.
