House Lowther is a minor noble house of the Reach, sworn to Highgarden, known only by its silver dolphin blazon. Neither their words nor any deed of theirs has come down through the chronicles.

No Lowther has been named among the players of the game of thrones in the years of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
