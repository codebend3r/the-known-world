House Algood is a minor house of the westerlands, sworn to House Lannister of Casterly Rock. Heraldic tradition gives their arms as a golden wreath upon a field of blue, bound within a golden border.

No lord of House Algood is recorded to have played any notable part in the great wars of the realm, and the house's words, seat, and current head are alike unrecorded by the maesters. It remains, so far as is known, a quiet and untroubled line of the westerlands.
