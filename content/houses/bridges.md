House Bridges is a minor noble house of the Reach, sworn to House Tyrell of Highgarden. Its arms, three blue pallets on white beneath a golden chief bearing a black three-arched stone bridge, are recorded by the heralds of the Citadel, though the house's words have gone unwritten.

No Bridges is known to have played any part in the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_, and little else of the house's history has survived into the present age.
