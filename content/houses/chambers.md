House Chambers is a minor house of the riverlands, presently sworn to House Baelish as Lord Paramount of the Trident, having formerly held its fealty to House Tully of Riverrun before Petyr Baelish's elevation in the wake of the War of the Five Kings.

During the Dance of the Dragons, a Lord Chambers declared for the black dragon, Rhaenyra Targaryen, and fought in her cause at the Battle by the Lakeshore. No Chambers has appeared among the notable figures of the realm during the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_.
