House Wormwood is a minor noble house of the Seven Kingdoms whose seat and region have gone unrecorded, along with their arms and words. Their sole recorded deed comes from the Dance of the Dragons: Ser Julian Wormwood, a supporter of the green faction, was sent across the narrow sea with Ser Marston Waters to hire sellswords for King Aegon II Targaryen.

No Wormwood has been recorded since, and the house's fortunes in the years leading to the War of the Five Kings are unknown.
