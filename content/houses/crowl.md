House Crowl of Deepdown holds its seat on the wild isle of Skagos, in the north, blazoning piles of flame-red and black upon their shields. The Crowls are numbered among the Stoneborn of Skagos, that stubborn and half-legendary people who keep their own ways apart from the mainland.

More than a thousand years ago, a Crowl rose to lead the Night's Watch itself as Lord Commander, proof that even so remote a house could produce men who served the realm beyond their own shores. In the present day, the Crowls' fealty is claimed by House Bolton of the Dreadfort as the new-made Wardens of the North, though how firmly Skagos itself accepts that claim, so far from Winterfell and so wary of outsiders, remains to be seen.
