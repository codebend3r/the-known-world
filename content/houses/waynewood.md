House Waynewood of Ironoaks is among the oldest and strongest of the lines sworn to the Eyrie, an Andal house of long standing whose seat rises in the eastern hills of the Vale, above the shore of the narrow sea. Their banners show a black broken wheel upon a field of green, and though the singers have set down no words for them, few houses of the mountain realm command so many swords or so much respect; the Waynewoods have answered the falcon's call since the Andal kings first came down out of the high passes, and the strength of Ironoaks is reckoned a thing not lightly spent.

In the years following Lord Jon Arryn's death, the widowed Lady Anya Waynewood holds Ironoaks for her house, and she is numbered among the Lords Declarant — those great vassals who banded together against Petyr Baelish's regency and the keeping of the boy lord Robert at the Eyrie. She has three sons in Ser Morton, Ser Donnel — the Knight of the Bloody Gate — and Wallace, and into her care was given the young Ser Harrold Hardyng, kin to her house through the daughters of Elys Waynewood and reckoned by many the heir presumptive to the Vale. It is upon this ward and his claim that Lord Baelish has set his designs, and so the fortunes of Ironoaks are bound, for good or ill, to the question of who shall one day sit the high seat of the Arryns.
