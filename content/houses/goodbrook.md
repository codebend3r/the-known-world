House Goodbrook holds lands somewhere east of the Red Fork, near House Smallwood's Acorn Hall, though the name and site of their own seat has gone unrecorded. In the age of the petty kingdoms, a Lord Goodbrook lent his sword to Harwyn Hardhand, King of the Iron Islands, in his war against the Storm King Arrec Durrandon.

The house paid dearly for choosing wrong during Robert's Rebellion: rather than follow their liege lord's banner, Lord Goodbrook stayed loyal to King Aerys II Targaryen, and Lord Hoster Tully descended on the Goodbrook lands and burned at least one village in punishment. Peace was made after the war, but the family's wealth never fully recovered.

Lord Lymond Goodbrook rides in Edmure Tully's company on the march to the Twins for his wedding to Roslin Frey, while Ser Garse Goodbrook, husband to Kyra Frey, is cut down by Sandor Clegane while putting Robb Stark's men to the sword during the Red Wedding. Lymond himself is pardoned by the Iron Throne after the massacre, and Goodbrook banners are later seen flying under Ser Daven Lannister at the siege of Riverrun.
