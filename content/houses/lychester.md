House Lychester holds a small square keep in the riverlands. During the Faith Militant uprising, Lord Jon Lychester fought against King Maegor I Targaryen at the Great Fork of the Blackwater, and Queen Visenya answered the following year by loosing Vhagar to burn the Lychester seat along with several others. In a later generation, Lord Lymond Lychester took six wounds defending a bridge from Ser Maynard, and every one of his sons died in Robert's Rebellion, some for the dragon and some for the stag.

By the War of the Five Kings, Lymond is an old, wandering-witted knight who can speak of little but his long-ago duel with Ser Maynard. The brotherhood without banners finds him thus when they come seeking word of Beric Dondarrion, and it is his maester, Roone, who points them toward the Lady of the Leaves. When Riverrun falls under siege, House Lychester's banner flies among the riverlords who have bent the knee to House Lannister.
