House Hunter of Longbow Hall is an ancient house of the Vale, numbered among the First Men who made their home in the eastern mountains long before the coming of the Andals. When Andal warlords crossed the Narrow Sea and drove into the Vale, the Hunters stood against them alongside other old First Men lords. So valued was their allegiance that High King Robar II Royce is said to have won it in an archery contest with the Lord Hunter of that age, outpacing him shaft for shaft before a great assembly of lords, though some songs claim Robar cheated. The Hunters marched with Robar to the Battle of the Seven Stars, where Artys Arryn broke the First Men host and slew Robar on the field. With their king dead and their cause lost, the Hunters bent the knee to Artys and have since remained sworn bannermen to the Eyrie.

At the time of _A Game of Thrones_, the house is led by Lord Eon Hunter, an elderly and gout-afflicted man who has ruled Longbow Hall for nearly sixty years. His three sons, Gilwood, Eustace, and Harlan, quarrel over his succession. Lord Eon attended Lysa Arryn at the Eyrie and was among those who offered to champion her in Tyrion Lannister's trial by combat. He would not long outlive that occasion.
