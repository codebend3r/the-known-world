House Briar was one of some dozen First Men houses that dwelt along the Greenblood in ancient Dorne, from whose ranks the Dornish periodically chose a High King to rule over all of them. Of its arms and words nothing has survived; not even the maesters of the Citadel can say what device the Briars bore into battle.

A disputed election among the Greenblood houses plunged the region into a generation of war, in which three of the electing houses were destroyed outright. House Briar endured that struggle only to be conquered along with its neighbors when the Andals came to Dorne, and no Briar is recorded to have lived to see the Rhoynish queen Nymeria unite the peninsula under House Martell many centuries later.
