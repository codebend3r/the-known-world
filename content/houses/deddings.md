House Deddings is a wealthy noble house of the riverlands, though no record of their arms or words survives in any account. Their seat, whatever it was called, stood among those burned in a single night by Visenya Targaryen astride Vhagar during the Faith Militant uprising, alongside the castles of Houses Blanetree, Lychester, Terrick, and Wayn, a devastation the house evidently survived and, in time, rebuilt from.

During the Dance of the Dragons, the Deddings paid dearly for the chaos that gripped the realm. Davos Deddings was stabbed in the eye while shielding his sister, Lady Darla, from rapers amid the riots that swept King's Landing, and Lord Lyonel Deddings burned alongside Lord Stanton Piper when the dragon Vermithor fell upon the Second Battle of Tumbleton.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings the house remains one of some wealth in the riverlands, owing fealty first to House Tully of Riverrun and later, as Lord Petyr Baelish rises to become Lord Paramount of the Trident, to House Baelish. The reigning Lord and Lady Deddings do not survive the war themselves, both slain by the forces of House Lannister as the riverlands burn once more.
