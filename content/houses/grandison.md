House Grandison of Grandview holds its seat in the stormlands under a black sleeping lion on yellow, and its words, "Rouse Me Not," suit a house whose lords are remembered as much for slumber as for sword-work. Lord Lorent Grandison served as a regent for the boy-king Aegon III, and was noted even then for loving his sleep; generations later Ser Harlan Grandison of the Kingsguard died in his own bed, vacating a white cloak that passed to Ser Jaime Lannister.

During Robert's Rebellion, the Lord Grandison of the day was captured alongside Lord Cafferen at the battles fought around Summerhall, and Robert Baratheon won them both over as allies rather than enemies; wounded fighting for Robert's cause at the Trident, Grandison died of those wounds within the year. His successor, the aged Lord Hugh Grandison, called Greybeard, is counted among the lords sworn to Storm's End by the time of the War of the Five Kings, and famously fell asleep between courses at a Dornish feast held to consider him as a match for Princess Arianne Martell. Ser Narbert Grandison, of uncertain kinship to Hugh, serves among Stannis Baratheon's queen's men guarding Selyse Florent at the Wall.
