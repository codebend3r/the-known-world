House Mertyns of Mistwood is a stormlander house sworn to House Baratheon, blazoning a white great horned owl upon grey. Their words are not recorded. During the First Dornish War, a Lord Mertyns and his entire household were poisoned by Dornish assassins who smuggled a cask of poisoned wine into Mistwood. In more recent generations, Lord Jon Mertyns attended the Golden Wedding of Queen Alyssa Velaryon and Lord Rogar Baratheon, and Ser Michael Mertyns, called the White Owl, served in the Kingsguard.

By the time of the War of the Five Kings, Mistwood is held by Lady Mary Mertyns, whose sons and grandsons ride to war for Renly Baratheon, one of them wounded at the Blackwater. With the Mertyns men away, Mistwood falls to the Golden Company during their invasion of the stormlands, and Lady Mary is kept under house arrest by Young John Mudd and Chain.
