House Shawney is a noble house of the riverlands whose seat's name and location have gone unrecorded, though clues place it somewhere in the southwestern riverlands near the Gods Eye. Their arms, per semi-canon sources, are per fess of blue, red, and green above, representing the Trident's three forks, and a white catfish on black below; their words are not known.

During the First Blackfyre Rebellion, Lord Shawney fought on the right of the Blackfyre host alongside Ser Aegor Rivers at the Battle of the Redgrass Field and was nearly killed by his wounds. Years later he attended Lord Ambrose Butterwell's wedding tourney at Whitewalls, where the more highly born Lord Costayne looked down on him as beneath his own station. By the time Petyr Baelish rules the Trident as its Lord Paramount, House Shawney answers to him rather than to Riverrun directly.
