House Breakstone is a minor noble house of the Vale of Arryn, sworn to House Arryn of the Eyrie. Little of its early history has come down, though its knights blazon a red sledgehammer as their arms, a device as plain and workmanlike as the mountain roads their home shares with the clansmen of the Mountains of the Moon.

The house's only recorded member of note is Ser Edmund Breakstone, who travels to the Gates of the Moon hoping to win a place among the Brotherhood of Winged Knights, a company Lord Robert Arryn's regents are said to be raising. As this episode is drawn from a released sample chapter of the forthcoming _The Winds of Winter_, the particulars remain subject to change before publication.
