House Jast was formed in the Andal invasion by the union of a First Men line with incoming Andal nobility, and has held its place among the lesser lords of the westerlands ever since. In the days of Lord Tytos Lannister's misrule, a private quarrel between Jast and House Falwell went unsettled by Casterly Rock and was instead resolved by melee: nine men killed and twenty-seven more maimed before the feud finally burned itself out.

Lord Antario Jast rides with Ser Stafford Lannister's host after the Battle of Oxcross and is reported slain, but survives as a prisoner at Pinkmaiden Castle for the rest of the war. Freed after the Lannister-Tyrell victory, a wounded and diminished Antario helps escort Lord Tywin Lannister's body home to Casterly Rock, his breastplate marked with three golden lion's heads.
