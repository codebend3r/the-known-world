House Wells of Dorne, sworn to House Martell of Sunspear, is not to be confused with the unrelated House Wells of the north; its own arms have never been recorded.

Ser Willam Wells serves among the party escorting Prince Quentyn Martell to the Free Cities, and is slain when raiders fall upon their ship, the _Meadowlark_, off the Disputed Lands. Ser Theodan Wells, called Ser Theodan the True, joins the Warrior's Sons in King's Landing and later walks beside Cersei Lannister on her walk of shame, though the maesters are uncertain whether he hails from this Dornish line or its northern namesake.
