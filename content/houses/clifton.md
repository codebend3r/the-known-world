House Clifton is a knightly house of the westerlands, sworn to House Farman of Fair Isle. Neither its arms nor its words have been recorded.

Ser Gareth Clifton, the current head of the house, wed Jeyne Farman, kinswoman of his own liege lord, and the couple had a dozen children together. Two more distant Cliftons have found their way into the wider affairs of the realm: Hugh Clifton served in Queen Margaery Tyrell's household guard in King's Landing and was swept up in Cersei Lannister's scheme against the queen, arrested on the strength of a warrant she signed against him; and Ser Humfrey Clifton rode north in the company of King Stannis Baratheon on his march toward Winterfell.
