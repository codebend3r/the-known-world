Daemon Blackfyre revolts against his half-brother Daeron II, and the war is decided at the Battle of the Redgrass Field, where Daemon and his twin sons die to the arrows of Bloodraven. An earlier clash at the Battle of the Honeywine had already checked the rebel advance.
