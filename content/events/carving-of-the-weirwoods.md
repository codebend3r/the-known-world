Before the coming of men there were the children of the forest, small and secret folk who called themselves those who sing the song of earth, and beside them walked the giants. In the dawn of days, the tales say, the children carved faces into the boles of the weirwoods, that the gods of stream and stone and tree might watch over the woods through eyes of bleeding sap. From these heart trees grew the worship of the old gods, the nameless gods that the First Men would later take for their own and carry to every godswood in Westeros. No maester can say when the first face was cut, for the children kept no books and raised no cities, and all that is left of that age is legend and the trees themselves.
