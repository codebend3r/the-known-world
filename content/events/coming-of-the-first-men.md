Some twelve thousand years before the Conquest, so the oldest histories claim, the First Men crossed from Essos into Westeros over the great land bridge that men now call the Arm of Dorne, driving their herds before them and bearing weapons of bronze. They found a green country held by the children of the forest and the giants, and they set about clearing it, burning the weirwoods and cutting down the carved faces, for they feared the trees that watched. War followed, long and bitter, until at last both races wearied of the killing. The maesters caution that no true record survives of that crossing, and that the count of years is the guesswork of singers; yet from these wanderers come the greater part of the blood of Westeros, and its oldest tongues, runes, and kings.
