Where Aegon the Conqueror first came ashore at the mouth of the Blackwater, a city grew up around his wooden Aegonfort on what men soon called Aegon's High Hill. From the swords surrendered by his defeated enemies, a thousand of them, Balerion's fire and fifty-nine days of smith-work wrought the Iron Throne — hard, cold, and jagged, for Aegon held that a king should never sit easy. In the first year of his reign, from which all years are now counted, the High Septon anointed him at Oldtown with the seven oils, and Aegon, Rhaenys, and Visenya made their seat at the new city rather than Dragonstone. King's Landing would grow into the greatest city of the realm, ringed in stone under Maegor, crowned with the Red Keep, and stinking, as its people cheerfully admit, all the way to Duskendale.
