The Valyrians were shepherd folk on a peninsula of eastern Essos when they found dragons lairing in the ring of volcanoes called the Fourteen Flames, and learned (by what art or accident no book truly says, though the Valyrians claimed descent from dragons themselves) to master them with horn and whip and sorcery. On dragonback the shepherds became the dragonlords, and their Freehold, so named because every free citizen held a voice in its governance though in truth forty rival families of dragonlords contended for mastery, grew across five thousand years into the greatest civilization the world has known. Fire and blood broke Old Ghis, humbled the Rhoyne, and carried Valyrian roads, glass candles, and spell-forged steel across a continent, until all the lands from the narrow sea to Slaver's Bay owed tribute to the Lands of the Long Summer.
