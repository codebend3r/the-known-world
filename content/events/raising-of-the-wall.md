When the Long Night ended and the Others were driven back into the uttermost north, the men of the dawn resolved that the darkness should never come again unlooked-for. Legend names Brandon the Builder its architect: a wall of solid ice thrown across the neck of the world, a hundred leagues from the Gorge to the Bay of Seals, raised higher by every generation that followed until it stood seven hundred feet above the forests. Giants, some tales say, helped set its foundations, and the children of the forest gave their magics to strengthen it against what waits beyond. To man it was founded the Night's Watch, the sworn brotherhood in black that takes no wife, holds no lands, and wins no glory, whose watch begins at sunset and ends only in death. Nineteen castles were raised along its length in time, though no more than a handful have ever been garrisoned at once.
