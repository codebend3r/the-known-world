With the Targaryens dead or fled and the Iron Throne won by his warhammer, Robert Baratheon needed a queen, and Jon Arryn, his Hand, counselled that none would serve the peace better than Cersei of House Lannister, golden daughter of the richest lord in the realm. They were wed in the Great Sept of Baelor with all the pomp the crown could muster, and for a time the realm rejoiced in its young warrior king. Yet the marriage was cold from its first night, for Robert mourned Lyanna Stark in his cups and said her name where his wife could hear. The peace that followed was long but never cheap: Robert's tourneys and feasts poured out gold faster than the realm brought it in, until the crown owed millions to Casterly Rock and the Iron Bank of Braavos alike, debts whose weight his successors would feel.
