For fourteen years Jon Arryn, Lord of the Eyrie and Hand of the King, held Robert's realm together, paying its debts, softening its quarrels, and raising no banners he could avoid. Then, in the two hundred and ninety-eighth year after the Conquest, a sickness took him — sudden and swift as a knife, in a man hale the week before — and he died in the Red Keep with his last words a riddle: the seed is strong. That it was poison few then suspected; that the poison was the Tears of Lys, given by his own squire at his own wife's bidding, fewer still would learn, and later whispers laying the deed on the Lannisters were aimed with purpose. His death set every stone moving: Robert rode north to Winterfell to make Ned Stark his Hand, Lysa Arryn fled to the Vale sowing accusations, and the realm began its long slide toward war.
