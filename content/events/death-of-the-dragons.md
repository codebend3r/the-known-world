The Dance of the Dragons consumed dragons even faster than it consumed Targaryens, and the creatures that survived the war did not thrive after it. In the reign of Aegon III (the Dragonbane, men called him, who had watched Sunfyre devour his mother and never loved the beasts thereafter), the last hatchlings came into the world sickly, stunted, and misshapen, and one by one they died. The last of them, a green female no larger than a dog, with withered wings and a clutch of eggs that never hatched, perished at King's Landing in or about the hundred and fifty-third year after the Conquest. Some whispered the Dragonbane had them poisoned; others blamed maesters, or the judgment of the gods. However it was, the fire went out of House Targaryen, and though kings kept dragon eggs and hatched schemes upon them for a century and a half, no flame would wake again until a funeral pyre on the Dothraki sea.
