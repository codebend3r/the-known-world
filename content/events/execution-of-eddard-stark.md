Eddard Stark, Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King, and the most honourable man in the Seven Kingdoms, learned the secret Jon Arryn had died for — that the queen's children were the Kingslayer's — and gave Cersei warning to flee, and was betrayed for his mercy. Littlefinger turned the gold cloaks, the king's testament was torn, and Ned was taken in the throne room with his household slain around him. To save his daughters he did the hardest thing: knelt before the Great Sept of Baelor and confessed to treasons never committed, on the promise of the black. But the boy king Joffrey wanted a bolder show, and called for Ser Ilyn Payne and Ice. The head came off to the wailing of the crowd and the breaking of two girls, and in the North men stopped speaking of justice from King's Landing and began crowning the Young Wolf.
