On the day the great pit of Daznak reopened, with the queen and her new king enthroned above the sand and poisoned locusts waiting at her elbow — a dish she never touched — the smell of blood brought an uninvited guest. Drogon, largest and blackest of her three, half-wild since she had chained his brothers beneath the pyramid, dropped screaming from the sky to feed upon the slain. As spears and boltmen closed on the dragon, Daenerys ran to him, took up a pitmaster's whip, and did what no one living had done: mounted a dragon and flew. Drogon bore her far out onto the Dothraki sea and would not be turned, and there, weeks later, sick and hungry among the grasses, she was found and ringed by the riders of Khal Jhaqo's khalasar. Behind her, Meereen girded for siege as the Yunkish alliance closed in, and Tyrion Lannister, Victarion Greyjoy, and half the ambitions of the east converged upon her city.
