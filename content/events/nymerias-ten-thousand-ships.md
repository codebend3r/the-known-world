When Valyria's dragons broke the armies of the Rhoyne and Garin's quarter-million perished, the warrior queen Nymeria gathered what remained of her people (the old, the women, the children, in every hull that would float) and led ten thousand ships into exile. For years they wandered the seas, harried from Basilisk Point to the Summer Isles to Naath, finding no rest, until at last Nymeria brought them to the dry shores of Dorne. There she wed Mors Martell, lord of the sands, and burned the ten thousand ships so that no Rhoynar might dream of returning to a home that was gone. Dorne's quarrelling kings she broke one by one, sending six defeated rivals to the Wall in golden fetters, and the Rhoynish ways came with her: the eldest child to rule regardless of sex, and House Nymeros Martell to rule from Sunspear ever after.
