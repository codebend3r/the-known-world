Condemned by his own father for a poisoning he did not commit, robbed of trial by combat when the Mountain crushed Prince Oberyn's skull, Tyrion Lannister waited in the black cells for the axe, until Jaime came with a key, and a confession that cut deeper than the axe would have: Tysha, the wife Tyrion had been made to believe a bought whore, had been no such thing. Instead of fleeing, Tyrion climbed the shaft to the Tower of the Hand, found Shae in his father's bed and strangled her with the chain of the Hand, then took a crossbow from the wall and found Lord Tywin on the privy. Wherever whores go, said the great lion, and the bolt took him through the bowels (proof, men japed, that he did not shit gold after all). Tyrion sailed east in a wine cask, and the realm Tywin had held by fear began at once to come apart in the hands of his daughter.
