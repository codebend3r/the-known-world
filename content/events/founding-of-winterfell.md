The same Brandon the Builder credited with the Wall is named the founder of Winterfell and of House Stark itself, raised, the tales say, in the years after the Long Night to stand as the great stronghold of the North. The castle grew over the millennia into a sprawl of walls within walls, built above hot springs whose waters run through its stones and keep its halls warm against the cruellest winters, with an ancient godswood at its heart where the heart tree's carved face has watched for ten thousand years. From Winterfell the Starks ruled as Kings of Winter, grinding down rival kings — the Red Kings of the Dreadfort, the Barrow Kings, the Marsh Kings — until all the North knelt to the direwolf. The maesters grant that the Starks' line is truly ancient beyond record, whatever the truth of Bran the Builder himself.
