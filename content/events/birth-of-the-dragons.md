Khal Drogo died as the maegi's bloodmagic had left him, an emptied thing behind emptied eyes, and his khalasar scattered to new khals, leaving a widowed girl with a handful of the old and the weak. Daenerys built her husband's pyre high on the Dothraki sea, laid the maegi Mirri Maz Duur bound upon it (blood pays for blood), and set the three petrified dragon eggs about Drogo's body. Then, as the flames roared to the sky and the eyes of her little khalasar, she walked into the fire. When the embers cooled at dawn they found her unburnt among the ashes, hair gone, and at her breast and shoulder and knee three creatures out of dead centuries: Drogon, Rhaegal, and Viserion, the first living dragons the world had seen in a hundred and fifty years, hatched beneath a bleeding red star. Those who had stayed knelt, and Ser Jorah named her what the world would learn to call her: the Mother of Dragons.
