Outside the walls of Pentos, in the manse of Magister Illyrio Mopatis, the beggar king Viserys Targaryen sold the only thing left to him: his thirteen-year-old sister, given to Khal Drogo of the Dothraki against the promise of forty thousand screaming riders to win back the Iron Throne. The wedding lasted from dawn to dusk in the Dothraki fashion — a dozen men died in the dancing, which the horselords account a good omen — and the bride sat in terror through it all. The gifts made the day: from Ser Jorah Mormont, books of the Seven Kingdoms; from Drogo, a silver filly grey as the winter sea; and from Illyrio, three petrified dragon eggs from the Shadow Lands, beautiful and dead. Whether the magister thought them mere treasure, only he could say. Daenerys Stormborn rode her silver out onto the grass that night a frightened child, and did not remain one long.
