To rid herself of the crown's debts to the Faith and to strike at Margaery Tyrell, Cersei Lannister granted the new High Septon what kings had denied the Faith for two and a half centuries: leave to restore the Faith Militant, the Swords and Stars of old, holy men under arms. The sparrows she armed proved no one's creatures but the gods'. Her plot to snare the little queen in false adultery ended with the High Sparrow's gaolers holding Cersei herself, and confession bought her freedom only at a price: shorn to the scalp, stripped bare at the doors of the Great Sept of Baelor, she walked the long way down and through Fishmonger's Square to the Red Keep with a septa behind her tolling shame, shame, shame, while the city she despised looked its fill. She came to the castle gates bloodied of foot and stripped of far more than clothing — and was met by an armoured colossus newly sworn to the Kingsguard.
