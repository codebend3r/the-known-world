At Vaes Dothrak beneath the Mother of Mountains, Daenerys ate the raw heart of a stallion before the crones of the dosh khaleen, who proclaimed the son in her womb the Stallion Who Mounts the World, the khal of khals. It was a triumph her brother could not forgive. Drunk, desperate, and years past patience for the crown he was owed, Viserys drew steel in the sacred city where all blood is forbidden, laid the point at his sister's belly, and demanded of Drogo his price. The khal answered that he would give Viserys the golden crown men would tremble to behold: his belt of medallions went into the cookfire, and the molten gold went over the beggar king's head. He was no dragon, Daenerys thought, watching; fire cannot kill a dragon. With him died the male line of the exiled kings, and the claim passed to the girl on the silver.
