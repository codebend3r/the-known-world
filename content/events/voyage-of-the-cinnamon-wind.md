The Cinnamon Wind, a swan ship of Tall Trees Town under the merchant captain Quhuru Mo, took aboard at Braavos a fat brother of the Night's Watch, a wildling girl and her babe, and a blind old maester — Samwell Tarly, Gilly, and Aemon of House Targaryen, bound for Oldtown by the long southern way. It was the sailor Xhondo who vouched for them after pulling Sam from the harbour, and the captain's daughter Kojja Mo who commanded the ship's archers. Aemon, one hundred and two years old, who had refused a crown and outlived his dynasty on the Wall, weakened as the ship ran south; hearing at last of dragons born across the sea, he burned his failing hours on prophecy — the dragon must have three heads — and died gently between Braavos and the Summer Isles. The crew, after their custom, celebrated his long life with wine and love, and gave his body to the sea.
