Of all the heroes of the dawn, none is claimed by more houses than Garth Greenhand, the High King of the First Men, who is said to have led them across the Arm of Dorne, or in other tales to have walked Westeros before them, teaching men to sow and reap. Wherever he passed the land grew rich; some singers say he wore a crown of vines and flowers and made the barren bloom, and darker tales whisper he demanded blood sacrifice to ensure a bountiful harvest. From his countless children the Reach counts its lineages: Garth the Gardener of Highgarden, Florys the Fox, John the Oak, Rowan Gold-Tree, and a dozen more, so that half the great houses of the south name him forefather. The maesters note drily that a man who fathered so much and lived so long belongs to myth, not history.
