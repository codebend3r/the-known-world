Ruling Meereen proved crueller than taking it. The Sons of the Harpy murdered freedmen and shavepates by night, chalking their tally on walls; Astapor, remade and ruined again, sent plague and refugees to her gates; and Yunkai hired sellswords and called the slavers of the east to war. So Daenerys did what queens have always done, and bought peace with herself: she took to husband Hizdahr zo Loraq, a noble of the old blood whose suit had been pressed upon her ninety times, whose promise was that if he could stop the killings for ninety days he was worth marrying. The killings stopped. They were wed in the Temple of the Graces after the Ghiscari fashion, and the price of the peace came due at once — the fighting pits she abhorred reopened in her name, and the queen sat the purple above the sand to watch men die for sport, wondering which smiling face about her was the Harpy's.
