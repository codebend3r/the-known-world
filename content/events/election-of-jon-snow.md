With the Old Bear murdered by his own men and the wildling host broken beneath the Wall, the Night's Watch fell to choosing its nine hundred and ninety-eighth Lord Commander, and fell to quarrelling instead, ballot after deadlocked ballot between Janos Slynt's schemers and the proud old knights. The choosing turned on a raven that hopped from Samwell Tarly's contriving to a bastard boy's shoulder, crying his name: Jon Snow, once Ned Stark's motherless son, lately a turncloak among the free folk, and the man King Stannis was courting to make a Stark. Elected over Slynt and Ser Denys Mallister, Jon hanged Slynt for defiance soon enough, gave Stannis hard counsel, and then did what no Lord Commander had dared: opened the gates and brought the free folk through the Wall, that the Others might find fewer corpses to raise. Half his brothers never forgave him.
