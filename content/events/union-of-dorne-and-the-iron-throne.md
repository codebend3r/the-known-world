What the Conqueror's dragons and Daeron the Young Dragon's forty thousand could not take, quiet diplomacy won at last. Dorne alone of the seven kingdoms had never bent the knee, bleeding every host sent against it, but a pair of marriages did what armies had not: Daeron, heir to the Iron Throne, wed Myriah Martell of Sunspear, and later his sister Daenerys was promised to Maron Martell, Prince of Dorne, though the Blackfyre pretender would make her the pretext of a rebellion. In the hundred and eighty-seventh year after the Conquest, Prince Maron knelt and did homage to King Daeron II, and Dorne came into the realm on terms no other kingdom enjoyed: the Martells kept the style of Prince and their Rhoynish laws and customs. The Seven Kingdoms were, for the first time, truly seven under one crown — a peace some marcher lords and Dornish hotheads have never quite forgiven.
