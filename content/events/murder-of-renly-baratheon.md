Renly Baratheon crowned himself with the love of the Reach and the stormlands, a golden king of summer with the greatest host seen in Westeros since the Conquest, and rode toward King's Landing at his leisure, jesting that his brother Stannis had the better claim and the smaller army. Beneath the walls of Storm's End the brothers met and traded terms and insults, and on the morning Renly was to give battle, as his squire Brienne of Tarth armed him in his green-and-gold, a shadow passed through the sealed pavilion (a thing of darkness with, Catelyn Stark swore ever after, Stannis's face) and opened his throat through the gorget. Born of the red priestess Melisandre beneath Storm's End, the deed was blamed on Brienne, who fled with Lady Catelyn. Renly's storm lords went over to Stannis; the Tyrells and their strength went home, to await a better offer.
