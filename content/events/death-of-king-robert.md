Robert of House Baratheon, the First of His Name, who had won a throne with his warhammer and then found he had no taste for sitting it, went hunting boar in the kingswood as his realm quietly came apart behind him. His squire, a Lannister cousin, kept his wineskin full of strongwine at the queen's instruction, and when the boar came Robert was too deep in his cups to take it cleanly. The beast opened him from groin to chest even as he split its skull. He was carried back to the Red Keep to die, pardoning Daenerys Targaryen with his last strength and dictating to Ned Stark a testament naming him Protector of the Realm, words Cersei burned unread. With Robert died the fragile fiction of his heirs' legitimacy, and within the year five kings' banners flew over Westeros.
