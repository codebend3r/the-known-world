Even the gentle Summer Isles settle their quarrels in blood upon occasion, though by their custom such wars are fought by picked champions on ritual ground, that the many be spared the madness of the few. In one such contest Jalabhar Xho, Prince of the Red Flower Vale on Jhala, was defeated and cast out, and by the isles' law an exile who returns does so on pain of death. He sailed for Westeros and the court of King Robert, where his feathered cloaks became a fixture of feasts and tourneys. Season upon season he petitioned the king for gold and swords to win back his vale, and Robert, who liked the man's company too well to refuse him outright, put him off with vague promises year after year, a habit the kings who followed have found equally convenient.
