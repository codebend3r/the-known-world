While King Robert's court feasted at Winterfell, Brandon Stark, seven years old and the best climber the castle had ever bred, went up the walls of the broken First Keep and found a window he was never meant to find. Within were the queen and her twin, the Kingslayer, entwined; and Jaime Lannister, with a rueful word for the things he did for love, pushed the boy from the ledge. Bran did not die. He lay a month in dreams, woke without the use of his legs and without memory of the window, and even then the knives were not done with him: a catspaw's dagger of Valyrian steel, sent to still his tongue forever, instead set Catelyn Stark on the road to Tyrion Lannister's arrest. From one boy's fall came wolf and lion at each other's throats and, in time, a broken prince dreaming of a three-eyed crow beyond the Wall.
