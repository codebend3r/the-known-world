Down the great Rhoyne through the fogs of the Sorrows came the poleboat Shy Maid, carrying south in secret the exile lord Jon Connington and a blue-haired sellsword's son called Young Griff — in truth, it was claimed, Aegon son of Rhaegar, saved from the Sack as a babe and raised to be a king. Their way ran through drowned Chroyane, the festival city the Rhoynar had loved, now the Palace of Sorrow where Garin's curse breeds greyscale in the mists and the stone men — those the curse has claimed whole — haunt the broken bridges. When the stone men came over the rails, the dwarf Tyrion Lannister was dragged into the river, and Connington went in after those who fell. He came out with grey deadness spreading from his fingers, and told no one, reckoning he had years enough to put his silver prince's son on the Iron Throne before the curse collected him.
