Eldest daughter of Eddard and Catelyn, raised on songs of knights and queens. Betrothed to Prince Joffrey Baratheon in 298 AC and brought south to court, where the songs taught her nothing useful. Held hostage at King's Landing after her father's fall, wed against her will to Tyrion Lannister, and after Joffrey's death spirited away by Petyr Baelish under the false name *Alayne Stone*.
