Youngest son of Lord Rickard. When Eddard returned from the south with Winterfell on his shoulders and an infant bastard in his arms, Benjen took the black rather than serve as steward of a hall that was no longer his. Rose to First Ranger of the Night's Watch. Vanished beyond the Wall on a ranging out of Castle Black not long before his nephew Jon arrived to swear the same oath; never returned.
