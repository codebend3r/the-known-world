A daughter of House Prester of Feastfires, taken as second wife to Ser Jason Lannister, fourth son of Lord Gerold the Golden, after the death of his first lady Alys Stackspear. She bore him six children — Stafford and Joanna chief among them, with two further sons and two further daughters whose names the chronicles do not keep, the boys both thought dead by the time of _A Song of Ice and Fire_. Through her daughter Joanna she would in time be grandmother to the queen, the Kingslayer, and the Imp.
