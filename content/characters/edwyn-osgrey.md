The eldest son of Ser Eustace Osgrey, a knight of House Osgrey during the reign of King Daeron II Targaryen. He joined his father in supporting Daemon I Blackfyre's rebellion in 196 AC and was killed at the Battle of the Redgrass Field. He was buried among the blackberries at Standfast. His brief career is recorded in _The Sworn Sword_.
