Ser Jon Hollard was a knight of House Hollard and steward of the Dun Fort at Duskendale, a brother to Ser Symon and Ser Steffon Hollard. He wed the sister of Lord Denys Darklyn, binding the Hollards still more tightly to their overlords. In the aftermath of the Defiance of Duskendale, put down in 277 AC, Ser Jon was executed together with his wife and his son.
