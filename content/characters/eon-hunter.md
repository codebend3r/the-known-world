Eon Hunter ruled Longbow Hall as Lord of House Hunter for nearly sixty years, an old man half-crippled by gout with liver-spotted hands, older even than Jon Arryn. He attended the tourney at Harrenhal, where he jested with Lord Robert Baratheon and Jon Arryn as Prince Rhaegar Targaryen crowned Lyanna Stark queen of love and beauty. After Jon Arryn's death he presented himself at the Eyrie as a suitor to the widowed Lady Lysa Arryn, sending wine from his own cellars; in _A Game of Thrones_ he was the second to offer to champion her in Tyrion Lannister's trial by combat. He was cursed, in Catelyn Stark's reckoning, with three quarrelsome sons, each more grasping than the last. Eon died suddenly late in 299 AC or early in 300 AC, having reigned close to sixty years; his younger sons Eustace and Harlan at once accused their eldest brother Gilwood of murder, though Petyr Baelish later named Harlan the true architect of his father's death.
