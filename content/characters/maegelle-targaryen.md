Sixth-born child of King Jaehaerys I and Queen Alysanne Targaryen, born in 62 AC, gentle and quiet and studious almost from her cradle, and given to reading her _Seven-Pointed Star_ every night before sleep. Promised to the Faith in 73 AC at the age of ten as her mother's thanksgiving to the Mother for the queen's many living children, she rose through the silent sisters and the brown to take the white of a septa, and made of herself a healer-septa among the poorest streets of Oldtown. She it was who told her mother that the Faith would not have her sister Princess Daella for that Daella could not read the prayers, and her parents heard her; she it was who took charge of the disgraced Princess Saera as a novice silent sister; and after her sister Alyssa and her sisters Daella and Viserra were all in their graves within five years of one another, she came home to King's Landing on leave to keep her grieving mother. She brought her father and mother back together upon her father's visit to Oldtown during the First Quarrel by reminding him that the archmaesters had named him the _Conciliator_, and again after the Second Quarrel after the king named Baelon his heir over their granddaughter Rhaenys. She nursed the children of Oldtown's lower town through the greyscale that came upon the city in 96 AC, took the grey scaling herself in the nursing of them, and died of it before her father did.
