A daughter of House Locke of Oldcastle, wed to Lord Benjen Stark of Winterfell in the decades after the Great Council of 101 AC. She bore him two sons: Rickon, who would inherit Winterfell, and Bennard, who would serve as regent during the minority of his own nephew Cregan.
