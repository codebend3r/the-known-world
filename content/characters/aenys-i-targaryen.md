First of his name, son of Aegon the Conqueror by Queen Rhaenys. A gentle, sickly king beloved of his father but ill-suited to a realm still bristling with sword-points, his brief reign was undone by the Faith Militant uprising and by his own half-brother Maegor's ambition. He died at three-and-thirty, some said by poison, and the throne passed at once to Maegor over the bodies of Aenys's own sons.
