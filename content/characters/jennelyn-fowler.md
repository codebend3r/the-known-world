Jennelyn Fowler is the younger of the twin daughters of Lord Franklyn Fowler, a tall woman with wispy yellow hair near identical to her elder sister Jeyne. In her youth she and her twin rode the shoulders of Princess Arianne Martell at play in the pools of the Water Gardens. In _A Feast for Crows_, Nymeria Sand was abed with Jennelyn and Jeyne when news of Prince Oberyn Martell's death reached them, and Prince Doran admits he cannot trust Nymeria for the closeness she keeps with the twins. In _A Dance with Dragons_, the Fowler twins are present at the Sunspear feast where Ser Balon Swann delivers the Mountain's skull, and they refuse to drink when Ricasso raises a toast to King Tommen I Baratheon.
