Elder daughter of Rickon Stark, heir of Winterfell, and Jeyne Manderly, named in her own day for the lost lady whose statue stands in the crypt. When her father died in the conquest of Dorne and her grandfather Cregan at last followed him into the crypt, the lordship passed not to her but to her half-uncle Jonnel One-Eye, who took her to wife to silence her claim. The marriage was childless. After Jonnel's death the rule passed not back to her line but on to his brother Barthogan Blacksword, by what reasoning the maesters of the Citadel can still only guess.
