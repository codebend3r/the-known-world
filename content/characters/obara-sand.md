Eldest of the Sand Snakes, got on an Oldtown whore the night Prince Oberyn fought a duel for her honour and won it. Oberyn rode to Oldtown a year later, found a wretched child in the gutter playing with a stick at her mother's heels, and asked the woman which she would have, his gold or his daughter; the woman took the gold, and Oberyn took the child. Obara is broad-shouldered and harsh-featured and rides like she means to break a horse in half, and her weapons are a long spear and a coiled whip. She wants the war her father did not live to start, and she means to give Cersei Lannister a hand for Elia's hand and a head for Elia's head.
