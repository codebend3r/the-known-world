A daughter of House Farman of Fair Isle, taken as the first wife of Lord Gerold Lannister the Golden in his early lordship and so Lady of Casterly Rock. The match bore no living issue; after her death Gerold wed again, to Rohanne of House Webber, who gave him his four sons.
