The second son of Ser Eustace Osgrey, a knight of House Osgrey during the reign of King Daeron II Targaryen. He joined his father in declaring for Daemon I Blackfyre in 196 AC and was slain at the Battle of the Redgrass Field, where his youngest brother Addam died trying to defend him. He was buried at Standfast. He is mentioned in _The Sworn Sword_.
