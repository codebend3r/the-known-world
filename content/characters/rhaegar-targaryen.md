Crown Prince of the Seven Kingdoms, eldest son of Aerys II. A scholar, harpist, and warrior beloved by smallfolk and lords alike. His abduction (or elopement) with Lyanna Stark sparked Robert's Rebellion; he fell at the Trident to Robert's warhammer.
