A daughter of House Kyndall, wed to Lord Tybolt Lannister and Lady of Casterly Rock through his brief lordship. By him she bore a single child, Cerelle, who inherited the Rock as an infant at her father's sudden death in 212 AC, only to follow him to the grave within the year.
