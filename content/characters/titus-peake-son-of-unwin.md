Ser Titus Peake was the third son and heir of Lord Unwin Peake of Starpike, the only one of Unwin's sons to grow to manhood after his elder brothers died in infancy and in a sailing mishap. He was knighted by Ser Jon Roxton for his valor after the Battle of the Honeywine during the Dance of the Dragons, only to die six days later in a meaningless skirmish with broken men he stumbled upon while scouting. His death left his sister Myrielle as their father's only surviving child.
