Lord of Casterly Rock, Shield of Lannisport, and Warden of the West, head of House Lannister through the reigns of Viserys I and Aegon II, and the elder twin of Ser Tyland Lannister. Tall, golden-haired, and dashing like his brother, he and Tyland both vied for the hand of Princess Rhaenyra Targaryen when she visited the westerlands in 112 AC, showering her with gifts at a feast in Casterly Rock to no avail. When the Dance of the Dragons broke out in 129 AC, his twin's place as master of coin to King Aegon II drew Lord Jason to the greens, and he raised a formidable host of a thousand knights and seven thousand archers and men-at-arms.

At the command of Prince Aemond Targaryen, Jason led his host down upon the riverlands at the Red Fork, where the riverlords rushed to meet him. Three times the westermen tried the ford and three times were turned back; in the third assault Lord Jason was mortally wounded by Pate of Longleaf, a grizzled squire afterward knighted as Longleaf the Lionslayer. Ser Adrian Tarbeck took up the command and shattered the riverlords' host, but Jason died of his hurts not long after, leaving Casterly Rock in disarray. He was succeeded by his young son Loreon, while his widow, Johanna of House Westerling, defended the thinly held west against the Red Kraken, Lord Dalton Greyjoy, whose ironborn sacked Lannisport. By Johanna he left five daughters, among them Cerelle and Tyshara, besides his heir Loreon and several natural daughters by a favored mistress.
