Named for his great-uncle, the brother of Eddard. Crippled at the age of seven when he was thrown from a tower window by Jaime Lannister for seeing what he should not have seen. Survived the sack of Winterfell by Theon Greyjoy by hiding in the crypts, then fled north of the Wall with Hodor, Meera and Jojen Reed, and his direwolf Summer. Sought out the last greenseer in a weirwood cave beyond the haunted forest to learn the old craft of the children of the forest.
