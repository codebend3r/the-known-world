Ser Lucamore Strong, called Lucamore the Lusty, won the grand melee at the tourney for the completion of the Dragonpit in 55 AC, and the year after took the white cloak when Ser Willam the Wasp passed away. He served Jaehaerys I Targaryen in the Kingsguard for seventeen years before his sworn brothers caught him out: he had taken three wives in secret and fathered sixteen children upon them. The Old King had him gelded and sent to the Wall, and ordered his sons and daughters all to bear the bastard names of Rivers, Waters, and Storm, scattered between Harrenhal, Driftmark, and Storm's End. A song is still sung of him in the kingdoms.
