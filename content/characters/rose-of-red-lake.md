The legendary founder of House Crane, named in The World of Ice & Fire as one of the many daughters said to have been sired by Garth Greenhand, the mythic High King and progenitor of the great houses of the Reach. By legend Rose could take the shape of a crane at will, a gift some hold reappears from time to time in the women of her line. From her the Cranes of Red Lake trace their descent.
