Daughter of the merchant-princes of Lys, wed to Prince Viserys when he was still her father's hostage. She bore him three children but never reconciled herself to Westeros; after the fall of her family's bank she left her husband and her young children behind and sailed home to Lys, where she lived out her days in a manse on the Long Bridge.
