Only son of Lord Willam Stark by his first wife Lyanne Glover, and half-brother to the future Lord Edwyle and the lady Jocelyn. By the order of succession, he should have inherited Winterfell after his father; that he did not, and that his half-brother did, suggests that he died young and unmarried. The chronicles do not record the year or manner.
