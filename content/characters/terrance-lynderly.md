Terrance Lynderly was born at Snakewood, the son and heir of Lord Jon Lynderly. When Petyr Baelish requests that the Vale lords send sons to ward at the Eyrie, Jon dispatches Terrance to serve as a squire to Lord Robert Arryn. On the day the household quits the Eyrie to descend to the Gates of the Moon, Terrance is given the task of laying out Lord Robert's clothes. He appears in A Feast for Crows and is also recorded in George R. R. Martin's A World of Ice and Fire. AWOIAF gives no year for his birth.
