Daughter of Lord Alaric Stark and his Mormont lady, and the Stark of her generation who grew closest to a queen. When Queen Alysanne Targaryen came to Winterfell in 58 AC, it was the girl Alarra who unbent first to her, and through whose laughter and curiosity Alaric himself was gradually charmed out of his sullenness with the court. The records do not say whom she wed, nor when she died.
