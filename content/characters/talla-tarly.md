A daughter of Lord Randyll Tarly of Horn Hill, the great captain of the southron marches who put Robert Baratheon's host to flight at Ashford, and his lady Melessa of House Florent. She and two other Tarly sisters were born across three successive years in the gap between her elder brother Samwell (the boy her father at last sent to the Wall for fear he would never be a sword) and her younger brother Dickon, the son in whom Lord Randyll set the keeping of his name. The chroniclers say no more of her than her name, her two sisters, and the place she holds in her father's line.
