A giant of the Frostfangs, fourteen feet tall and shaggy as a mammoth, who came south with Mance Rayder's host and survived the rout at the Wall. After Stannis Baratheon broke the wildling army, Wun Wun bent his great head and passed through the gate at Castle Black with the rest of the free folk Lord Commander Jon Snow took into the Watch's keeping. He sleeps in the lichyard, drinks barrels of ale at a draught, and speaks the Old Tongue in a rumble the brothers cannot follow; only Leathers and a handful of wildlings can make themselves understood to him. The black brothers gawp at him as at a living wonder, for no giant has been seen south of the Wall in living memory, and the maesters had written the race as extinct.
