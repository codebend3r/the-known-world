Myria Jordayne is the daughter and heir of Lord Trebor Jordayne of the Tor. In _A Storm of Swords_ she is among the Dornish escort that accompanies Prince Oberyn Martell to King's Landing when the prince comes to claim his seat on the small council. She is named the heir to the Tor in the appendices of _A Feast for Crows_ and _A Dance with Dragons_; whether the unnamed "Lady Jordayne" who entertains Ser Balon Swann at the Tor in those volumes refers to her is left uncertain by the chronicles.
