Brandon Tallhart is the eldest son of Leobald Tallhart, castellan of Torrhen's Square, and Berena Hornwood. In _A Feast for Crows_ he is held captive at Torrhen's Square by the ironmen, together with his mother Berena, his younger brother Beren, and his cousin Eddara, the young Lady of Torrhen's Square.
