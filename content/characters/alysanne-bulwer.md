Alysanne Bulwer, born in 291 AC, is the only known child of Lord Jon Bulwer and his lady wife Victaria Tyrell, and through her mother a distant cousin of Lord Mace Tyrell of Highgarden. When her father died of a summer fever she succeeded him while still a girl, becoming Lady of Blackcrown and head of House Bulwer. She serves as a lady attendant of Queen Margaery Tyrell in King's Landing, where she plays come-into-my-castle with the queen.
