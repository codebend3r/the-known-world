Fifth and youngest son of Lord Beron Stark and Lorra Royce, the great-grandfather of Eddard Stark by direct descent through his daughter Lyarra. He earned his byname the Wandering Wolf in the long, restless years of his youth, when he ranged from the Wall to the Stony Shore and from Bear Island to the Last Hearth in service of his elder brothers. He wed Arya Flint of the northern mountain clans and had of her two daughters: Branda, who wed Ser Harrold Rogers, and Lyarra, who wed her own first cousin Lord Rickard Stark and bore him the four children whose names would echo through Robert's Rebellion.
