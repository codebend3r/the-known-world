Deana Hardyng is a noblewoman of House Hardyng whose precise kinship to Ser Harrold Hardyng, the heir to the Vale, is unrecorded. She wed Walton Frey of the Crossing, and the two have three children together (Steffon, Walda, and Bryan Frey) as noted in the appendices of _A Clash of Kings_, _A Storm of Swords_, and _A Feast for Crows_.
