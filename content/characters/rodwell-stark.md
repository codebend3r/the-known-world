Eldest son of Brandon Stark and Alys Karstark, and Lord of Winterfell after his father in the later decades of the second century. He wed Lady Myriame Manderly of White Harbor and ruled the north quietly for some years, but the marriage was childless. Upon his death the lordship passed not by his line but to his younger brother Beron, and the convoluted Stark succession of the age at last entered its final chapter.
