Of House Woolfield, sworn to Manderly. Wed to Ser Wylis Manderly, heir to White Harbor; mother to Wynafryd and Wylla. Tearful, plump, and given to praying at the Sept-of-the-Snows for her husband's safe return through the long years of his captivity at Harrenhal.
