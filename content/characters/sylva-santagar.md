Sylva Santagar, called "Spotted Sylva" for her freckles, is the daughter and heir of Ser Symon Santagar, the Knight of Spottswood. A childhood friend of Princess Arianne Martell from their days at the Water Gardens, she joined Arianne's plot in _A Feast for Crows_ to crown Princess Myrcella Baratheon and was taken with the other conspirators on the Greenblood. Doran Martell, Prince of Dorne, laid no punishment upon her, but her father sent her to Greenstone to wed the aged Eldon Estermont, Lord of Greenstone. When Cersei Lannister's man Qyburn was questioned about the match, the bride was reported to be three-and-twenty to her husband's near seventy.
