Robin Hollard was a squire of House Hollard during the Defiance of Duskendale, thought to have been the unnamed son of Ser Jon Hollard. While King Aerys II Targaryen was held captive in the Dun Fort, the boy would dance about him and pull at his beard. When the defiance was crushed in 277 AC, Robin died upon the rack.
