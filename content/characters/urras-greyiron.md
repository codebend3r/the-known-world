Urras Greyiron, called Urras Ironfoot, was the first High King of the Iron Islands raised since the death of the Grey King, and the greatest reaver of his age. When the prophet Galon Whitestaff summoned the rock kings and salt kings of the isles to a kingsmoot upon Old Wyk, the captains and kings chose Urras, the salt king of Orkmont, to wear the driftwood crown. Upon his death his son Erich seized the crown, but never having been chosen at a kingsmoot, was compelled to give it up.
