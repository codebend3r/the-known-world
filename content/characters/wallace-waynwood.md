Ser Wallace Waynwood is the youngest son of Lady Anya Waynwood of Ironoaks and was knighted only recently. In the sample The Winds of Winter chapter he rides with his mother, his nephew Ser Roland Waynwood, and the Waynwood ward Ser Harrold Hardyng to the Gates of the Moon for the tourney to name the first Brotherhood of Winged Knights, where Alayne Stone and Myranda Royce greet the party.
