An exile of the Golden Company at the time of the events of _A Dance with Dragons_, bearing the name of a house Aemond Targaryen put to the sword more than a hundred and fifty years before. Whether he is in truth a son of that ancient line, or whether the name was simply taken up when he crossed the Narrow Sea, no man in the free companies can say with certainty; it is the custom of sellswords to wear any name that fits them.
