Youngest child of Aegon II and Helaena. The boy whom his mother would not name to Blood and Cheese, and whom they spared in that hour. Smuggled out of King's Landing toward Oldtown three years later for safekeeping, he was recognised at a Bitterbridge inn and torn to pieces by a mob.
