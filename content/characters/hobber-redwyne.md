Younger twin son of Paxter Redwyne, Lord of the Arbor, and Mina Tyrell, a homely youth with his father's orange hair and a square, freckled face, christened *Slobber* by Sansa Stark and Jeyne Poole in the bailey of the Red Keep, as his brother Horas was christened *Horror*. When Renly Baratheon raised his banners and the Reach went over to him, the Iron Throne kept both twins at King's Landing as surety for their father's good behaviour, and so kept the Redwyne fleet from sailing for Storm's End. After Renly's death Tyrion Lannister sent Hobber home to the Arbor with Petyr Baelish's retinue as a token of trust, holding Horas back as insurance, and Lord Paxter brought his galleys to King Tommen's cause. Knighted in the war's aftermath, he attended the funeral of Lord Tywin and the wedding of Tommen and Margaery, and in 300 AC sailed with the Arbor fleet to the siege of Dragonstone, the reduction of which was meant to free his father to answer Euron Greyjoy's reavers on the Shield Islands.
