Wynafrei Whent was a noblewoman of House Whent and the wife of Ser Danwell Frey, the ninth son of Lord Walder Frey of the Twins. The marriage produced no living children (only many stillbirths and miscarriages), and her husband took part in the Red Wedding. AWOIAF records no birth or death year for her and notes that her relation to the main line of House Whent is unknown.
