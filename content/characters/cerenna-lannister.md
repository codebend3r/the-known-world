A daughter of Ser Stafford Lannister and Lady Myranda of House Lefford, sister to Ser Daven and Myrielle. She appears in the appendices of _A Storm of Swords_ as one of the lions of the Rock at her good-cousin Joffrey's wedding, and nothing further is recorded of her in the chronicles.
