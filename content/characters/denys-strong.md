Another exile of the Golden Company at the time of _A Dance with Dragons_, listed in the rolls alongside Duncan Strong. As with his sworn brother of the same surname, his descent from the Strongs of Harrenhal is unproven; the sellsword companies of the Free Cities have always been loose with names, and the line of Lord Lyonel was held to have ended on the block at the Hour of the Wolf.
