Gwyneth Yronwood is the youngest daughter of Lord Anders Yronwood. At twelve she is a small, scrawny girl whose dark eyes and brown hair set her apart in a house of blue-eyed blondes, clever and as quick with her words as with her hands. While Prince Quentyn Martell was fostered at Yronwood she grew fond of him and hoped to wed him once she had flowered, a wish Quentyn himself echoed in his thoughts at Meereen, dreaming of returning to marry her and watch her flower into beauty. Her elder brother Ser Cletus died on Quentyn's voyage to Essos. She is mentioned in A Dance with Dragons.
