The ninth Brandon in the long line of Kings of Winter, known only for an act of furious retribution against Skagos. When the stoneborn launched their longships against the eastern coast, Brandon answered by sailing on the island and putting their fleet to the torch. The destruction is said to have left Skagos without seaworthy hulls for generations; otherwise of Brandon's reign no records remain at Winterfell.
