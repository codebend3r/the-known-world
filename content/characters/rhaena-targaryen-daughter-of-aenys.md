Eldest child of Aenys I, twice queen and twice widowed. She wed her brother Aegon and bore him twin daughters; after his death at the Battle Beneath the Gods Eye she was forced into marriage with her uncle Maegor to forestall his butchering of her line. Outliving them all, she lived out her last years as Lady of Dragonstone under her brother Jaehaerys, the Queen in the West who never wished a crown.
