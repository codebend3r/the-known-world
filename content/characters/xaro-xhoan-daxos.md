A merchant prince of Qarth and one of the Thirteen, the trading company that counts itself the first power of that city. He is a languid, elegant man, pale and lean and bald, with long delicate fingers and a great beak of a nose crusted over with rubies, opals, and flakes of jade, so that he has the aspect of some strange glittering bird. Like most Qartheen men he can weep at will and does so freely in the course of a bargain, and his flowery protestations of love sit ill with his manner, for the beautiful boys who flit through his halls in wisps of silk hold his eye far better than any woman ever has. His palace beyond the walls of Qarth is larger than some market towns. He owns four and eighty vessels, among them the galleas _Silken Cloud_ and a pleasure barge, keeps slaves in his service, wears maroon silk and cloth of gold, and goes abroad in a palanquin heaped with satin cushions or upon a camel in a horned saddle worked with jewels.

It was Xaro who rode out to Vaes Tolorro with the party that found Daenerys Targaryen in the red waste, and Xaro who saw her dragons and brought her home to Qarth and gave over a wing of his palace to her khalasar. He buried her in gifts: a silver collar set with an enchanted amethyst against poison, perfume and pomegranates, tumbling monkeys and spitting snakes, scrolls out of lost Valyria, a palanquin of ebony and gold with a matched pair of bullocks to draw it (one white as ivory, one black as jet), and a thousand knights no taller than her little finger, wrought of jade and beryl and onyx and tourmaline. He told her which of the Pureborn to bribe and where to turn when they refused her, and by his quick wits saw to it that every seeker who came east chasing the rumor of living dragons left some token at her feet. He also asked repeatedly for her hand, for by a marriage custom of his city he might then have claimed one thing from her goods and had it granted, and the thing he meant to claim was a dragon to make himself the master of Qarth. When she would neither wed him nor sell him a dragon for ships, he told her to go, and she went. He came to her again at Meereen aboard _Silken Cloud_, bearing a tapestry of Valyria as it had been before the Doom and the gift of thirteen galleys, which were in truth the gift of the Thirteen and offered on the condition that she take them and sail for Westeros and trouble the slave trade of the east no longer. She had the ships surveyed and refused them. Xaro left a bloodstained glove upon a satin pillow behind him, which is how Qarth declares a war.
