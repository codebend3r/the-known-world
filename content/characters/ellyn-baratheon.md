Third of Lord Borros Baratheon's four daughters by Elenda Caron, reckoned less pretty than her sister Floris and less clever than her sister Maris, and so passed over by Prince Aemond Targaryen when he came to Storm's End in 129 AC to win a Baratheon bride. When Prince Lucerys Velaryon arrived after him with his own offer of betrothal and was sent away upon his dragon _Arrax_ to be slain in the storm, Ellyn was the only one of the Four Storms not to be married off in her father's making of the war, and so passed through the Dance and out the other side with her hand unpledged. After the death of Lord Borros and her brother Royce's accession in 131 AC, she rode north with her sisters Cassandra and Floris to represent the storm coast at Aegon III's coronation, and remained at the Red Keep thereafter as a companion to the young Queen Jaehaera.
