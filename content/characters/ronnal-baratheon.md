A younger brother of Lord Rogar Baratheon of Storm's End, married with sons and two daughters in his lord brother's keep when the great winter of 59–60 AC brought the Shivers across the narrow sea. The plague struck Storm's End hard: Ronnal, his wife, and his sons all took the fever and were dead before the thaw, leaving only the two young daughters who had been kept apart in another wing of the castle. When Lord Rogar came to King's Landing the next year to plead with King Jaehaerys and Queen Alysanne, it was Ronnal's two orphans he brought with his own daughter Jocelyn to be fostered together at the royal court.
