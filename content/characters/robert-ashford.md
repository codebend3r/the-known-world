Robert Ashford was a knight of House Ashford, the younger son of the ruling Lord Ashford during the reign of King Daeron II Targaryen. He bore personal arms of a white sun-and-chevron on orange within a white border. At the tourney at Ashford Meadow in 209 AC he stood, with his elder brother Ser Androw, as one of his sister's champions. In the first tilt he was challenged by Ser Lyonel Baratheon, who beat him after ten lances and a fight afoot. He appears in _The Hedge Knight_.
