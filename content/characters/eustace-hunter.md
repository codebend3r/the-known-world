Eustace Hunter is the second son of Lord Eon Hunter, a knight as quarrelsome and grasping as his brothers. When Eon died suddenly in _A Storm of Swords_, Eustace and his younger brother Harlan accused their eldest brother Gilwood of having murdered their father. He stands heir presumptive to Longbow Hall after Lord Gilwood, and in _A Feast for Crows_ Petyr Baelish reckoned either Eustace or Harlan would one day kill Gilwood, judging Harlan the likelier hand.
