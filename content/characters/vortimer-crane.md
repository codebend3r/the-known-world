An anointed knight of House Crane who served as master-at-arms at Highgarden, the seat of House Tyrell. He is named in the appendices from A Game of Thrones through A Dance with Dragons and is the one Crane the wiki ties to the main branch, though the chronicles record nothing of his birth, his kin, or his deeds beyond his office.
