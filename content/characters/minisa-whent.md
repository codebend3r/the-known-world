Minisa Whent, née of House Whent and known after her marriage as Minisa Tully, was a noblewoman of Harrenhal who wed her family's liege, Lord Hoster Tully of Riverrun, Lord Paramount of the Trident. The always-calm Minisa, soft-handed and warm of smile, bore Hoster two sons who died in infancy before her daughters Catelyn and Lysa and her son Edmure grew to adulthood; she died in childbed attempting to give Lord Hoster another son, and the babe perished with her. Some of the life went out of Hoster afterward, and ailing decades later he would mistake his daughter Catelyn for her, for Catelyn shared Minisa's cheekbones and jaw. The sept of Riverrun stands in the gardens that were hers. AWOIAF calculates her birth at 250 AC or before and her death between 271 and 278 AC, gives no exact year for either, and states that A Song of Ice and Fire never gives her relation to other members of House Whent.
