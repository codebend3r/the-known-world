Lord of White Harbor and the wealthiest banner of the North, an enormously fat old man whose girth has not for years permitted him to mount a horse. Loyal beyond grievance to the Starks who gave his line a second seat a thousand years before the Conquest, he sent both his sons south with Robb's host — and lost the elder to Lannister captivity and the younger to a crossbow bolt at the Twins. Plays the broken old fool before Frey envoys, calls for pies at every meeting, and reminds whoever will listen that the north remembers.
