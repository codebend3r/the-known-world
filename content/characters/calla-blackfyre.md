Eldest daughter of Daemon I Blackfyre by Rohanne of Tyrosh, betrothed to her uncle Aegor Rivers, called Bittersteel, before the Redgrass Field. When the black dragon banner fell she was carried across the Narrow Sea with her surviving brothers, and there in Tyrosh, by some accounts, the betrothal was at last sealed in marriage.
