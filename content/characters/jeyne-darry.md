The younger sister of Lady Mariya Darry and a sister of Ser Raymun, Jeyne was a daughter of the Lord of Darry given in marriage to Ser Cleos Frey, eldest son of Lady Genna Lannister and Lord Emmon Frey. She bore him two sons, Tywin and Willem Frey. Her husband was slain while escorting his cousin Ser Jaime Lannister back to King's Landing during the War of the Five Kings, and the death of her kinsman Lyman Darry at Ser Gregor Clegane's hands ended the male line of her birth house. By Lady Genna's account, Jeyne was furious that the lordship of Darry passed not to her sons but to Lancel Lannister and his wife Amerei Frey, the daughter of her sister Mariya—though as heirs of Cleos and grandsons of Emmon, her boys stood first in line to inherit Riverrun.
