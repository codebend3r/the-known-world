A shipwright's daughter of Hull on Driftmark, mother of Addam and Alyn of Hull and master of the trading cog _Mouse_, after which she was sometimes named by the wharf men. She claimed that Ser Laenor Velaryon had fathered both boys, though the court fool Mushroom held in his _Testimony_ that the true father was Lord Corlys himself.
