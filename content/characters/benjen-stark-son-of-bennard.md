Eldest son of Bennard Stark, named for his grandfather Lord Benjen. When his father refused to surrender the regency to the lawful Lord Cregan in 126 AC, Benjen was imprisoned with him in the dungeons of Winterfell. The chronicles do not record what became of him after.
