Ser Walton Towers was a household knight of King Maegor I Targaryen and the founder of House Towers of Harrenhal. After the king extinguished House Harroway in 44 AC, Maegor decreed that the strongest of his household knights should have Harrenhal, and Walton was among the three-and-twenty knights who fought for the prize in the blood-soaked streets of Lord Harroway's Town. Though gravely wounded in the melee, Walton emerged the victor and was named Lord of Harrenhal, albeit of much diminished domains. He did not live to enjoy the honor, for he died within a fortnight of the wounds he had taken, and Harrenhal passed to his eldest son Jordan.
