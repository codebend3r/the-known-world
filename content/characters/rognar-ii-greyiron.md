Rognar II Greyiron was the last Greyiron king of the Iron Islands. He was brought down by pirates, sellswords, and Andal warlords who came to the isles and allied with the Orkwoods, Drumms, Hoares, and Greyjoys, ending the rule of his house. He was succeeded by Harras Hoare, who, so the tale goes, won the crown in a contest of the finger dance.
