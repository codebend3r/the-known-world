Princess of Dorne, sister to Prince Maron, wed to the future Daeron II in the great Dornish-Targaryen pact. She bore him four sons in succession and gave the realm a queen of warmth and humour where there had been little of either; her marriage was the bond that finally brought Dorne into the Seven Kingdoms.
