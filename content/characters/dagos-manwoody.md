Dagos Manwoody is the Lord of Kingsgrave and head of House Manwoody during the War of the Five Kings. Together with his brother Ser Myles and his two sons, Mors and Dickon, he rode in the entourage of Prince Oberyn Martell to King's Landing, where Oberyn came to claim the small council seat that Tyrion Lannister had offered Doran Martell, Prince of Dorne. In _A Storm of Swords_ he was present when Tyrion came to see Oberyn on the day of the prince's trial by combat against Ser Gregor Clegane, helping the prince don his battle armor. In _A Dance with Dragons_, Dagos was present at the feast in Sunspear when Ser Balon Swann delivered the Mountain's skull, and he refused to drink when the seneschal Ricasso raised a toast to King Tommen I Baratheon.
