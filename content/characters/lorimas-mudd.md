Lorimas Mudd is a sellsword and serjeant of the Golden Company, a Westerosi exile bearing the surname of the old river kings. He may be kin to another serjeant of the company, Young John Mudd, though the connection is unconfirmed, and as with all who serve the free companies it is unknown whether he truly descends from the ancient House Mudd. He is mentioned in A Dance with Dragons.
