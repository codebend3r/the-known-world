Lord of Driftmark and Master of Ships for King Aerys II Targaryen, one of the late king's last great loyalists in the years before his fall. He stood with the crown against his own former charge Prince Rhaegar, Prince of Dragonstone, and against the rebellion that rose to put a Baratheon on the Iron Throne; how he died in the war or after the chronicles do not record.
