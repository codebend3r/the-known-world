Twin to his brother Torrhen and a son of Edric Stark and Serena, Cregan's granddaughter. Named for his grandfather, the Old Man of the North. By the known laws of succession he should have stood high in line for Winterfell after his uncle Jonnel's childless death, but the seat passed past him and his father both to a younger uncle, Barthogan. The reasons are not recorded, nor is the manner of his own death.
