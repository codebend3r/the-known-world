A Pentoshi noblewoman, dancer, and reputed poisoner whom Maegor took as his third wife in defiance of Westerosi custom. She ruled his web of informers and was widely blamed for the dead and deformed children born to his other queens. In the end Maegor cut her heart from her breast with his own hand after she confessed to poisoning his unborn heirs.
