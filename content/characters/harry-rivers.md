Natural son of Lord Jonos Bracken, known in the riverlands as the Bastard of Bracken. Slain by Lannister men in the burning of Stone Hedge during the War of the Five Kings. A fair, comely lad, by all accounts, which gave Lord Tytos Blackwood occasion to remark to Ser Jaime Lannister that Jonos Bracken was neither, and to wonder aloud who had truly sired the boy.
