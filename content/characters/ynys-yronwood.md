Ynys Yronwood is the eldest daughter of Lord Anders Yronwood and the wife of Ser Ryon Allyrion, the heir to Godsgrace, by whom she has at least two sons. AWOIAF's age calculation places her birth in or before 287 AC. A blonde with blue eyes, she was the object of Prince Quentyn Martell's affection when he was fostered at Yronwood, though he never spoke of his feelings to her. She is mentioned in _A Dance with Dragons_.
