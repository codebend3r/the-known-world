Ser Donnel Waynwood is the second son of Lady Anya Waynwood of Ironoaks. He led the detachment from the Bloody Gate that escorted Catelyn Stark's party the rest of the way into the Vale after they were attacked on the high road by mountain clansmen, ceremonially asking leave of Ser Brynden Tully, the Knight of the Gate, to enter. In A Clash of Kings he succeeded Brynden as Knight of the Gate. His squire is Sandor Frey, a kinsman of House Frey.
