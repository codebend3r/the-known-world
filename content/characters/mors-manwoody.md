Mors Manwoody is the eldest son and heir of Lord Dagos Manwoody of Kingsgrave. In _A Storm of Swords_ he traveled to King's Landing alongside his brother Dickon, his father Lord Dagos, and his uncle Ser Myles, all riding in the entourage of Prince Oberyn Martell when the prince came to claim the small council seat that Tyrion Lannister had offered Doran Martell, Prince of Dorne.
