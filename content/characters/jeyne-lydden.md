Jeyne Lydden was a noblewoman of House Lydden whose relation to Lord Lewys Lydden is unknown. She became the second wife of Ser Stevron Frey, heir to the Twins, and bore him two children, Aegon and Maegelle Frey. Aegon, called Jinglebell, was a lackwit who served as the fool of the Twins. Jeyne died of a fall from a horse.
