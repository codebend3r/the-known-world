Eleanor Mooton is the eldest daughter of Lord William Mooton of Maidenpool and, after the deaths of her brothers in the War of the Five Kings, his heir. In _A Feast for Crows_, with Maidenpool occupied by Lord Randyll Tarly following the battle at Duskendale, her father agrees to wed her to Randyll's heir, Dickon Tarly. By the appendix of _A Dance with Dragons_, the thirteen-year-old Eleanor has married Dickon. AWOIAF places her birth in 286 or 287 AC.
