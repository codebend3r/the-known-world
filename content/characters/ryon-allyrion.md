Ser Ryon Allyrion is a knight of House Allyrion, the son and heir of Delonne Allyrion, Lady of Godsgrace. AWOIAF's age calculation places his birth in or before 264 AC. He is wed to Ynys Yronwood, by whom he has at least two trueborn sons, and he also fathered a bastard, Ser Daemon Sand, called the Bastard of Godsgrace. In _A Storm of Swords_ Ryon numbers among the Dornish nobles who accompany Prince Oberyn Martell to King's Landing for the wedding of King Joffrey I Baratheon. In _A Dance with Dragons_ he is present in Dorne, with Daemon, at the feast where Ser Balon Swann delivers the Mountain's skull to Sunspear, and he drinks when Ricasso raises a toast to King Tommen I Baratheon.
