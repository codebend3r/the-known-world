Lord of Winterfell and Warden of the North in the reign of King Jaehaerys I Targaryen, and the Stark who attended the Golden Wedding of Daemon Velaryon and his bride in 49 AC. He brought with him to the feast his two sons, Walton the elder and Alaric the younger, and is recorded by the Citadel as having boasted overmuch in his cups of the strength of the north and the long memory of its lords. He died not long after his return from King's Landing, and his eldest son Walton became Lord of Winterfell in his place.
