Harlan Hunter is the youngest son of Lord Eon Hunter, a younger man as quarrelsome and grasping as his elder brothers. When their father died suddenly in _A Storm of Swords_, Harlan and his brother Eustace accused the eldest, Gilwood, of murder. In _A Feast for Crows_, however, Petyr Baelish revealed to Alayne Stone that it was Harlan himself who had arranged Eon's death, and predicted that Harlan would in time murder Lord Gilwood as well.
