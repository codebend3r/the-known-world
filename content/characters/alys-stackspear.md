A daughter of House Stackspear and the first wife of Ser Jason Lannister, fourth son of Lord Gerold the Golden. She gave him a single son, Damon, before her death; her place at Jason's side was taken thereafter by Lady Marla of House Prester, and Damon was reared at Casterly Rock as half-brother to Marla's children Stafford and Joanna.
