Albin Manwoody was the last king of House Manwoody, a troublesome madman who claimed dominion over the Red Mountains of Dorne. When the Rhoynar came to Dorne, the warrior queen Nymeria and her husband Prince Mors Martell defeated him during Nymeria's War. He was numbered among the six Dornish kings whom Nymeria sent to the Wall in golden fetters, after which the Manwoodys bent the knee to House Martell. He is recorded in _The World of Ice & Fire_.
