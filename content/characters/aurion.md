One of the few dragonlords to outlive the Doom of Valyria, Aurion was in Qohor when the Fourteen Flames burst. The histories of that city record that he raised a host of thirty thousand from the Qohorik colonists, declared himself the first Emperor of Valyria, and flew south upon his dragon at the head of his army to claim what remained of the Freehold and raise it up again. Neither the emperor, his dragon, nor a single man of his host was ever seen again, and the smoking ruin of the peninsula kept whatever it did with them.
