Old Lord Dustin, never named in the texts, was Lord of Barrowton and the father of Lord Willam Dustin. During the reign of King Aerys II Targaryen he fostered Brandon Stark, heir to Lord Rickard Stark, at Barrowton, though the boy spent most of his time in the Rills among the Ryswells. He is recalled in _A Dance with Dragons_ through the memories of his gooddaughter, Lady Barbrey Dustin.
