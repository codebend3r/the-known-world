Third son of Daemon I, a small and slender prince given to prophetic dreams. He was crowned by Lord Gormon Peake in the Hour of the Ghost at the false tourney at Whitewalls, lifted up as the Black Dragon returned. Ser Duncan the Tall pulled him down before the lists, and Bloodraven's ravens descended on the castle the same day. The Second Blackfyre Rebellion died in its cradle, and Daemon II spent the rest of his life a hostage in the dungeons of the Red Keep.
