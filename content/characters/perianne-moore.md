A maiden of House Moore and a lady companion to Princess Saera Targaryen during the long reign of King Jaehaerys I. Of an age with the princess, she became one of Saera's dearest friends in 82 AC (nicknamed "Pretty Peri") and one of her inseparable favorites alongside Alys Turnberry, Jonah Mooton, Lord Roy Connington, and Ser Braxton Beesbury. Queen Alysanne judged her a pretty, empty-headed little fool. When the favorites' scandal broke in 84 AC, Perianne confessed under the queen's questioning that the girls had taken turns "training" at kissing and that their affair with the young men had grown from a chain of dares. Though she had not been got with child, a hasty marriage was arranged to Jonah Mooton, heir to Maidenpool, the king holding that as Jonah had shared in her ruin he should share in her redemption. The match proved a happy one, and in time the two ruled together as Lord and Lady of Maidenpool.
