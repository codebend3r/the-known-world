Arron Qorgyle is a knight of House Qorgyle, the second son of Lord Quentyn Qorgyle of Sandstone and younger brother of Ser Gulian. In _A Storm of Swords_ he rides in the escort of distinguished Dornish nobles that accompanies Prince Oberyn Martell to King's Landing for the wedding of King Joffrey I Baratheon and Margaery Tyrell, and he helps Oberyn don his armor before the prince fights as Tyrion Lannister's champion in his trial by combat.
