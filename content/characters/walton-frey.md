Walton Frey is a member of House Frey, the third-born son of Ser Stevron Frey and the firstborn child of Stevron's third wife, Marsella Waynwood, which makes him a grandson of Lord Walder Frey of the Crossing. He is wed to Deana Hardyng, who has given him three children (Steffon, Walda, and Bryan Frey). Little else of his life is recorded; he appears chiefly in the family histories of the riverlands, with no death yet noted.
