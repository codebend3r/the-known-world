Quentyn Qorgyle is the Lord of Sandstone and head of House Qorgyle, one of the principal Dornish bannermen sworn to House Martell of Sunspear. By his unnamed wife he fathered two sons: Ser Gulian, his eldest and heir, and Ser Arron, his second. He appears in the appendices of _A Feast for Crows_ and _A Dance with Dragons_.
