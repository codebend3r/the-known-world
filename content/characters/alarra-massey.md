A noblewoman of House Massey, esteemed a great beauty, who lived during the reign of King Aegon I Targaryen. She wed Lord Aethan Velaryon, Lord of the Tides and Master of Driftmark, and so became Lady of Driftmark. To him she bore several sons, among them Daemon Velaryon, and a daughter, Alyssa, born in 7 AC, who in time would wed King Aenys I and become queen.
