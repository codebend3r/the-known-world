Lord of Winterfell after his father Benjen, and the man who held the seat for the brief, troubled years between the Great Council and the Dance. He wed Lady Gilliane Glover of Deepwood Motte, and had of her two sons: Cregan, who would in time become the Old Man of the North, and a younger boy whose name no chronicle preserves. The fool Mushroom claimed in his _Testimony_ that Rickon also fathered a bastard daughter, Sara Snow, upon a woman of the household; whether this is truth or one of Mushroom's many lies has been argued at the Citadel for centuries. Rickon died in 121 AC and was succeeded by the thirteen-year-old Cregan, with his younger brother Bennard serving as regent until the boy lord came of age.
