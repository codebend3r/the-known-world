Eldest daughter of Lord Gawen Westerling of the Crag and a Spicer mother whose people had grown rich on the trade between Lannisport and the Free Cities. She tended King Robb Stark when he took an arrow at the storming of the Crag, gave him comfort the night his brothers Bran and Rickon were reported dead, and in honour of her ruined maidenhead Robb wed her at dawn — a marriage that broke his promise to Lord Walder Frey and set in motion the betrayal at the Twins. After the Red Wedding she was returned to her father, kept under Lannister watch, and is held by some to have been counselled by her mother to drink a moon tea so that no Stark heir would survive to challenge the crown.
