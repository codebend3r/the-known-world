Jon Lynderly is the Lord of the Snakewood and head of House Lynderly, counted among the friends of Petyr Baelish in the Vale of Arryn. When Littlefinger, ruling as Lord Protector, asks the lords of the Vale to send sons to ward at the Eyrie as squires to the sickly Lord Robert Arryn, Jon answers by sending his heir, Terrance. He attends the wedding of Lord Lyonel Corbray. His seat is the castle of Snakewood, which sits on the southernmost of the Fingers, along the mountainous shore of a bay of the narrow sea. He is mentioned in A Feast for Crows and appears in the appendix to A Dance with Dragons. AWOIAF records neither the year of his birth nor a wife.
