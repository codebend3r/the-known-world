Heir to a poor tower on the smallest of the Fingers, fostered as a boy at Riverrun in Lord Hoster Tully's hall alongside the daughters Catelyn and Lysa. He took the mocking name _Littlefinger_ there for his small stature and his smaller seat, fell hopelessly in love with Catelyn, and was cut nearly in half by Brandon Stark in a duel he had no chance to win. He came up from the wound a colder man: King Robert raised him to Master of Coin on Lysa Arryn's quiet recommendation, and within a few short years he was the architect of a war he had begun by whispering to friends on both sides.
