A noblewoman of House Crane born at Red Lake and called Merry, ever ready with a funny tale. In A Storm of Swords she served as a lady attendant to Margaery Tyrell at King's Landing, told amusing stories to Sansa Stark, and danced with Jalabhar Xho after the wedding of Sansa to Tyrion Lannister. In A Feast for Crows she remained of Queen Margaery's retinue, going hawking with Janna Fossoway in the kingswood and picking flowers there.
