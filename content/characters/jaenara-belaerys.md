A dragonrider of the Valyrian Freehold, of the Belaerys family, remembered for a single feat of exploration. Upon her dragon _Terrax_ she flew farther south into Sothoryos than any who came before her, and returned to Valyria after three years having found nothing but endless jungle, deserts, and mountains. Jaenara declared the southern continent to be as large as Essos, "a land without end", and no Valyrian is recorded to have flown beyond the bounds she set.
