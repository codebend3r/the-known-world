Jessamyn Redfort was a noblewoman of House Redfort and the dear companion of Lady Jeyne Arryn, the Lady of the Eyrie, during the reign of King Aegon III. As told in _Fire & Blood_, Lady Jeyne, who never married, died in Jessamyn's arms at the Motherhouse of Maris in 134 AC. No connection to the later Redforts of Lord Horton's line is recorded.
