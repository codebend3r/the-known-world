Urragon III Greyiron, called Urragon the Bald, was a High King of the Iron Islands. When he died his younger sons hurriedly convened a kingsmoot while their elder brother Torgon was away raiding up the Mander, certain one of their number would be chosen. Instead the captains and kings crowned Urrathon Goodbrother of Old Wyk, whose first command was to put the sons of Urragon to death.
