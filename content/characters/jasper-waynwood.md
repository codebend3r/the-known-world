Jasper Waynwood was the only son of Ser Elys Waynwood and Lady Alys Arryn, and was presumably named for his maternal grandfather, Lord Jasper Arryn of the Eyrie. He died at the age of three, kicked in the head by a horse, leaving his eight sisters as the only surviving issue of Elys and Alys.
