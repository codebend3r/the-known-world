Elder daughter of Rodrik the Wandering Wolf and Arya Flint, sister to the lady Lyarra. She wed Ser Harrold Rogers, a knight of Eddard Stark's grandfather's generation, and through her line ran one of the closer collateral branches of the modern Starks.
