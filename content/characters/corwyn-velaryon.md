Eldest son of Lord Daemon, Hand to King Jaehaerys I, and father of the great Lord Corlys Velaryon called the Sea Snake, who would in time surpass even his own father and grandfather in the renown of his name. Of Corwyn himself the chronicles record little beyond his place in the line of succession.
