Elder daughter of Lord Hoster Tully of Riverrun, betrothed first to Brandon Stark and wed in the same week to his brother Eddard after Brandon's murder in King's Landing. Raised five trueborn children at Winterfell (though she never quite reconciled herself to her husband's bastard sharing their hall), and held Winterfell as Lady regent through the years of his stewardship at Eddard's side. Murdered at the Red Wedding alongside her son Robb in 299 AC, in the great hall of her own kin at the Twins.
