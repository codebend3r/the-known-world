Second son of Lord Balon Greyjoy and Alannys Harlaw. When Robert Baratheon's host crossed the bay and laid siege to Pyke, Maron stood the wall of the old south tower where Thoros of Myr led the breach. The tower came down beneath him and his bones were never recovered from the rubble.
