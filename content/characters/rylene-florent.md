The only sister of Lord Alester Florent of Brightwater Keep, given in marriage to Ser Rycherd Crane of the Reach. Through her brother she is kin to Queen Selyse Baratheon and aunt to the children of Brightwater. The chronicles record little of her beyond her parentage and her match, and she is named only in passing in the histories of the War of the Five Kings.
