Ser Arryk Cargyll was a knight of the Crownlands who, with his twin brother Ser Erryk, served in the Kingsguard of King Viserys I Targaryen, the two so alike that not even their sworn brothers could tell one from the other. Both were unhorsed by Ser Criston Cole at the tourney for the king's accession at Maidenpool in 105 AC, and it was Arryk whom Septon Eustace named as the knight who discovered Prince Daemon and Princess Rhaenyra together, a tale that ended in Daemon's exile. When the Dance of the Dragons came, Arryk sided with King Aegon II Targaryen and the greens, while his brother went over to Queen Rhaenyra and the blacks. At the command of his Lord Commander, Ser Criston, Arryk stole into Dragonstone in his twin's guise, and there the brothers met by chance in a hallway and fought to the death. By the songs they died weeping in one another's arms in 129 AC; the fool Mushroom held only that they named each other traitor and fell within moments.
