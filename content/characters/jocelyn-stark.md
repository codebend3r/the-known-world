Daughter of Lord Willam Stark by his second wife Melantha Blackwood, and sister to Lord Edwyle Stark of Winterfell. She wed Ser Benedict Royce of the Vale, a kinsman of the Royces of Runestone, and bore him issue whose names the modern chronicles do not preserve; through them ran one of the only ties of Stark blood into the houses of the Vale of Arryn.
