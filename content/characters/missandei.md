A Naathi child sold to slavers and bought from Kraznys mo Nakloz of Astapor for a fraction of the price of a single Unsullied, on the day Daenerys turned a dragon on the slaver. She spoke nineteen tongues already at ten years old; thereafter she remained at the queen's side as scribe and as favourite, telling Daenerys of the gentle butterflies of her stolen island and turning the queen's commands into the harsh High Valyrian of the slave-cities of the bay.
