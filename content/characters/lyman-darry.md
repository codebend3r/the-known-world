The young son of Ser Raymun Darry and grandson of the Lord of Darry, Lyman was born in 290 or 291 AC. After his father was slain at the Mummer's Ford, the boy was brought to Riverrun by Lord Karyl Vance and Ser Marq Piper following the Battle of the Camps, and at the war council he declared he would never call a Lannister his king as the rivermen and northmen proclaimed Robb Stark King in the North. When Robb gave the river lords leave to return home, Lyman went back to Darry, but held the castle less than a fortnight before Ser Gregor Clegane retook it and put the whole garrison to the sword, the boy among them, in 299 AC. Only eight years old and, apart from a bastard cousin, the last Darry of the male line, his death extinguished the male line of the house; King Joffrey I Baratheon later granted the castle and lands to Ser Lancel Lannister.
