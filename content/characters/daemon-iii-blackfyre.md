Younger son of Haegon I Blackfyre, raised in exile after his father's murder under parley. With Bittersteel long dead in his dungeon, Daemon III led the Fourth Blackfyre Rebellion out of Massey's Hook and was met on Wendwater Bridge by a hedge knight named Ser Duncan the Tall, then of the Kingsguard, who clove him down with a single stroke and ended the rising in an afternoon.
