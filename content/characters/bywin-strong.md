Ser Bywin Strong was the brother of Ser Lucamore of the Kingsguard and the first of his house to sit Harrenhal. After the death of Queen Rhaena Targaryen in 73 AC, King Jaehaerys I named him Lord of Harrenhal, and so the line of Osmund came to the great cursed seat by the Gods Eye, where it would remain until the Dance brought the house down.
