The last and most beloved child of Good Queen Alysanne, called the Winter Child for the season of her birth. Simple of mind and gentle of heart, she was seduced by a travelling singer and drowned herself in the Blackwater after she lost the child she carried; the truth was kept from Alysanne, who was told her daughter had died of a summer fever.
