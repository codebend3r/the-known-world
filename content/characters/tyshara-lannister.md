A daughter of Lord Jason Lannister and Lady Johanna of House Westerling, sister to Loreon and Cerelle. With her sister she was put forth in the early regency of King Aegon III as a possible bride for the boy king, in a campaign of Lannister matchmaking that came in the end to nothing.
