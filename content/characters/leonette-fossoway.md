Lady of the red-apple Fossoways of Cider Hall, dainty and bright-eyed and reckoned a fine hand upon the high harp, wed to Ser Garlan Tyrell the Gallant, second son of Lord Mace Tyrell of Highgarden. After her husband's part at the Battle of the Blackwater, where he rode at the van cloaked in the green plate of the late King Renly and broke the morale of Stannis's host, Lord Tywin Lannister stripped House Florent of Brightwater Keep and gave the seat to Ser Garlan, raising Leonette up to Lady of Brightwater. She rode to King's Landing in Margaery Tyrell's train, was named among the new queen's bedchamber companions, and was put forward by her good-sister to teach Lady Sansa Stark the high harp in the Maidenvault before the gold-cloaks took Sansa from the city.
