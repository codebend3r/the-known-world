Lord Maegor Towers, born in 44 AC, was the last surviving son of Lord Jordan Towers and the third and final lord of his house to hold Harrenhal. He came to the seat in 53 AC as a sickly boy of nine, his elder brothers having died in King Maegor's wars and his father of a chest congestion. Impoverished in a castle built to house thousands, he dwelt in the Tower of Dread with only a cook and three elderly men-at-arms. When Princess Rhaena Targaryen took up residence at Harrenhal she befriended the lonely lord and, after his death without heirs in 61 AC, saw to the care of his servants. With Maegor's passing House Towers became extinct, and King Jaehaerys I later granted Harrenhal to House Strong.
