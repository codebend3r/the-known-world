A son of Brandon the Builder, remembered (where he is remembered at all) for a single tale: that it was he, and not his more famous father, whom King Uthor of the High Tower set to the raising of the Hightower upon the Battle Isle. The fragments differ on which Stark did which work, and on whether the son was Brandon or one of his brothers; the maesters of the Citadel have argued the matter for centuries without resolution.
