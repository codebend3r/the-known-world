Son of Aenar the Exile and second Targaryen Lord of Dragonstone, known to later chroniclers as Gaemon the Glorious. He wed his sister Daenys the Dreamer and ruled the lonely island fortress through the years of the Doom and after, holding the line that all subsequent Targaryens would descend from.
