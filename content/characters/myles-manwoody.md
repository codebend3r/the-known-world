Ser Myles Manwoody is a knight of House Manwoody and the brother of Lord Dagos Manwoody of Kingsgrave. In _A Storm of Swords_ he accompanied his brother and his two nephews, Mors and Dickon, as part of Prince Oberyn Martell's entourage to King's Landing for the wedding of King Joffrey I Baratheon and Margaery Tyrell.
