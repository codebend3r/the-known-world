A daughter of the Flints of the northern mountains, wed to Rodrik the Wandering Wolf, youngest son of Lord Beron Stark. She bore him two daughters, Branda and Lyarra, the latter of whom would in time wed her own first cousin Lord Rickard Stark and become the mother of Eddard Stark of Winterfell.
