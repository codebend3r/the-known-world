Youngest child of Aegon V. Given as a girl as cupbearer to Lord Lyonel Baratheon to seal the peace after her elder brother Duncan jilted Lyonel's daughter, and wed in time to Lyonel's heir Ormund. She bore him a single son, Steffon — father of King Robert, Stannis, and Renly — and so the dragon line by her body passed into House Baratheon and onto the Iron Throne by the back door, a generation after the last Targaryen king was struck down.
