A daughter of the Lannisters of Lannisport, the cadet branch of the Rock that rules the city at its feet, and wife to her cousin Ser Damon Lannister, eldest son of Ser Jason. She bore him a single son, Ser Damion, made castellan of Casterly Rock by Queen Cersei after the death of Lord Tywin.
