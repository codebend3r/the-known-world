Second son of Lord Wyman, knighted and full-bearded, jovial and fond of his cups. Rode south with Robb Stark as one of the King in the North's bannermen, served as Lady Catelyn's escort during her parley with Renly Baratheon, and was at her side again at the Twins when the music turned. Slain by a Frey crossbow bolt through the open mouth at the Red Wedding; his bones were returned to White Harbor and the chests they came in were filled with salt.
