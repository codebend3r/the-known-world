A daughter of the chiefs of Clan Norrey of the northern mountains, and first wife of Cregan Stark of Winterfell. She bore him a single son, Rickon, before her early death; some hold that childbed took her. Cregan would in his long life take two more wives, Black Aly Blackwood and Lynara Stark, but it is from Arra's line, through Rickon and his daughters, that the eldest claim of the early descendants of Cregan flowed.
