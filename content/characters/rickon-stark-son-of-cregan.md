Eldest son and heir of Lord Cregan Stark by his first wife Arra Norrey, and named for his own grandfather. He wed Lady Jeyne Manderly of White Harbor and had of her two daughters, Sansa and Serena. In the reign of King Daeron I Targaryen, when the Young Dragon led the host of the Seven Kingdoms in the conquest of Dorne, Rickon went south at the head of the northern levies; he was killed in one of the final battles of the war and did not return to the north. He predeceased his father by some twenty years, and the long, tangled succession that followed Cregan's eventual death began in that southern grave.
