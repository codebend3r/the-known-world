Second son of Viserys II, the most beloved knight of his age and Lord Commander of his brother Aegon's Kingsguard. Champion of his sister Queen Naerys in a trial by combat; bearer of a hundred chivalric tales after. He died throwing himself in front of an assassin's quarrel meant for the king he had never loved.
