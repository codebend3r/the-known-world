Fourth son of Lord Quellon Greyjoy by Lady Sunderly, called Urri by the brother who loved him best. He and the younger Aeron were near-inseparable in their boyhood on Pyke. He died as a boy of the wound he took in a finger dance (the axe-juggling game in which ironborn lads test their nerve and their fingers both), and the maesters could not save him from the festering that followed. Of all Quellon's nine sons, he is the one Aeron Damphair will still name aloud.
