Eddara Tallhart is the daughter of Ser Helman Tallhart, a girl of almost ten in 299 AC. Following the deaths of her father at Duskendale and her elder brother Benfred on the Stony Shore that same year, she became Lady of Torrhen's Square. In _A Feast for Crows_ she is held captive within the castle by the ironmen after Dagmer Cleftjaw's seizure of Torrhen's Square, alongside her aunt Berena Hornwood and cousins Brandon and Beren.
