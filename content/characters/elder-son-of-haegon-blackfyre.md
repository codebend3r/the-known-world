The elder brother of Daemon III Blackfyre, whose name the histories have not preserved. He never wore the crown his father and brother died for, but on some woman across the Narrow Sea he sired a son of monstrous aspect: Maelys, born with the half-formed head of his twin growing from his own neck, who would be the last male Blackfyre.
