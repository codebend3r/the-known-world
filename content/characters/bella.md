A young whore of the Peach, the brothel in the town of Stoney Sept in the riverlands, and a bastard daughter of King Robert I Baratheon. Her mother had been the favorite of the young Lord Robert when he lay hidden at the brothel from Lord Jon Connington's hunt during the Battle of the Bells, the early stormlander engagement of the rebellion, and the child she got of him she named for the bells of that battle. The old proprietress of the Peach, Leslyn, kept the matter quiet for as long as Robert sat the Iron Throne, lest his queen take an interest. When the brotherhood without banners rode through Stoney Sept after Robert's death, Arya Stark and Gendry, the boy bull who knew nothing yet of his own father, slept under the Peach's roof; Bella was offered to Gendry as the brothel's gift to a son of Robert, and was confused and a little stung when he turned her down without understanding why.
