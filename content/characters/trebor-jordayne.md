Trebor Jordayne is the Lord of the Tor and head of House Jordayne during the events of _A Song of Ice and Fire_. He rules from the Tor on the southern coast of the Sea of Dorne and counts House Martell as his overlord. His daughter Myria is named his heir and rides among the Dornish escort that accompanies Prince Oberyn Martell to King's Landing. In _A Feast for Crows_ and _A Dance with Dragons_ a "Lady Jordayne" of the Tor delays Ser Balon Swann with games arranged in his honor and later attends a feast at Sunspear, though the chronicles do not make plain whether this lady is an error for Lord Trebor himself, his wife, or his daughter Myria. His name is a play by George R. R. Martin on the author Robert Jordan, a friend of Martin's: "Trebor" is "Robert" reversed, "Jordayne" echoes "Jordan," and the Tor takes its name from Jordan's publisher.
