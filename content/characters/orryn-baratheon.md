Youngest of Lord Rogar Baratheon of Storm's End's four younger brothers, a knight who fell out with the family over the same quarrels that drove his elder brother Borys into the east. He left the storm coast with a dozen Baratheon men-at-arms at his heels, crossed the narrow sea by way of Oldtown, and took service at the court of the Archon of Tyrosh. Within a year of his arrival in that city he won the hand of the Archon's buxom blue-green-haired daughter and was married into the rulership of the Free City, fathering upon her one daughter who carried Baratheon blood into the Tyroshi nobility.
