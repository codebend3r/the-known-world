A younger son of House Fossoway of Cider Hall who rode out at the side of his elder brother Lord Steffon when the realm divided in the First Blackfyre Rebellion of 196 AC. Where Steffon raised the red-apple banner for Daemon I Blackfyre, Ser Raymun broke with his house and kept his oaths to the Iron Throne, plucking a green apple from a roadside tree and pinning it to his shield in place of the ripe red of his fathers. He fought for King Daeron II on the Redgrass Field, and for his loyalty the Targaryens raised him to a lord in his own right, founding the green-apple branch of House Fossoway that holds New Barrel to this day.
