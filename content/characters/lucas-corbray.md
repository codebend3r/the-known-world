Ser Lucas Corbray is the youngest of the three sons of the Lord Corbray wounded at the Trident, brother to Lord Lyonel and the heir Ser Lyn. He appears only in the appendices of _A Feast for Crows_ and _A Dance with Dragons_, and the texts record no deeds of his beyond his place in the family of Heart's Home.
