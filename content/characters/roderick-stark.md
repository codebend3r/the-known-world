A Lord of Winterfell in the long decades after Aegon's Conquest, falling between Brandon the Boisterous and Brandon the Boastful in the king-list. By what relation he came to the seat, and by what relation his successor came to it from him, the chronicles do not preserve. He is named only as a stop on the journey from the King Who Knelt to the Golden Wedding.
