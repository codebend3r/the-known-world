Lewys Piper is the younger son of Lord Clement Piper, a boy who is said to resemble his father. After his elder brother Ser Marq was taken hostage at the Red Wedding and Lord Clement bent the knee to the Iron Throne, Lewys was named as one of Ser Jaime Lannister's squires, serving him both as squire and as a hostage for his father's continued obedience. He rode north with Jaime toward Riverrun, amusing the knight with the names he gave the horses, and was taunted by Ser Daven Lannister on their arrival at the siege; Jaime, having grown fond of the boy, asked his cousin to go easy on him.
