A daughter of House Tarbeck of Tarbeck Hall, wed to Lord Lyman Lannister and Lady of Casterly Rock through the Faith Militant years and the early reign of King Jaehaerys I Targaryen. She gave Lyman trueborn sons whose names are not preserved in the chronicles, and saw him through the sheltering of Prince Aegon and Princess Rhaena at the Rock in 42 AC, the Golden Wedding seven years after, and his own slow death from the Shivers in 59 AC.
