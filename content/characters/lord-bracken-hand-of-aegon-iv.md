Lord of Stone Hedge through the reigns of Baelor I, Viserys II, and Aegon IV Targaryen. When his elder daughter Barba caught the new king's eye, Aegon IV named him Hand of the King and took her openly as royal mistress, and Stone Hedge rose higher at court than any Bracken had stood in living memory. When Barba was set aside for Melissa Blackwood the old enmity surfaced again, and Lord Bracken began grooming his younger daughter Bethany to win back the king's favour. The scheme ended in 178 AC, when Bethany was discovered in the bed of Ser Terrence Toyne of the Kingsguard, and both she and her father were put to death.
