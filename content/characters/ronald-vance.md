Ser Ronald Vance, known as Ronald the Bad, is the eldest son and heir of Lord Norbert Vance of Atranta. He is a knight and a good friend of Lord Edmure Tully, in whose company he was at Riverrun during the War of the Five Kings. He apparently survived the Red Wedding and the war.
