Twin daughter of Prince Daemon and Laena Velaryon, raised on Driftmark beside her cousin-stepbrothers. She rode the young she-dragon Moondancer above the rooftops of Dragonstone against her great-uncle Aegon II and the great Sunfyre, brought him out of the sky — and was hurled from her saddle as the dragons crashed together, half-burnt but living. After the war she wed Alyn Velaryon, the Sea Snake's young heir, and lived long enough to see her own granddaughters at Driftmark.
