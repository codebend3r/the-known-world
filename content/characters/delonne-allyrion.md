Delonne Allyrion is the Lady of Godsgrace and the head of House Allyrion during the final years of the third century after Aegon's Conquest. AWOIAF's age calculation places her birth in or before 252 AC. She is named as the mother of Ser Ryon Allyrion, her son and heir, who carried the house's banners alongside Prince Oberyn Martell. The appendices of _A Storm of Swords_, _A Feast for Crows_, and _A Dance with Dragons_ each list her as Lady of Godsgrace, though some confusion attends a feast in _A Dance with Dragons_ where a "Lord of Godsgrace" toasts King Tommen. The reference may indicate her husband, her son Ryon succeeding her, or simply an error in the text.
