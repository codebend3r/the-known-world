A daughter of Ser Stafford Lannister and Lady Myranda of House Lefford, sister to Ser Daven and Cerenna. She is named in the appendices among the lions of the Rock present at Joffrey's wedding to Margaery Tyrell, and the matter of her own betrothal (to a son of House Crakehall, by quiet arrangement) turns up only in passing through her elder brother's reckoning of family debts.
