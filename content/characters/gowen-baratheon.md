Third son of an unnamed Lord Baratheon of Storm's End in the last years of King Aerys I and the early reign of King Maekar I, born about 191 AC. He was wed in 208 AC to Tya Lannister, a daughter of Lord Tytos's father in Casterly Rock; the match was the last marriage between Storm's End and the Rock for the better part of a century, not seen again until King Robert took Cersei Lannister to wife in 284 AC. Tya gave him a son, a large lusty boy with a full head of black hair, but the child died in his cradle before his first nameday and there were no other children of the marriage.
