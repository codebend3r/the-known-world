Eldest daughter of Lord Luthor Tyrell and Olenna of House Redwyne, born at Highgarden and sister to Mace, who came after her to sit the Oakenseat. Wed back into her mother's house when Lord Paxter Redwyne of the Arbor, her own first cousin, took her to wife and bore her to his island seat of vines and galleys. By Lord Paxter she gave the Arbor twin sons in Ser Horas and Ser Hobber, mocked at court as _Horror_ and _Slobber_, and a daughter Desmera of the red Redwyne hair. As Lady of the Arbor she keeps her lord's hall through the years he spends at sea in service of the Iron Throne, with the wealth of Westeros' finest wines passing under her hand.
