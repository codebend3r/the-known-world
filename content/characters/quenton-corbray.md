Lord Quenton Corbray, son and successor of Lord Leowyn Corbray and nephew of Ser Corwyn, became Lord of Heart's Home on his father's death from the Winter Fever in 133 AC. The chronicler Gyldayn describes him as both powerful and martial. When his uncle Corwyn was slain at Runestone by a crossbowman while serving as a royal regent in 135 AC, Quenton declared House Corbray for Ser Joffrey Arryn, who was duly installed as Lord of the Eyrie. In 136 AC he came to King's Landing alongside Lord Joffrey Arryn, Lord Gunthor Royce, and Isembard Arryn for the Great Council at which new regents for Aegon III were chosen by lot.
