Urragon IV Greyiron was a High King of the Iron Islands and the son of King Torgon Greyiron. Called upon by his aged father to help rule the isles in court and council, he remained at Torgon's side for the best part of five years through war and peace; when Torgon died, Urragon assumed the kingship without a kingsmoot and with no priest of the Drowned God to denounce him. His reign was long but undistinguished. His dying wish was that his great-nephew, Urron Greyiron, should succeed him, though the priests demanded a kingsmoot to choose the successor.
