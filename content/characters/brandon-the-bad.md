A King of Winter named only by his statue in the crypt of Winterfell and by the bald epithet the singers gave him. Whatever cruelty or weakness made him bad is not preserved in any chronicle the maesters keep; only the stone wolf at his feet, the cold sword laid across his stone lap, and the warning embedded in his byname remain.
