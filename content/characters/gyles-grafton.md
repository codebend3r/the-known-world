Gyles Grafton is the youngest son of Lord Gerold Grafton of Gulltown and a squire of House Grafton. In _A Feast for Crows_, after Petyr Baelish asks his father to send a son to ward at the Eyrie, Gyles is sent and becomes squire to the boy lord Robert Arryn. On the day of the descent to the Gates of the Moon, he is ordered to clean up the contents of a chamberpot that Robert had thrown at Maester Colemon, protesting that he is no scrubwoman.
