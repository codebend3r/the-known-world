Bastard son of Lord Brandon Stark of Winterfell by Wylla Fenn of the Neck, and known in his own day by the smaller name his half-siblings gave him: Lonny. Of his life, his trade, or the rest of his time at or away from Winterfell the chronicles preserve nothing.
