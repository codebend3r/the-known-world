A daughter of Lord Jason Lannister and Lady Johanna of House Westerling, sister to Loreon and Tyshara. In the early regency of King Aegon III Targaryen she was one of the marriage candidates put forward by her great-uncle Lord Hand Ser Tyland Lannister for the boy king's hand, with her mother's hopes set upon a Lannister queen at the cost of binding the Rock more closely to the Iron Throne; the match came to nothing.
