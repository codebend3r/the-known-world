Twin to his brother Cregard, named in his great-grandfather's memory as Torrhen the King Who Knelt. With his brother and their sisters Arrana and Argelle, he was one of the four trueborn children of Edric Stark and Serena, Cregan's granddaughter, and one of the heirs whose claims were passed over when the lordship of Winterfell went to a younger uncle.
