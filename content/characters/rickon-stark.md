Youngest of Eddard's trueborn children, scarcely more than a babe when his father rode south. Survived the sack of Winterfell in the company of the wildling Osha and his direwolf Shaggydog, who was wilder still. Last seen taking ship for Skagos, the isle of stoneborn and unicorns, where men do not lightly go.
