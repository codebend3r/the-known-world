Eldest son of Lord Balon Greyjoy and Alannys Harlaw, and once the heir to Pyke. He sailed in his father's rebellion and was killed beneath the walls of Seagard, fighting Jason Mallister's men in the failed ironborn assault on the castle. He was likely named for his maternal uncle, Lord Rodrik Harlaw of Ten Towers.
