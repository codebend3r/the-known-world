Gilwood Hunter is the eldest son and heir of Lord Eon Hunter, a knight closer to fifty than forty, with a bushy mustache and a red nose and cheeks that bespeak a fondness for the grape. When his father died suddenly in _A Storm of Swords_, Gilwood inherited Longbow Hall and the style Young Lord Hunter, whereupon his younger brothers Eustace and Harlan accused him of having murdered Eon. In _A Feast for Crows_ he was one of the six Lords Declarant who massed their levies at the Gates of the Moon to drive Petyr Baelish from his post as Lord Protector of the Vale; Gilwood was the last to arrive with his forces. Baelish judged him no lasting threat, predicting he would soon be murdered by his youngest brother, Harlan.
