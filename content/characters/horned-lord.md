The Horned Lord reigned as a King-Beyond-the-Wall in the dim centuries of the free folk's history, his given name long since lost to the singers north of the Wall. The wildling tales place him a thousand or two thousand years after the brothers Gendel and Gorne, when he gathered the spearwives and raiders of the haunted forest and led them south against the North. Where the kings before him had burrowed beneath the ice or assailed it with arms, the Horned Lord is said to have worked sorcery to pass the Wall and fall upon the lands of the Starks. It is to him that the free folk attribute the grim and oft-repeated maxim that sorcery is a sword without a hilt, and that there is no safe way to grasp such a blade. His ending, like his name, has not come down to us, and he endures only in the wary counsel of those who would meddle with the old magics.
