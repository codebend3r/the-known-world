Bastard son of Lord Alyn Velaryon the Oakenfist by Princess Elaena Targaryen, twin to his sister Jeyne, born long after Elaena's hopes of a marriage to Alyn had broken upon his disappearance at sea. Jon won knighthood, took a wife, and fathered a trueborn son who in his own time founded House Longwaters.
