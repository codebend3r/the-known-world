Grandson of Lord Alaric Stark and his successor at Winterfell, having outlived both of his uncles in his grandfather's lifetime. Which of Alaric's two sons was his father, the chronicles do not record. He held the seat through the latter years of Jaehaerys I and into the early reign of Viserys I, and was succeeded by Lord Ellard Stark, who attended the Great Council of 101 AC in the place of an aged or absent Winterfell.
