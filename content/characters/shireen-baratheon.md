Only child of Lord Stannis and Lady Selyse, and heir to Dragonstone. As an infant she caught greyscale from a doll a Dornish trader had carried into the keep; the disease was halted by the maesters before it could kill her, but it left half her cheek and neck scaled and stone-grey. She is bookish, lonely, and the only person on the island her father visibly loves.
