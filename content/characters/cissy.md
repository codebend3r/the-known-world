A pretty lowborn woman in the service of House Waynwood in the Vale. She caught the eye of Ser Harrold Hardyng and bore him his eldest bastard daughter, Alys Stone, but the weight she gained in carrying the child cooled his ardour. To settle the matter, Lady Anya Waynwood arranged her marriage to one of the Waynwood men-at-arms.
