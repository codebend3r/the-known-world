First of his name, called Baelor the Blessed. Came to the Iron Throne at seventeen on his brother's death, walked barefoot to Sunspear to redeem the peace with the Dornish, and reigned ten years as a king who was half a septon and a saint. He refused to consummate his marriage to his sister Daena, locked her with their sisters in the Maidenvault, and at the last fasted himself to death.
