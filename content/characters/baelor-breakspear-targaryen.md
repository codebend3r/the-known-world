Eldest son of Daeron II, called Breakspear for his deeds in the tournaments of his youth, and the finest crown prince the realm had seen since the days of Rhaegar's grandfather. He served his father as Hand and crushed the Blackfyre Rebellion in the field. At the Tourney at Ashford Meadow he fought in the Trial of Seven on the side of Ser Duncan the Tall, and took a blow to the head that killed him days later. He was eight-and-thirty, and the realm has never stopped mourning what he might have been.
