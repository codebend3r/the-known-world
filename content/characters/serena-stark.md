Younger daughter of Rickon Stark, heir of Winterfell, and the more remembered of Cregan's two granddaughters by his first son. She was twice wed: first to Lord Jon Umber of the Last Hearth, and after his death to her own half-uncle Edric Stark, by whom she had four children (the twin sons Cregard and Torrhen and the daughters Arrana and Argelle) and through her daughters, grandchildren of her own. Her line and her sister's claims hung over Winterfell for generations after, drifting through the long, tangled rivalry that the maesters call the She-Wolves of Winterfell.
