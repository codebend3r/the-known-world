A Lord Umber of the Last Hearth in the long years after the Dance of the Dragons, and the first husband of Serena Stark, granddaughter of Cregan. His death (the chronicles do not say of what) left her a young widow, and she returned to Winterfell to be wed a second time to her own half-uncle.
