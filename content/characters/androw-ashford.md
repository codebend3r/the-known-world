Androw Ashford was a knight of House Ashford, the eldest son and heir of Lord Ashford during the reign of King Daeron II Targaryen. He participated in the tourney at Ashford Meadow in 209 AC, where, alongside his younger brother Ser Robert, he served as one of his sister's champions. In the first tilt he was defeated by Ser Tybolt Lannister after six broken lances. He appears in _The Hedge Knight_.
