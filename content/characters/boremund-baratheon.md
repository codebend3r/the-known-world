Lord of Storm's End during the long reign of King Jaehaerys the Conciliator and into the early years of King Viserys I. Father of Princess Jocelyn, who wed the king's eldest son Aemon, and great-grandsire of the Borros Baratheon who would later cast his lot with the greens in the Dance of the Dragons.
