Youngest child of Ser Kevan Lannister and Lady Dorna of House Swyft, born at Casterly Rock and reared in the shadow of her elder brothers Lancel, Willem, and Martyn. Still a small girl through the war, she remained at the Rock under her mother's care while her kin marched and died in the riverlands and at the Red Wedding. After her father's murder in the chambers of the Tower of the Hand she was placed in the keeping of Ser Damion Lannister, castellan of Casterly Rock, to be raised among the cousins of the senior branch.
