Trueborn daughter of Lord Alyn Velaryon the Oakenfist by Lady Baela Targaryen, named for the elder Laena who had been her mother's mother. Whether her own line in turn produced the modern Velaryons of Driftmark, or whether the family rather descended from one of her father's other children, the chronicles do not say.
