Sister-wife to Aerys II and mother to Rhaegar, Viserys, and Daenerys. Smuggled to Dragonstone with her surviving children as Robert's rebellion broke the dynasty; died in childbed bringing Daenerys into the world during a storm that scattered the Targaryen fleet.
