Daughter of Lord Estermont of Greenstone, married to Steffon Baratheon and mother of three sons who would each, in their turn, declare themselves king. She drowned with her husband when their ship broke up in the storm-lashed waters off Storm's End on the return from the failed Volantene voyage.
