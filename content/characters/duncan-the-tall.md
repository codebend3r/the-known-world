A hedge knight of unknown birth, called Duncan the Tall for his great height, who took a young Targaryen prince for his squire at Ashford Meadow without knowing him. Through hedge-knight chivalry alone he stood the Trial of Seven beside Prince Baelor Breakspear and so changed the course of the dynasty; thereafter he rode with his boy-squire Egg through every adventure of their long road through the realm. When at last Egg came to the Iron Throne as Aegon V, Duncan rose with him to Lord Commander of his Kingsguard, and stood at his shoulder for thirty years before falling beside him in the fire at Summerhall.
