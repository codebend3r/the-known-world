Ser Roland Waynwood is a knight of House Waynwood and a grandson of Lady Anya of Ironoaks; the wiki infers he is the son of her heir, Ser Morton. Long-faced and lantern-jawed, with stringy brown hair and a pinched nose, he struck Sansa Stark as horse-faced and homely, and he was proud of his knighthood. In the sample The Winds of Winter chapter he accompanies his grandmother Lady Anya, his younger uncle Ser Wallace, and his cousin Ser Harrold Hardyng to the Gates of the Moon to take part in the tourney for the Brotherhood of Winged Knights.
