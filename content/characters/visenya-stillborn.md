Stillborn daughter of Daemon and Rhaenyra, brought too early at Dragonstone the night her grandfather Viserys's death reached the island. Her mother sobbed over the misshapen body until they prised it from her hands; the Targaryens hastened to crown Rhaenyra that same night, lest the bad omen ripen.
