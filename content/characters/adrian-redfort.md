Ser Adrian Redfort was a knight of House Redfort who served in Queen Rhaenyra Targaryen's Queensguard during the Dance of the Dragons, inducted after Rhaenyra seized King's Landing. He was killed in 130 AC defending Rhaenyra at Dragonstone, stabbed in the back by the men of Ser Alfred Broome. He is recorded in _Fire & Blood_ and _The Princess and the Queen_. No genealogical link to other Redforts is preserved.
