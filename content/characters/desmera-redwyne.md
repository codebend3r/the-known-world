Only daughter and youngest child of Lord Paxter Redwyne of the Arbor and Mina Tyrell, niece to Mace Tyrell, and younger sister to the twins Horas and Hobber, whom King's Landing has nicknamed _Horror_ and _Slobber_. She is freckled and carries the orange hair of her father's line, a sufficient dowry that more than one house has sought her hand: Lord Randyll Tarly meant to leave his soft-bellied heir Samwell as Lord Paxter's page in hope of a betrothal, until the twins drove the boy weeping back to Horn Hill, and Ser Stafford Lannister opened talks for his son Daven before Lord Tywin promised the match instead to a daughter of the Crossing. So she remains at the Arbor through the War of the Five Kings, unwed and spoken of more often than seen.
