Second son of Cregan Stark by his third wife Lynara, and the only one of his five sons to leave issue. He wed his half-niece Serena Stark after the death of her first husband Jon Umber, and got of her two sons (the twins Cregard and Torrhen) and two daughters, Arrana and Argelle. By the known laws of succession his line should have followed Jonnel One-Eye's at Winterfell, but the seat passed past him to his younger brother Barthogan; whether he was already dead when Jonnel died, or set aside for some quarrel the chronicles do not record, the Citadel cannot now say. Through his daughters and theirs the convoluted Stark succession of the late second century ran on for generations.
