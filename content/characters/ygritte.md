A spearwife of the free folk, short and round-faced and red of hair, with the crooked teeth common among her people and a quick, sharp tongue. She rode with Rattleshirt's band in the Frostfangs and was taken by Qhorin Halfhand's rangers at the Skirling Pass, only to be spared by the Lord Commander's bastard Jon Snow when the Halfhand bade him kill her, a mercy that bound her to him as surely as any vow. _Kissed by fire_, the wildlings called her hair, and lucky for it. When Jon was sent among the wildlings to deceive Mance Rayder, she became his lover beneath the furs of a cave south of the Wall, and would tell him, again and again, that he knew nothing.

She climbed the Wall in Styr's raiding party and harried the villages of the Gift, and at last died in Jon's arms in the yard of Castle Black, struck down by an arrow during the assault on the gate. Her last words to him were the same words she had spoken when they first lay together, and the brothers of the Night's Watch burned her body beyond the Wall, as the free folk would have wished.
