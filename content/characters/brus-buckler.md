Ser Brus Buckler is a knight of House Buckler and a cousin of Lord Ralph Buckler of Bronzegate. During the events of _A Dance with Dragons_ he is one of the queen's men sworn to King Stannis Baratheon, and remains at Eastwatch-by-the-Sea among the protectors of Queen Selyse Florent. When Selyse departs Eastwatch for the Nightfort, Brus accompanies her to Castle Black, where he is nervous to learn the giant Wun Wun is a guest of the Night's Watch. Half drunk at the wedding of Alys Karstark and Sigorn of Thenn, he gives Princess Shireen Baratheon her first dance during the celebration. Selyse arranges his betrothal to the second daughter of the wildling raider Gerrick Kingsblood.
