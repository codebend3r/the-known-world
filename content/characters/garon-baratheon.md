A younger brother of Lord Rogar Baratheon of Storm's End, the middle of Rogar's four sibling Baratheons. He kept faith with the storm coast and his brother through the troubles of Rogar's regency, and was at Storm's End in the winter of 59–60 AC when the Shivers passed across the narrow sea and into the Seven Kingdoms. The plague carried off his lady wife in the season's worst weeks, but Garon survived, and lived on to father issue of his own from a later match.
