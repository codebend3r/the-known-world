Younger daughter of the Lord Bracken who served as Hand to Aegon IV, groomed by father and elder sister Barba to win back the favour the Brackens had lost to Melissa Blackwood. Caught the aging king's eye when he visited Stone Hedge in 177 AC to see his bastard Aegor, and was carried back to King's Landing as his seventh mistress. The king's embraces distressed her, and she turned for solace to Ser Terrence Toyne of the Kingsguard. Aegon discovered them abed the following year; Bethany was put to death with her father, and Ser Terrence with his brothers.
