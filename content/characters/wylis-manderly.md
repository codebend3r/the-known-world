Elder son of Lord Wyman and heir to White Harbor, scarcely less fat than his father and near as well-spoken. Rode south with the Northern host, was taken at the Battle of the Green Fork, and spent two years a prisoner of the Lannisters at Harrenhal before his ransom was at last bought home. Wed to Leona of House Woolfield, who has given him two daughters and no sons.
