Ser Amaury Peake was a knight of House Peake and a nephew of Lord Unwin Peake of Starpike, descended from Unwin's uncle Ser Gedmund Peake. During the regency of Aegon III Targaryen in 133 AC, when his kinsman Unwin served as Hand of the King and overturned the boy king's appointments, Amaury was raised to the Kingsguard alongside the bastard Ser Mervyn Flowers. In 135 AC he was sent to seize Maegor's Holdfast and arrest Lady Larra of Lys, where he was slain by her guard Sandoq the Shadow, dying at the drawbridge in King's Landing.
