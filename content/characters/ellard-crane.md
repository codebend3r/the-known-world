A squire of House Crane during the long reign of King Jaehaerys I Targaryen, born around 64 AC. He was among the young men who kept company with Princess Daella Targaryen, the king's timid daughter, but fell out of her favor when he kissed her upon the lips without her leave. His brief place at court is recorded in Fire & Blood.
