Sole heir of Lord Wyman Webber of Coldmoat in the Reach, and called the Red Widow in her own lifetime for the auburn of her hair and the brevity of her marriages. By the time the hedge knight Ser Duncan the Tall came riding into her hall during the drought of 211 AC, she had buried husbands Staunton, Perryn, and Lothston in turn and was being pressed in her holdings by her marcher neighbor Ser Eustace Osgrey of Standfast over a stream that ran between their lands; that summer she ended the quarrel by wedding the old knight, and not long after laid claim to his small holding as her own.

Years afterward she was carried west to Casterly Rock as second wife to Lord Gerold Lannister, the Golden, who had courted her in his youth, and bore him four sons in close succession: Tywald and Tion the twins, then Tytos, and at the last Ser Jason. In 230 AC she vanished from Casterly Rock under causes never recorded; no shovel turned up her body and no song carried her name out of the westerlands again, and Lord Gerold himself was reported never to have spoken of her after.
