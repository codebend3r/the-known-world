Ser Jeffory Mallister was a knight of House Mallister whose place within the main branch is unrecorded; his relation to Lord Jason Mallister is explicitly unknown. He rode with Brandon Stark to King's Landing after Lyanna Stark's abduction, and was among the companions accused of treason and executed by order of King Aerys II Targaryen in 282 AC. His father was afterward summoned to court by the Mad King and likewise executed.
