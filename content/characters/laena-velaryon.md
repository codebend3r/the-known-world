Daughter of the Sea Snake and the Queen Who Never Was, rider of Vhagar when the great she-dragon was largest in the world. She wed Prince Daemon Targaryen as his second wife and bore him the twins Baela and Rhaena. She died in 120 AC at Driftmark of a childbed fever bringing forth a son who outlived her by a single day.
