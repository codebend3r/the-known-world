A King of Winter who, centuries before Aegon's Conquest, ended one of the longest feuds of the north by starving out the Dreadfort. The siege of the Bolton seat, by the singers' reckoning, lasted two years; when the last of the flayer kings opened their gates, House Bolton bent the knee to Winterfell, set aside their old custom of flaying captives, and would not rise against the Starks again until the Dance of the Dragons.
