Lord of Starpike, Dunstonbury, and Whitegrove, and head of House Peake during the early reign of King Jaehaerys I Targaryen. In 55 AC, Queen Alysanne Targaryen arranged his marriage to Lady Prunella Celtigar, a lady-in-waiting to the queen and a daughter of Lord Edwell Celtigar — a union reckoned an exceptional match for Prunella and counted a small triumph on the queen's behalf.

His tenure is recorded only in passing, and in time he was succeeded in his three lordships by Armen Peake. The greater notoriety of the name would fall to a later generation, in Lord Unwin Peake, who fought in the Dance of the Dragons and rose to serve as Lord Regent and Hand of the King during the minority of Aegon III.
