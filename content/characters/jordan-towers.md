Lord Jordan Towers was the eldest son of Ser Walton Towers and the second of his house to hold Harrenhal, succeeding when his father died of his melee wounds in 44 AC. He remained a loyal bannerman to King Maegor I Targaryen throughout the king's troubled reign and was among the last men to see Maegor alive before his mysterious death upon the Iron Throne in 48 AC. Jordan himself died of a congestion in his chest in 53 AC. His elder sons had perished in Maegor's wars, and so Harrenhal passed to his last surviving son, the sickly child Maegor Towers.
