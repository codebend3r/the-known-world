Eldest of Robert Baratheon's acknowledged bastards, conceived in the Vale during his fostering at the Eyrie. She leads sure-footed mules along the steep switchbacks of the Giant's Lance, ferrying highborn travellers up to the Eyrie and back without ever a fall.
