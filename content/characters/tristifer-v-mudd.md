Tristifer V Mudd, remembered as Tristifer the Last, was the fifth and final King of the Rivers and the Hills to bear that name. He came to the throne when his father, Tristifer IV the Hammer of Justice, was slain in battle. He was not the equal of his very capable father, and his realm was soon overrun; with his death House Mudd's royal line was extinguished. Catelyn Stark recalls in A Storm of Swords that with Tristifer the Fifth died House Mudd, which had ruled the riverlands for a thousand years before the Andals came.
