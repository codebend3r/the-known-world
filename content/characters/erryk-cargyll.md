Ser Erryk Cargyll was a knight of the Kingsguard under King Viserys I Targaryen, identical in every feature to his twin brother Ser Arryk, so that even their fellow white cloaks could not tell them apart. Both were unhorsed by Ser Criston Cole at the accession tourney at Maidenpool in 105 AC, and in 120 AC the king named Erryk sworn shield to Princess Rhaenyra Targaryen in place of Ser Harwin Strong. When Viserys died in 129 AC and the realm split asunder, Erryk followed Ser Steffon Darklyn and Ser Lorent Marbrand to the blacks, joining Rhaenyra's Queensguard on Dragonstone and sitting upon her black council, while his twin held to King Aegon II and the greens. When Arryk crept into the castle wearing Erryk's likeness, the brothers came upon one another and drew their swords; accounts differ, but by Grand Maester Munkun's telling they battled near an hour as the court looked on helpless, unable to say which twin was which, until both lay mortally wounded and died weeping in each other's arms at Dragonstone in 129 AC.
