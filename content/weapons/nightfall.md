Nightfall is the Valyrian-steel longsword of House Harlaw of Harlaw, one of the few such blades in the Iron Islands. It is borne by Ser Harras Harlaw, called the Knight, a rarity among the ironborn for having taken knightly vows and the only one of his kind to carry a Valyrian sword.

At the kingsmoot held on Old Wyk after the death of King Balon Greyjoy, Ser Harras stood among the captains and kings who pressed their claims to the Seastone Chair, Nightfall at his hip, before the crown went to Euron Crow's Eye.

Status: extant, in the Iron Islands.
