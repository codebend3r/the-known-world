The catspaw dagger is a Valyrian-steel blade with a hilt of dragonbone, nameless in the chronicles but remembered for the crime it served. After Bran Stark was crippled at Winterfell, a hired catspaw stole into his sickroom to cut his throat with it; Lady Catelyn Stark seized the blade barehanded and was cut to the bone before the boy's direwolf tore out the assassin's throat.

Petyr Baelish claimed he had lost the dagger to Tyrion Lannister in a wager, a lie that set Catelyn against the Imp and helped light the War of the Five Kings; in truth the blade more likely came from King Robert's own armoury. After Catelyn carried it south, the histories lose sight of the dagger, though it surely survives somewhere. Valyrian steel does not rust or break.

The notion that the dagger was a Targaryen heirloom bearing a king's prophecy belongs to the tale told in _House of the Dragon_ and finds no place in the novels.

Status: extant; its keeper unrecorded.
