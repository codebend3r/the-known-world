The Just Maid was the holy sword of Ser Galladon of Morne, the Perfect Knight of the Age of Heroes. The Maiden herself, it is sung, loved Galladon so well for his virtue that she gave him an enchanted blade against which no common sword or shield could stand.

So honourable was the Perfect Knight that he drew the Just Maid only three times in all his life, for he held it dishonourable to wield a weapon no mortal man could match. The sword and its bearer belong wholly to legend, and no trace of either survives in the world.

Status: lost to legend.
