Red Rain is the Valyrian-steel longsword of House Drumm of Old Wyk in the Iron Islands. The ironborn tell that a Drumm of old took the blade from a knight of the green lands generations ago, winning it with no weapon in hand but his wits — the sort of boast the ironborn favour, and one no green-land chronicle records.

In the days of the War of the Five Kings the sword is held by Lord Dunstan Drumm, called the Dread, an old and cunning lord who keeps to the customs of the Old Way and reckons Red Rain among the proudest treasures of his house.

Status: extant, in the Iron Islands.
