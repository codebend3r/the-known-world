Caggo's arakh is one of the very few Valyrian-steel arakhs known to exist, a curved blade of the rippled grey metal carried by the sellsword Caggo, called Corpsekiller, a captain among the Windblown free company in the Disputed Lands of Essos.

Where a common arakh of castle-forged steel would notch or shatter against plate and mail, Caggo's Valyrian blade shears through them, earning him both his grim name and the envy of every sellsword who has seen it swung.

Status: extant, in Essos.
