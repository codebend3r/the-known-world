Truth was a Valyrian-steel longsword of the immensely wealthy House Rogare of Lys, a banking family of the Free City whose fortunes briefly reached across the narrow sea into the Seven Kingdoms. During the regency of King Aegon III, when a Rogare marriage and the Lysene bank lifted the family to dizzying heights at court, the blade was carried in Westeros by the soldier Moredo Rogare.

When the bank failed and the Rogares fell as swiftly as they had risen, Moredo returned to Lys, and Truth passed out of the histories of the Seven Kingdoms with him.

Status: lost; last carried by Moredo Rogare in the days of Aegon III.
