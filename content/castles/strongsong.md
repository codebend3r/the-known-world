Seat of House Belmore, standing among the Mountains of the Moon in the west of the Vale. Few castles sit so deep in the high country, and its lords have long kept watch over the passes against the wildmen of the mountain clans.

## The Bells of Belmore

The Belmores fly six silver bells upon purple, and the ringing of bells is woven through the customs of their seat. When danger comes down from the heights the bells of Strongsong sound across the valley, calling the smallfolk within the walls and the lords of the Vale to remembrance that the mountains are never wholly tamed.
