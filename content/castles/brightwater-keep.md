Seat of House Florent, standing above the waters of the Honeywine upstream of Oldtown. Its lands are counted among the richest in the Reach, and its lords among the proudest, for the Florents claim the blood of the Gardener Kings runs truer in their veins than in those who hold Highgarden.

## The Fox and the Rose

The Florents of Brightwater have never ceased to press their descent from Florys the Fox, daughter of Garth Greenhand, and with it a claim to Highgarden itself. That old grievance has made the fox-lords restless bannermen, quick to weigh their oaths against their ambitions, and the keep has changed hands more than once when the fortunes of the realm turned against them.
