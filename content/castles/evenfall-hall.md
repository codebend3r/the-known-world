Seat of House Tarth upon the isle men call the Sapphire Isle, set amid mountains, waterfalls, and meadows above the blue waters of the narrow sea. The Evenstar of Tarth has ruled here since before the coming of the Andals.

## The Sapphire Isle

Tarth's name for beauty is old as song: its waters shine a blue so deep that sailors swear the isle is ringed with sapphires. From Evenfall Hall the Evenstars have watched over the straits between Tarth and the mainland, holding their island for the Storm Kings of old and for the lords of Storm's End after them, and tracing their line, so they claim, to kin of the storm-born kings themselves.
