Seat of House Farman upon Fair Isle, the green and pleasant island that lies off the western coast north of Lannisport. From Faircastle's towers the Farmans watch the straits between their isle and the mainland.

## Fair Isle

The isle is well named, blessed with good harbors, rich fishing grounds, and gentle hills. Yet its fairness has ever drawn the hunger of the ironborn, and Fair Isle has been raided, conquered, and won back more times than its singers can count. The waters off its shores have seen great fleets shattered in battle.

## Lords of the Straits

The Farmans keep ships of their own, near alone among the lords of the west, and their cogs and galleys guard the sea lanes to Lannisport. No house of the westerlands bears less love for the ironborn, whose longships have burned Farman fields in every age.
