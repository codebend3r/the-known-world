Seat of House Cerwyn, a half-day's ride south of Winterfell along the Kingsroad. No lordly seat in the North stands nearer to the castle of the Starks, and none has been quicker to answer when the direwolf calls its banners.

## In Winterfell's Shadow

Proximity has bound the Cerwyns to their lieges more tightly than any oath of parchment. Their lords have ridden to Winterfell for feast and council alike within a single day, and the battle-axe upon their banner has flown beside the direwolf in every war the North remembers.
