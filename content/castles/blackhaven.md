Seat of House Dondarrion in the Dornish Marches, a stout castle of black basalt guarding the Boneway where it descends from the red mountains. With Nightsong and Stonehelm it forms the old shield of the stormlands against Dorne.

## The Forked Lightning

The songs tell of the first Dondarrion, a messenger riding through a storm-black night with word for the Storm King, beset by two Dornishmen upon the road. A bolt of purple lightning split the sky and struck his foes down, and in thanks he took for his banner a forked purple bolt on black, strewn with four-pointed stars. That sigil has flown over Blackhaven ever since.
