A port town on the great western cape north of Lannisport, held by House Kenning of Kayce. Its harbor shelters fishing fleets and coastal traders, and its walls guard the seaward approaches to the heart of the west.

## The Kennings of Kayce

The Kennings sprang first from the Iron Islands, and a branch of that ironborn line won lands and lordship on the green shores of the west. The lords of Kayce long ago put aside the Old Way for the ways of their neighbors, though the sea is in their blood still.

## The Western Harbor

Kayce thrives on fish, salt, and the coastal trade that moves between Lannisport and the northern shores of the west. When ironborn raiders threaten the coast, the town's walls and the Kennings' ships are the first defense of the cape.
