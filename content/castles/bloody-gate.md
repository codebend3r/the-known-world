Twin watchtowers astride the high road where it narrows to a thread between the peaks, joined by a covered bridge of weathered stone. This is the western door of the Vale of Arryn, and no host has ever forced it, though a dozen armies dashed themselves against it in the days of the mountain kings. The rocks below have earned the pass its grim name.

## The Knight of the Gate

Command of the pass is given to the Knight of the Gate, an honor bestowed by the Lord of the Eyrie upon a trusted sword. By ancient custom the knight calls a challenge to all who approach: who would pass the Bloody Gate? Those who come in the name of the Arryns are bid enter freely; all others must turn back or die upon the road.
