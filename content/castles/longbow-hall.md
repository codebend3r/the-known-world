Seat of House Hunter in the northern Vale, its name a boast of the skill that won its lords their lands. The Hunters fly five silver arrows fanned upon brown, and their archers are reckoned among the finest in the Vale of Arryn.

## The Hunter's Bow

The lords of Longbow Hall have always prized the bow above the lance, and their levies march to war with quivers full. In the hunting runs about the hall the sons of the house are schooled at the butts from boyhood, and it is said no goose flies over Longbow Hall but at its peril.
