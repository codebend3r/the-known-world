Seat of House Wylde, standing at the storm-lashed tip of Cape Wrath where the rainwood runs down to meet the sea. No castle in the stormlands is more truly named, for the gales that give the kingdom its name break first upon its walls.

## The Wet Watch

The men of the Rain House keep watch over the waters where the narrow sea meets the Sea of Dorne, and many a ship driven before the autumn storms owes its survival to their beacons. The Wyldes have served the lords of Storm's End as castellans and counselors for generations, hard men bred to hard weather.
