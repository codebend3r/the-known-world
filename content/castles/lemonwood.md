Seat of House Dalt, standing near the mouth of the Greenblood a short ride south of Sunspear. The castle sits amid the lemon orchards that give it its name, and their sharp sweetness is said to reach a traveler on the road before the walls come into view.

## The Lemon Groves

The Dalts fly a purple banner sown with lemons, and their groves are the finest in Dorne. Lemons, oranges, and pomegranates from Lemonwood fill the markets of the shadow city and the holds of trading ships bound for the Free Cities.

## Knights of Sunspear

So close to the Old Palace, the Dalts have ever been tied to House Martell, serving the princes of Dorne as knights, companions, and trusted swords. The Knight of Lemonwood is a familiar figure at Sunspear's court, and Dalt spears have followed the sun-and-spear banner in every war.
