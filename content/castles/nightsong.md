Seat of House Caron, Lords of the Marches, set among the foothills of the red mountains where the stormlands face Dorne. For centuries beyond counting the nightingales of Caron have stood first against Dornish spears riding up out of the passes.

## Lords of the Marches

The Storm Kings granted the Carons their proud style for valor in a hundred border wars, and no marcher house has held it longer. The men of Nightsong are reckoned among the finest bowmen and singers in the realm alike, for in the marches a lord must be ready with harp and horn both.
