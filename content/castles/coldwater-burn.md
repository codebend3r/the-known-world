Seat of House Coldwater, standing by a swift cold stream in the far north of the Vale, near where the land breaks apart into the Fingers and the grey sea beyond. It is a modest seat, yet an old one, guarding the northern approaches to the Vale proper.

## Watch on the North

From Coldwater Burn the lords of House Coldwater keep watch over the bleak northern shore, where raiders and wreckers have troubled the coast since the days of the old kings. Their vigil is a quiet one, far from the tourneys of Gulltown, but the Arryns have never had cause to doubt it.
