Seat of House Meadows in the eastern Reach, set amid broad grasslands on the Blueburn, where the river country rises toward the stormlands beyond. The castle proper is named Grassfield Keep, though lords and smallfolk alike name the place for the vale that cradles it, and its fields and pastures are counted among the sweetest grazing lands in the realm.

## The Eastern Meadows

The lords of Grassy Vale have long lived by the herd and the harvest rather than the sword, and their lands have prospered for it. Yet sitting as they do upon the eastern approaches of the Reach, the Meadows have seen more than one host from the stormlands cross their pastures, and the castle's modest walls have sheltered the smallfolk of the vale through raid and war alike since the days of the Gardener Kings.
