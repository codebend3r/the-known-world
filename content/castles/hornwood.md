Seat of House Hornwood, standing within the great forest that shares its name, between the lands of the Dreadfort to the north and the rich holdings of White Harbor to the south. Castle, house, and wood are bound so close that none can say which named the others.

## The Forest

The Hornwood is a broad expanse of oak and pine, well stocked with game, and its timber and hunting have long been the wealth of the house that keeps it. The moose upon the Hornwood banner speaks to the deep woods the family has ruled since days beyond memory.

## A Coveted Seat

Set between powerful neighbors, Hornwood has ever been a prize. Its lands border those of Bolton and Manderly alike, and when its line has faltered, the claims of rival houses have gathered like crows.
