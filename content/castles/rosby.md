Seat of House Rosby, a modest castle with a small village in its shadow, set upon the road that runs from King's Landing to Duskendale. Every traveler between the capital and the port town passes beneath its walls, and its lords have grown prosperous on the traffic.

## Between City and Port

Rosby's lands are gentle and well-tended, given over to fields and orchards that help provision the capital. Its lords have ever been fixtures at court rather than men of war, serving the Iron Throne as counselors and masters of coin; the Rosby ermines are more often seen in the halls of the Red Keep than at the head of a column.
