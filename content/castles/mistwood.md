Seat of House Mertyns, hidden deep within the dripping green vastness of the rainwood. The castle is well named, for the sea mists roll in off Cape Wrath most mornings and wrap its towers in grey until the sun burns high.

## A Seat Among the Trees

The rainwood presses close about Mistwood's walls, ancient oaks and soldier pines hung with moss, and the lords Mertyns have ever been huntsmen and foresters as much as knights. Their white owl banner is a familiar sight along the forest tracks, and travelers through the wood count themselves fortunate to sup at their hall.
