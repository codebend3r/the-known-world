Seat of House Penrose, a storm lord house of old and honorable name. The castle's curious name is matched by its banner, a white field bordered like a scroll and charged with crossed golden quills, for the Penroses have ever prized learning as highly as arms.

## Quills and Honor

The lords of Parchments are famed through the stormlands for keeping their word once given; it is said a Penrose promise is worth more than another lord's parchment and seal. Sons of the house have served the lords of Storm's End as castellans, maesters' patrons, and men of trust for many generations.
