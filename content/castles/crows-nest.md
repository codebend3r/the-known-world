Seat of House Morrigen, set high upon Cape Wrath above the green sprawl of the rainwood. The castle earns its name from the crows that wheel about its towers, kin in the smallfolk's telling to the black crow of the Morrigen banner.

## The Crows of the Cape

The Morrigens have ridden to war beneath the storm lords' banners since the days of the old Storm Kings, and their knights are a byword for fierceness in the melee. From the Nest's high perch their riders range the cape's forest roads, keeping the king's peace between the rainwood villages and the sea.
