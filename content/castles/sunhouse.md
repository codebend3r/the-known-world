Seat of House Cuy on the southern coast of the Reach, east of the Whispering Sound, where the warm waters of the Summer Sea break upon golden strands. The six yellow flowers of its lords have flown over the shore road between Oldtown and the marches for long centuries.

## The Sunny Shore

The lands of Sunhouse are blessed with the mildest airs in the Reach, and the castle's gardens bloom in every season. Yet the same seas that bring soft winds bring sails as well, and the lords Cuy keep watch upon the coast against corsairs out of the Stepstones and raiders from further shores. Many a knight of Sunhouse has won renown in the retinues of Highgarden, and the house has ever answered the rose's call.
