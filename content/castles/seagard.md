Seat of House Mallister, a walled town and castle standing watch over Ironman's Bay. For thousands of years the Mallisters have kept the western shore of the riverlands against the longships of the ironborn, and their silver eagle has flown over many a red field by the sea.

## The Booming Tower

Above the harbor rises the Booming Tower, wherein hangs a mighty bronze bell. In elder days its voice rang out whenever longships were sighted, calling the smallfolk behind the walls and the men of Seagard to arms. It was beneath these walls that Lord Jason Mallister cut down Rodrik Greyjoy when the ironborn last came reaving, and the bell has not forgotten its song.
