Seat of House Waxley on the northern shore of the Bay of Crabs, in the south of the Vale of Arryn. The Waxleys fly crossed black keys upon white, and their lands look across the bay toward the rivermouth and the distant towns of the Trident.

## The Candles of Wickenden

Wickenden is famed through half the realm for its scented candles, worked from beeswax and sweet oils by chandlers whose craft is passed from parent to child. Septs and lordly halls from Gulltown to King's Landing burn Wickenden candles at feast and prayer, and the trade has made the Waxleys wealthier than their modest lands might promise.
