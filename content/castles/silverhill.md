Seat of House Serrett, crowning a hill in the southern westerlands from which the castle takes its name. The peacock of the Serretts, displayed in all its pride, flies above walls made rich by the silver dug from the lands below.

## The Silver Mines

Where the Lannisters mine gold, the Serretts delve for silver, and the veins beneath Silverhill have kept the house wealthy for generations. Its lords are known for fine dress and proud bearing, and rival houses whisper that the peacock suits them in more ways than one.

## The Proud Peacocks

For all their display, the Serretts have proven steadfast bannermen of Casterly Rock, and the knights of Silverhill have ridden in western hosts in every great war. Their hall is famed for its feasts, where the silver plate outshines the candles.
