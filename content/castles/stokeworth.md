Seat of House Stokeworth, standing a short ride north of King's Landing amid rich farmlands and pastures. Its nearness to the capital has ever bound the fortunes of its lords to the court, and Stokeworth men have long served the Iron Throne as stewards, justiciars, and knights of the household.

## Larder of the Capital

When war or winter closes the roads, the granaries and herds of Stokeworth help feed the hungry mouths of King's Landing, and its lords have grown comfortable on the trade. Comfort has made them cautious; the Stokeworths are famed less for valor in the field than for a talent, honed over centuries, of siding with the winner.
