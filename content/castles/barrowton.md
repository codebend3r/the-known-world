A town of wooden buildings and broad dirt streets in the heart of the Barrowlands, built in the shadow of the Great Barrow. House Dustin rules the town and the rolling plains about it from Barrow Hall, the seat that crowns the ancient mound.

## The Great Barrow

The hill above the town is said to be the grave of the First King, who led the First Men into Westeros in the dawn of days. Other tales name it the tomb of a Barrow King, and older whispers speak of a curse laid upon any who would claim it.

## Barrow Hall

Atop the barrow stands the hall of the Dustins, heirs by long descent to the Barrow Kings who once ruled these lands and warred with the Kings of Winter for a thousand years.
